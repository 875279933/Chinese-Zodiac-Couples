Why Wood

Why 1984 Is the Wood Element

The 1984 chinese zodiac element is Wood, and there is a clean way to see it. The Chinese calendar runs on a 60-year cycle built from 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches. Each year gets one Stem (the element) and one Branch (the animal). 1984 maps to Stem 甲 (Jia) and Branch 子 (Zi), giving us 甲子 (Jia Zi).

Quick way to know your element without a chart. Look at the last digit of the birth year. Even digits land on Yang stems (0=Metal, 2=Water, 4=Wood, 6=Fire, 8=Earth). Odd digits land on Yin stems (1=Metal, 3=Water, 5=Wood, 7=Fire, 9=Earth). The year 1984 ends in 4, which is the Yang Wood Heavenly Stem, 甲 (Jia). That is why the 1984 chinese zodiac element is Wood, and specifically the tall-tree, bold, hard-to-move version of it.

One catch worth knowing: the Chinese year does not start on January 1. Chinese New Year 1984 began on February 2, 1984 and ran through February 19, 1985. Anyone born from January 1 through February 1, 1984 is actually part of the previous year (the Yin Water Pig, 癸亥). Anyone born from February 2, 1984 onward is 1984 Yang Wood Rat.
