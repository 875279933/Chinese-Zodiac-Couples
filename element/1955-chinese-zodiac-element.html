Why Wood

Why 1955 Is the Wood Element

The 1955 chinese zodiac element is Wood, and there is a clean way to see it. The Chinese calendar runs on a 60-year cycle built from 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches. Each year gets one Stem (the element) and one Branch (the animal). 1955 maps to Stem 乙 (Yin Wood) and Branch 未 (Goat), giving us 乙未.

Quick way to know your element without a chart. Look at the last digit of the birth year. Even digits land on Yang stems (0=Metal, 2=Water, 4=Wood, 6=Fire, 8=Earth). Odd digits land on Yin stems (1=Metal, 3=Water, 5=Wood, 7=Fire, 9=Earth). The year 1955 ends in 5, which is Yin Wood, 乙 (Yǐ). That is why the 1955 chinese zodiac element is Wood, and specifically the soft, vine-like version of it.

One catch worth knowing: the Chinese year does not start on January 1. Chinese New Year 1955 began on January 24, 1955 and ran through February 11, 1956. Anyone born from January 1 through January 23, 1955 is actually part of the previous year (the Yang Wood Horse, 甲午). Anyone born from January 24, 1955 onward is 1955 Wood Goat.
