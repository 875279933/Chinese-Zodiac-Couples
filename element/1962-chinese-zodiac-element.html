Why Water

Why 1962 Is the Water Element

The 1962 chinese zodiac element is Water, and there is a clean way to see it. The Chinese calendar runs on a 60-year cycle built from 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches. Each year gets one Stem (the element) and one Branch (the animal). 1962 maps to Stem 壬 (Ren) and Branch 寅 (Yin), giving us 壬寅 (Ren Yin).

Quick way to know your element without a chart. Look at the last digit of the birth year. Even digits land on Yang stems (0=Metal, 2=Water, 4=Wood, 6=Fire, 8=Earth). Odd digits land on Yin stems (1=Metal, 3=Water, 5=Wood, 7=Fire, 9=Earth). The year 1962 ends in 2, which is the Yang Water Heavenly Stem, 壬 (Ren). That is why the 1962 chinese zodiac element is Water, and specifically the tall-tree, bold, hard-to-move version of it.

One catch worth knowing: the Chinese year does not start on January 1. Chinese New Year 1962 began on February 5, 1962 and ran through January 24, 1963. Anyone born from January 1 through February 4, 1962 is actually part of the previous year (the Yin Metal Ox, 辛丑). Anyone born from February 5, 1962 onward is 1962 Yang Water Tiger.
