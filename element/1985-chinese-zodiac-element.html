Why Wood

Why 1985 Is the Wood Element

The 1985 chinese zodiac element is Wood, and there is a clean way to see it. The Chinese calendar runs on a 60-year cycle built from 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches. Each year gets one Stem (the element) and one Branch (the animal). 1985 maps to Stem 乙 (Yi) and Branch 丑 (Chou), giving us 乙丑 (Yi Chou).

Quick way to know your element without a chart. Look at the last digit of the birth year. Even digits land on Yang stems (0=Metal, 2=Water, 4=Wood, 6=Fire, 8=Earth). Odd digits land on Yin stems (1=Metal, 3=Water, 5=Wood, 7=Fire, 9=Earth). The year 1985 ends in 5, which is the Yin Wood Heavenly Stem, 乙 (Yi). That is why the 1985 chinese zodiac element is Wood, and specifically the soft, vine-like, flexible version of it.

One catch worth knowing: the Chinese year does not start on January 1. Chinese New Year 1985 began on February 20, 1985 and ran through February 8, 1986. Anyone born from January 1 through February 19, 1985 is actually part of the previous year (the Yang Wood Rat, 甲子). Anyone born from February 20, 1985 onward is 1985 Yin Wood Ox.
