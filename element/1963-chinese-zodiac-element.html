Why Water

Why 1963 Is the Water Element

The 1963 chinese zodiac element is Water, and there is a clean way to see it. The Chinese calendar runs on a 60-year cycle built from 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches. Each year gets one Stem (the element) and one Branch (the animal). 1963 maps to Stem 癸 (Gui) and Branch 卯 (Mao), giving us 癸卯 (Gui Mao).

Quick way to know your element without a chart. Look at the last digit of the birth year. Even digits land on Yang stems (0=Metal, 2=Water, 4=Wood, 6=Fire, 8=Earth). Odd digits land on Yin stems (1=Metal, 3=Water, 5=Wood, 7=Fire, 9=Earth). The year 1963 ends in 3, which is the Yin Water Heavenly Stem, 癸 (Gui). That is why the 1963 chinese zodiac element is Water, and specifically the soft, vine-like, flexible version of it.

One catch worth knowing: the Chinese year does not start on January 1. Chinese New Year 1963 began on January 25, 1963 and ran through February 12, 1964. Anyone born from January 1 through January 24, 1963 is actually part of the previous year (the Yang Water Tiger, 壬寅). Anyone born from January 25, 1963 onward is 1963 Yin Water Rabbit.
