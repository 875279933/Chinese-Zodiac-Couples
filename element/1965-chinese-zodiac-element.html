Why Wood

Why 1965 Is the Wood Element

The 1965 chinese zodiac element is Wood, and there is a clean way to see it. The Chinese calendar runs on a 60-year cycle built from 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches. Each year gets one Stem (the element) and one Branch (the animal). 1965 maps to Stem 乙 (Yi) and Branch 巳 (Si), giving us 乙巳 (Yi Si).

Quick way to know your element without a chart. Look at the last digit of the birth year. Even digits land on Yang stems (0=Metal, 2=Water, 4=Wood, 6=Fire, 8=Earth). Odd digits land on Yin stems (1=Metal, 3=Water, 5=Wood, 7=Fire, 9=Earth). The year 1965 ends in 5, which is the Yin Wood Heavenly Stem, 乙 (Yi). That is why the 1965 chinese zodiac element is Wood, and specifically the soft, vine-like, flexible version of it.

One catch worth knowing: the Chinese year does not start on January 1. Chinese New Year 1965 began on February 2, 1965 and ran through January 20, 1966. Anyone born from January 1 through February 1, 1965 is actually part of the previous year (the Yang Wood Dragon, 甲辰). Anyone born from February 2, 1965 onward is 1965 Yin Wood Snake.
