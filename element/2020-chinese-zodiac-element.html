Why Metal

Why 2020 Is the Metal Element

The 2020 chinese zodiac element is Metal, and there is a clean way to see it. The Chinese calendar runs on a 60-year cycle built from 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches. Each year gets one Stem (the element) and one Branch (the animal). 2020 maps to Stem 庚 (Geng) and Branch 子 (Zi), giving us 庚子 (Geng Zi).

Quick way to know your element without a chart. Look at the last digit of the birth year. Even digits land on Yang stems (0=Metal, 2=Water, 4=Wood, 6=Fire, 8=Earth). Odd digits land on Yin stems (1=Metal, 3=Water, 5=Wood, 7=Fire, 9=Earth). The year 2020 ends in 0, which is the Yang Metal Heavenly Stem, 庚 (Geng). That is why the 2020 chinese zodiac element is Metal, and specifically the tall-tree, bold, hard-to-move version of it.

One catch worth knowing: the Chinese year does not start on January 1. Chinese New Year 2020 began on January 25, 2020 and ran through February 11, 2021. Anyone born from January 1 through January 24, 2020 is actually part of the previous year (the Yin Earth Pig, 己亥). Anyone born from January 25, 2020 onward is 2020 Yang Metal Rat.
