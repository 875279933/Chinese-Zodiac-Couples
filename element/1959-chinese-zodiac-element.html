Why Earth

Why 1959 Is the Earth Element

The 1959 chinese zodiac element is Earth, and there is a clean way to see it. The Chinese calendar runs on a 60-year cycle built from 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches. Each year gets one Stem (the element) and one Branch (the animal). 1959 maps to Stem 己 (Ji) and Branch 亥 (Hai), giving us 己亥 (Ji Hai).

Quick way to know your element without a chart. Look at the last digit of the birth year. Even digits land on Yang stems (0=Metal, 2=Water, 4=Wood, 6=Fire, 8=Earth). Odd digits land on Yin stems (1=Metal, 3=Water, 5=Wood, 7=Fire, 9=Earth). The year 1959 ends in 9, which is the Yin Earth Heavenly Stem, 己 (Ji). That is why the 1959 chinese zodiac element is Earth, and specifically the soft, vine-like, flexible version of it.

One catch worth knowing: the Chinese year does not start on January 1. Chinese New Year 1959 began on February 8, 1959 and ran through January 27, 1960. Anyone born from January 1 through February 7, 1959 is actually part of the previous year (the Yang Earth Dog, 戊戌). Anyone born from February 8, 1959 onward is 1959 Yin Earth Pig.
