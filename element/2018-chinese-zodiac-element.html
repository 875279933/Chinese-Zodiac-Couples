Why Earth

Why 2018 Is the Earth Element

The 2018 chinese zodiac element is Earth, and there is a clean way to see it. The Chinese calendar runs on a 60-year cycle built from 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches. Each year gets one Stem (the element) and one Branch (the animal). 2018 maps to Stem 戊 (Wu) and Branch 戌 (Xu), giving us 戊戌 (Wu Xu).

Quick way to know your element without a chart. Look at the last digit of the birth year. Even digits land on Yang stems (0=Metal, 2=Water, 4=Wood, 6=Fire, 8=Earth). Odd digits land on Yin stems (1=Metal, 3=Water, 5=Wood, 7=Fire, 9=Earth). The year 2018 ends in 8, which is the Yang Earth Heavenly Stem, 戊 (Wu). That is why the 2018 chinese zodiac element is Earth, and specifically the tall-tree, bold, hard-to-move version of it.

One catch worth knowing: the Chinese year does not start on January 1. Chinese New Year 2018 began on February 16, 2018 and ran through February 4, 2019. Anyone born from January 1 through February 15, 2018 is actually part of the previous year (the Yin Fire Rooster, 丁酉). Anyone born from February 16, 2018 onward is 2018 Yang Earth Dog.
