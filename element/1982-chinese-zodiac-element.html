Why Water

Why 1982 Is the Water Element

The 1982 chinese zodiac element is Water, and there is a clean way to see it. The Chinese calendar runs on a 60-year cycle built from 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches. Each year gets one Stem (the element) and one Branch (the animal). 1982 maps to Stem 壬 (Ren) and Branch 戌 (Xu), giving us 壬戌 (Ren Xu).

Quick way to know your element without a chart. Look at the last digit of the birth year. Even digits land on Yang stems (0=Metal, 2=Water, 4=Wood, 6=Fire, 8=Earth). Odd digits land on Yin stems (1=Metal, 3=Water, 5=Wood, 7=Fire, 9=Earth). The year 1982 ends in 2, which is the Yang Water Heavenly Stem, 壬 (Ren). That is why the 1982 chinese zodiac element is Water, and specifically the tall-tree, bold, hard-to-move version of it.

One catch worth knowing: the Chinese year does not start on January 1. Chinese New Year 1982 began on January 25, 1982 and ran through February 12, 1983. Anyone born from January 1 through January 24, 1982 is actually part of the previous year (the Yin Metal Rooster, 辛酉). Anyone born from January 25, 1982 onward is 1982 Yang Water Dog.
