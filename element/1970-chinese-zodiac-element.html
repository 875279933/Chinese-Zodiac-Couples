Why Metal

Why 1970 Is the Metal Element

The 1970 chinese zodiac element is Metal, and there is a clean way to see it. The Chinese calendar runs on a 60-year cycle built from 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches. Each year gets one Stem (the element) and one Branch (the animal). 1970 maps to Stem 庚 (Geng) and Branch 戌 (Xu), giving us 庚戌 (Geng Xu).

Quick way to know your element without a chart. Look at the last digit of the birth year. Even digits land on Yang stems (0=Metal, 2=Water, 4=Wood, 6=Fire, 8=Earth). Odd digits land on Yin stems (1=Metal, 3=Water, 5=Wood, 7=Fire, 9=Earth). The year 1970 ends in 0, which is the Yang Metal Heavenly Stem, 庚 (Geng). That is why the 1970 chinese zodiac element is Metal, and specifically the tall-tree, bold, hard-to-move version of it.

One catch worth knowing: the Chinese year does not start on January 1. Chinese New Year 1970 began on February 6, 1970 and ran through January 26, 1971. Anyone born from January 1 through February 5, 1970 is actually part of the previous year (the Yin Earth Rooster, 己酉). Anyone born from February 6, 1970 onward is 1970 Yang Metal Dog.
