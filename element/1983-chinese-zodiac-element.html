Why Water

Why 1983 Is the Water Element

The 1983 chinese zodiac element is Water, and there is a clean way to see it. The Chinese calendar runs on a 60-year cycle built from 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches. Each year gets one Stem (the element) and one Branch (the animal). 1983 maps to Stem 癸 (Gui) and Branch 亥 (Hai), giving us 癸亥 (Gui Hai).

Quick way to know your element without a chart. Look at the last digit of the birth year. Even digits land on Yang stems (0=Metal, 2=Water, 4=Wood, 6=Fire, 8=Earth). Odd digits land on Yin stems (1=Metal, 3=Water, 5=Wood, 7=Fire, 9=Earth). The year 1983 ends in 3, which is the Yin Water Heavenly Stem, 癸 (Gui). That is why the 1983 chinese zodiac element is Water, and specifically the soft, vine-like, flexible version of it.

One catch worth knowing: the Chinese year does not start on January 1. Chinese New Year 1983 began on February 13, 1983 and ran through February 1, 1984. Anyone born from January 1 through February 12, 1983 is actually part of the previous year (the Yang Water Dog, 壬戌). Anyone born from February 13, 1983 onward is 1983 Yin Water Pig.
