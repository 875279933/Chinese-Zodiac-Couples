Why Earth

Why 2008 Is the Earth Element

The 2008 chinese zodiac element is Earth, and there is a clean way to see it. The Chinese calendar runs on a 60-year cycle built from 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches. Each year gets one Stem (the element) and one Branch (the animal). 2008 maps to Stem 戊 (Wu) and Branch 子 (Zi), giving us 戊子 (Wu Zi).

Quick way to know your element without a chart. Look at the last digit of the birth year. Even digits land on Yang stems (0=Metal, 2=Water, 4=Wood, 6=Fire, 8=Earth). Odd digits land on Yin stems (1=Metal, 3=Water, 5=Wood, 7=Fire, 9=Earth). The year 2008 ends in 8, which is the Yang Earth Heavenly Stem, 戊 (Wu). That is why the 2008 chinese zodiac element is Earth, and specifically the tall-tree, bold, hard-to-move version of it.

One catch worth knowing: the Chinese year does not start on January 1. Chinese New Year 2008 began on February 7, 2008 and ran through January 25, 2009. Anyone born from January 1 through February 6, 2008 is actually part of the previous year (the Yin Fire Pig, 丁亥). Anyone born from February 7, 2008 onward is 2008 Yang Earth Rat.
