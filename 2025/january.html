January 2025 in the Wood Snake Year

January 2025 is a transitional month on the Chinese zodiac calendar. The first 28 days still belong to the Wood Dragon year that opened in February 2024, and the lunar New Year falls on January 29, 2025. From that point through the end of the month, the Wood Snake takes over, and the rest of 2025 follows its quieter, more reflective rhythm.
