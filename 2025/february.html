February 2025 in the Wood Snake Year

February 2025 is the first complete month of the Wood Snake year. The lunar New Year opened on January 29, and the Lantern Festival on February 12 marks the official close of the New Year celebration period. From there, the month slides into the Rain Water solar term, the first of the spring markers on the traditional calendar.

For the chinese zodiac february 2025 cycle, the rhythm is short and busy. Twenty-eight days, two major cultural markers, and a clear shift from the deep winter of January into the early stirrings of spring. The notes below for each of the twelve signs are written with that in mind.
