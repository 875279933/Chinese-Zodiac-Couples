April 2025 in the Wood Snake Year

April 2025 brings the Qingming solar term on the 4th, a public holiday in much of the Chinese-speaking world. The lunar month is the third of the year, and the wood element of the Snake year is at its strongest. The month is a productive stretch on the chinese zodiac april 2025 calendar, with the season moving from early spring into late spring.

The notes below for each of the twelve signs are written with that productive energy in mind. The chinese zodiac april 2025 cycle rewards steady work and the kind of patient progress the Snake year tends to favor.

Strong
