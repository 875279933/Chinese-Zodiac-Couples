June 2025 in the Wood Snake Year

June 2025 brings the Dragon Boat Festival on May 31 into the early days of the month, with a long weekend in much of the Chinese-speaking world. The lunar month is the fifth of the year, and the wood element of the Snake year is still in effect. The month is a productive stretch on the chinese zodiac june 2025 calendar, with the season moving from early summer into mid-summer.

The notes below for each of the twelve signs are written with that productive energy in mind. The chinese zodiac june 2025 cycle rewards steady work and the kind of patient progress the Snake year tends to favor.
