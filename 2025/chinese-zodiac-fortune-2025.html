About the 2025 Chinese Zodiac Calendar

The 2025 cycle follows the Wood Snake year, which begins on January 29, 2025 and runs through February 16, 2026 on the traditional lunar schedule. The Wood element shapes the year with a quieter, more reflective energy than a Fire Snake year would carry, and it pairs with the Snake's natural tendency toward strategy and patience.

Each of the twelve months brings its own seasonal shift, its own pairing of solar terms, and its own patterns of activity. The pages linked below break those patterns down for every sign, with notes on the lunar animal of the day, traditional markers, and how the month tends to unfold for Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, and Pig.

If you read the calendar in order, you can watch the year build from winter quiet in January through the longer days of summer and back into the close of December. If you prefer to jump straight to a particular sign or a particular month, the cards below will take you there.

How to Read the Year Ahead

The 2025 cycle is built around the twelve earthly branches that name the years, the months, the days, and the two-hour blocks of the day. Most readers know the year animal, but each month and each day on the lunar schedule also has its own branch, and the way those branches interact is what gives the year its texture.

Months on the Calendar

Each month corresponds to one of the twelve signs as well as a solar term that anchors the season. The pages linked above walk through the month from a calendar view, then look at how each of the twelve signs tends to handle it. The notes are written as cultural reference, not as rules to follow.

Days on the Calendar

The day of the lunar cycle moves through the same twelve signs as the year. The first day of the lunar month opens with Rat, the second with Ox, and so on. If you want to match a particular Western date to a day animal, the calendar tables on each monthly page do that for you.

Notes for Each Sign

For every month, the page lists the twelve signs and offers a short note on the general rhythm of the period. These notes are written from a cultural and seasonal point of view, not as promises about any individual's life. The cycle works best as a reference you can use to plan, reflect, and stay in touch with the year.

The Wood Snake Year in Brief

The Wood Snake year is one of the more thoughtful entries in the twelve-year cycle. The Snake itself is associated with insight, careful planning, and a willingness to wait for the right moment. Wood, as an element, adds a layer of growth, flexibility, and quiet persistence. Together, they tend to favor a slower, more deliberate approach than the years around them.

Across the year, the months that pair well with the Snake's energy are the ones built around study, review, and steady progress. The months that ask for quick, public moves are usually the ones where most signs will want to plan more carefully. The individual sign pages go into that in more detail.

Element Pairings for 2025

  • Snake (year bearer): Wood is the resource element, supporting the Snake with growth and patience.
  • Ox, Rabbit: Earth and Wood form a productive relationship, so months favoring these signs tend to feel steady.
  • Rooster: Metal controls Wood in the cycle, so the Rooster and Snake may want to give each other more space in 2025.
  • Pig: Wood and Pig share a productive but cautious pairing, with the strongest payoff in the second half of the year.
