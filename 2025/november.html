November 2025 in the Wood Snake Year

November 2025 brings the Lidong solar term on the 7th and the formal start of astronomical winter in the traditional calendar. The lunar month is the ninth of the year, and the wood element of the Snake year is still in effect. The month is a productive stretch on the chinese zodiac november 2025 calendar, with the season moving from mid-autumn to late autumn.

The notes below for each of the twelve signs are written with that productive energy in mind. The chinese zodiac november 2025 cycle rewards steady work and the kind of patient progress the Snake year tends to favor.
