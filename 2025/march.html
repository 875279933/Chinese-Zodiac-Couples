March 2025 in the Wood Snake Year

March 2025 opens with the Awakening of Insects solar term and closes with the Spring Equinox on the 20th. The lunar month is the second of the year, and the wood element of the Snake year is in full effect. The month is a turning point on the chinese zodiac march 2025 calendar, with the season moving from late winter into early spring.

The notes below for each of the twelve signs are written with that seasonal shift in mind. The chinese zodiac march 2025 cycle rewards early, deliberate work and patience through the middle weeks.
