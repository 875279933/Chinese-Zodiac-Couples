July 2025 in the Wood Snake Year

July 2025 is the sixth lunar month and one of the hottest stretches of the year. The wood element of the Snake year is still in effect, but the season calls for shorter work blocks and longer rest periods. The month is a productive stretch on the chinese zodiac july 2025 calendar, with the season moving from mid-summer toward late summer.

The notes below for each of the twelve signs are written with that seasonal shift in mind. The chinese zodiac july 2025 cycle rewards patience through the hot weeks and steady work in the cooler hours.
