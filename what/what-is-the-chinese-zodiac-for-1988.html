What is the Chinese zodiac for 1988?

If you were born in 1988, your Chinese zodiac sign is the Earth Dragon—a perfect mix of ambition and practicality.

1988 Earth Dragons: Power Meets Practicality

So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 1988? Let's cut to the chase—it's the Year of the Earth Dragon! If your birthday falls between February 17, 1988 and February 5, 1989, you're part of this impressive group. Earth Dragons are like the reliable CEO of the zodiac—confident, capable, and always grounded no matter how high they aim. Dragons are natural-born leaders—charismatic, ambitious, and brimming with confidence. But add Earth to that fire, and you get someone who's not just all talk. These Dragons keep their feet firmly planted on the ground. They dream big, sure—but they also have the practical smarts to turn those dreams into reality, one step at a time.

Wait—When Exactly Was the 1988 Dragon Year?

Here's a common question: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if you were born before February 17, 1988, you're actually a Fire Rabbit from 1987, not an Earth Dragon.

This matters! A lot of people born in early February get their zodiac sign wrong. Always double-check your exact birth date to know for sure what is your Chinese zodiac sign.
