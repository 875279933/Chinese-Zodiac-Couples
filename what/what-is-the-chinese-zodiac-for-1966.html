Wait—Am I Actually a Fire Horse?

Here's a common question: if you were born in early February 1966, you might not be a Fire Horse! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if your birthday is before February 9, 1966, you're actually a Wood Snake from 1965.

This is why it's always good to double-check your exact birth date against the lunar calendar. A few days can make all the difference in what is your Chinese zodiac sign!
