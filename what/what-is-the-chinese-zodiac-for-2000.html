Is 2000 the Year of the Dragon for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 5, 2000, your zodiac sign is actually the Earth Rabbit (from the previous year). Only those born between February 5, 2000 and January 23, 2001 are Metal Dragons.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 2000 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
