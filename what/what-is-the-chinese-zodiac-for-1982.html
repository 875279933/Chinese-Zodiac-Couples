Is 1982 the Year of the Dog for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. The 1982 Chinese zodiac year starts on January 25, 1982, so anyone born before that date is actually a Metal Rooster (from the previous year). Only those born between January 25, 1982 and February 12, 1983 are Water Dogs.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early January 1982 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in February. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
