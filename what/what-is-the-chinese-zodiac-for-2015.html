2015 Wood Goats: Soft-Hearted, Creative, and Deeply Kind

So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 2015? It's the Year of the Wood Goat! If your birthday falls between February 19, 2015 and February 7, 2016, you belong to one of the gentlest signs in the zodiac. Wood Goats have a softness that makes people feel safe around them—and a quiet strength that surprises you when it shows up.

Goats are known for their kindness, creativity, and love of beauty. They care about people, they notice the small things, and they make the world feel a little warmer just by being in it. Add Wood to the mix and you get a Goat that's a little more grounded, a little more generous, and a little more willing to grow. Wood Goats don't just dream—they build things that last.

Section OneThe True Nature of Wood Goats

What really drives 2015 Wood Goats? It's Goat kindness rooted in Wood growth.

Wood Goats feel like a soft place to land. They care—about their friends, their families, their communities, the world at large. Goats on their own are already warm and empathetic. They feel what other people feel and they want to help. The Wood element gives that kindness a backbone. Wood Goats don't just sympathize—they show up, follow through, and put real time and energy into the people and causes they love.

They're naturally creative. Wood Goats see beauty where others don't—colors, sounds, words, textures, ideas. They make good art, good design, good food, good conversation. They also make good parents, teachers, and friends because they actually pay attention.

The challenge? Wood Goats can be too hard on themselves. They take on everyone else's feelings and forget to check in with their own. The growth edge is learning to say no without guilt, and to invest in themselves with the same care they give everyone else.

Section TwoLove, Work & Money

How Wood Goat traits shape the way 2015 babies handle relationships, careers, and finances.

Wood Goats bring their warm, easy energy into every part of life. In love, they're devoted partners who show up. At work, they thrive in roles that let them create and care. With money, they're careful but generous. The only real risk? They give so much to everyone else that they forget to save some for themselves.

Wood Goats are loyal, romantic, and endlessly supportive. They match well with Rabbits, Pigs, and Horses—signs that match their softness and share their love of beauty. They can find Monkeys and Rats a little too sharp for their taste.

02

Wood Goats thrive in creative and caring roles—art, design, education, healthcare, hospitality, counseling, writing, fashion. They work best in environments that value quality over speed and people over metrics. They also make excellent team players who keep morale high.

03

With Money

Wood Goats are careful with cash but generous when it matters. They save for the things that matter, and they open their wallets for experiences with the people they care about. The Wood element keeps them from going overboard either way.

04

Wood Goats thrive when they remember themselves. They can give and give and give—but they need to also receive. Saying yes to help, to rest, to doing nothing, isn't selfish. It's what keeps them able to keep showing up.

Wait—Am I Actually a Wood Goat?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if you were born before February 19, 2015, you're actually a Wood Horse from 2014, not a Wood Goat.

Section Three2015: A Year That Shaped the World

Five moments that defined the Year of the Wood Goat.

February 19, 2015
Year of the Wood Goat Begins
The lunar new year ushered in the Wood Goat, with lantern festivals and family gatherings in cities from Singapore to San Francisco. The Goat is associated with peace, harmony, and creativity—themes that would play out across the year in different ways.
June 26, 2015
Same-Sex Marriage Legalized Nationwide
The U.S. Supreme Court ruled in Obergefell v. Hodges that same-sex marriage is a constitutional right, legalizing it in all 50 states. The decision was the culmination of decades of activism and litigation.
July 14, 2015
New Horizons Flies By Pluto
NASA's New Horizons spacecraft made its closest pass of Pluto, sending back the first clear images of the dwarf planet's surface. The flyby marked the end of the era of initial reconnaissance of the classical planets.
November 13, 2015
Paris Terror Attacks
Coordinated terrorist attacks in Paris killed 130 people and injured hundreds more at the Bataclan concert hall, restaurants, and the national stadium. It was the deadliest attack on French soil since World War II.
December 12, 2015
Paris Climate Agreement
196 countries adopted the Paris Agreement at COP21, committing to limit global warming to well below 2°C above pre-industrial levels. The deal marked a turning point in international climate diplomacy.
