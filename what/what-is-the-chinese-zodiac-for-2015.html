What is the Chinese zodiac for 2015?

The 2015 Chinese zodiac is the Wood Goat. If you were born in 2015, your zodiac sign combines the Goat's kindness with the growth of the Wood element.

The 2015 Wood Goat: Gentle & Artistic

Curious what is the Chinese zodiac for 2015? It's the Year of the Wood Goat! If your birthday falls between February 19, 2015 and February 7, 2016, you're a Wood Goat. These are the gentle creatives who bring warmth and artistry to everything.

Goats are the nurturers of the zodiac—gentle, artistic, and deeply compassionate. When you mix that with Wood, you get someone who's not just caring, but also creative, nurturing, and always growing into something beautiful.

Love & Relationships for 2015 Wood Goats

How do Wood Goats born in 2015 approach love and relationships?

Wood Goats are loving partners who value emotional connection. They're caring and nurturing, always supporting their loved ones. They believe in building relationships based on mutual respect and understanding.

Is 2015 the Year of the Goat for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 19, 2015, your zodiac sign is actually the Wood Horse (from the previous year). Only those born between February 19, 2015 and February 7, 2016 are Wood Goats.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 2015 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
