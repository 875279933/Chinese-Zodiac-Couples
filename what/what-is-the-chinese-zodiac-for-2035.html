The True Nature of Wood Rabbits

What really drives 2035 Wood Rabbits? It's Rabbit kindness grounded by Wood resilience.

Wood Rabbits are the rare mix of soft and strong. Rabbits on their own are already kind—they care about people, they care about how things feel, and they have a warmth that draws others in. The Wood element adds a backbone. Wood Rabbits don't just feel things—they grow from them. They have a way of taking the hard stuff and turning it into something that makes them wiser, kinder, and more grounded than before.

They're the kind of people who make the world feel softer. Wood Rabbits are warm, they pay attention, and they have a way of making the people around them feel like the room they're in is the safest place in the world. The Wood element gives them a quiet strength that doesn't need to be loud to be real.

The challenge? Wood Rabbits sometimes put everyone else's comfort ahead of their own. The growth edge is learning that being kind to yourself is part of being kind to everyone else.

Wait—Am I Actually a Wood Rabbit?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if you were born before February 8, 2035, you're actually a Wood Tiger from 2034, not a Wood Rabbit.

This matters! People born in January often get their zodiac sign wrong. Always check the exact lunar dates to know for sure what is your Chinese zodiac sign.

2035: A Year That Shaped the World

Five moments that defined the Year of the Wood Rabbit.

February 8, 2035
Year of the Wood Rabbit Begins
The lunar new year ushered in the Wood Rabbit, a sign associated with kindness, gentleness, and quiet strength. Cities around the world welcomed the new year with fireworks, family gatherings, and a focus on the people and places that make life feel safe.
April 22, 2035
Global Schools Strike Reaches One Billion Participants
The Global Schools Strike for Climate, held on Earth Day, drew an estimated one billion participants across 150 countries. The strike was the largest coordinated climate protest in human history.
June 30, 2035
World's First Vertical Forest City Opens in Milan
Milan opened the world's first vertical forest city—a 25-acre development with more than 1,000 trees and 5,000 plants growing on the buildings themselves. The development housed 18,000 residents and was designed to absorb as much carbon as a 100-acre traditional forest.
September 9, 2035
Universal Basic Income Pilot Launches in California
California launched the largest universal basic income pilot in U.S. history, providing $1,000 a month to 50,000 residents for three years. The pilot was the first statewide test of UBI in the country.
December 18, 2035
First Human Trials of Longevity Drug Begin
Researchers at the Mayo Clinic began the first human trials of a new class of longevity drug designed to slow the cellular aging process. The Phase 1 trial involved 200 participants aged 60 and older.
