Is 1990 the Year of the Horse for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. The 1990 Chinese zodiac year starts on January 27, 1990, so anyone born before that date is actually an Earth Snake (from the previous year). Only those born between January 27, 1990 and February 14, 1991 are Metal Horses.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early January 1990 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in February. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
