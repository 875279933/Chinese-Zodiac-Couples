What is the Chinese zodiac for 1968?

The 1968 Chinese zodiac is the Earth Monkey. If you were born in 1968, your zodiac sign combines the Monkey's cleverness with the stability of the Earth element.

The 1968 Earth Monkey: Intelligent & Practical

Ever found yourself asking, "What is the Chinese zodiac for 1968?" Let me let you in on it—it's the Year of the Earth Monkey! If your birthday falls between February 18, 1968 and February 6, 1969, you're part of this clever bunch. Earth Monkeys are like that friend who always has a clever solution up their sleeve—super smart, endlessly curious, and quick as a whip.

Monkeys are naturally curious and quick-witted, right? Add Earth into the mix, and you've got someone who's not just intelligent—they're reliable, down-to-earth, and always keep their feet firmly on the ground!

The True Nature of Earth Monkeys

What really makes 1968 Earth Monkeys tick? It's a blend of Monkey wit and Earth groundedness.

Earth Monkeys are the ones who walk into a problem and leave with the answer. Regular Monkeys are already sharp, quick, and full of ideas. Earth Monkeys take all that cleverness and ground it—suddenly the big ideas come with a plan, the jokes come with a kind streak, the fast thinking comes with follow-through. People born in 1968 have a way of making hard things look easy, and they make everyone around them feel a little smarter for being in the room.

What sets Earth Monkeys apart is the way they mix brain and heart. They see angles other people miss, but they also see the people in the room and what they need. They're the friend who solves the problem you didn't know you could talk about, the coworker who turns a meeting around, the one in the family who knows how to fix things—and not just the broken cabinet. They have a steady energy that other people lean on, sometimes without even realizing it.

The flip side? Earth Monkeys can get restless. They're curious, which is great, but they sometimes start the next thing before the current thing is done. They can be too clever for their own good—cutting corners, finding loopholes, winning the argument but losing the point. They also have a hard time asking for help, because being the capable one is part of how they see themselves. The real growth for an Earth Monkey is learning that letting people in isn't the same as letting them down.

Wait—Am I Actually an Earth Monkey?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if you were born before February 18, 1968, you're actually a Fire Goat from 1967, not an Earth Monkey.

This matters! A lot of people born in early February get their zodiac sign wrong. Always double-check your exact birth date to know for sure what is your Chinese zodiac sign.
