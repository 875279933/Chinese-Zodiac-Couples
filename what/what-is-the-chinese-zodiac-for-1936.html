If you were born in 1936, your Chinese zodiac sign is the Fire Rat—a clever, charismatic combination of intelligence and passion.

1936 Fire Rats: Clever, Energetic, and Full of Charm

So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 1936? It's the Year of the Fire Rat! If your birthday falls between February 16, 1936 and February 3, 1937, you're part of this dynamic group. Fire Rats are like the life of the party—smart, energetic, and always ready for the next adventure.

Rats are the first sign in the zodiac, known for being quick-witted and adaptable. Add Fire to the mix, and you get someone who's not just clever—they're passionate and charismatic. These folks have a natural ability to connect with people and turn ideas into reality.

Wait—Am I Actually a Fire Rat?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if you were born before February 16, 1936, you're actually a Wood Pig from 1935, not a Fire Rat.

This matters! A lot of people born in early February get their zodiac sign wrong. Always double-check your exact birth date to know for sure what is your Chinese zodiac sign.
