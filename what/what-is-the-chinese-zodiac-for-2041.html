What is the Chinese zodiac for 2041?

The 2041 Chinese zodiac is the Metal Rooster. If you were born in 2041, your zodiac sign combines the Rooster's diligence with the strength of the Metal element.

The 2041 Metal Rooster: Confident & Hardworking

Wondering what is the Chinese zodiac for 2041? Let me break it down for you—it's the Year of the Metal Rooster! If you were born between February 1, 2041 and January 20, 2042, you carry this powerful sign. Metal Roosters blend reliability with incredible resilience in a way that's truly inspiring.

The Rooster is the tenth animal in the Chinese zodiac—naturally hardworking, confident, and always truthful. Add the Metal element into the equation, and you've got someone who's not just diligent—they're strong, focused, and absolutely unshakable when they set their mind to something.

Is 2041 the Year of the Rooster for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 1, 2041, your zodiac sign is actually the Metal Monkey (from the previous year). Only those born between February 1, 2041 and January 20, 2042 are Metal Roosters.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 2041 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
