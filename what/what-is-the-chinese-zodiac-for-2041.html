Wait—Am I Actually a Metal Rooster?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if you were born before February 1, 2041, you're actually a Metal Monkey from 2040, not a Metal Rooster.

This matters! People born in January often get their zodiac sign wrong. Always check the exact lunar dates to know for sure what is your Chinese zodiac sign.
