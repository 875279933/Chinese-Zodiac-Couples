What is the Chinese zodiac for 1971?

The 1971 Chinese zodiac is the Metal Pig. If you were born in 1971, your zodiac sign combines the Pig's kindness with the strength of the Metal element.

The 1971 Metal Pig: Kind & Loyal

Ever asked yourself, "What is the Chinese zodiac for 1971?" Let me break it down for you—it's the Year of the Metal Pig! If your birthday falls between February 15, 1971 and February 1, 1972, you're one of these wonderfully kind-hearted souls. Metal Pigs are like that warm neighbor who's always sharing their homemade cookies—generous, loyal, and genuinely good-natured through and through.

The Pig is the last sign in the zodiac cycle, all about kindness and compassion. When you add Metal energy to that mix, you get someone who's not just caring—they're rock-solid reliable, honest, and always ready to lend a helping hand to anyone in need.

Is 1971 the Year of the Pig for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 15, 1971, your zodiac sign is actually the Metal Dog (from the previous year). Only those born between February 15, 1971 and February 1, 1972 are Metal Pigs.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 1971 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
