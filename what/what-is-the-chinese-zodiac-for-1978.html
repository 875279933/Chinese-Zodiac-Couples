What is the Chinese zodiac for 1978?

The 1978 Chinese zodiac is the Earth Horse. If you were born in 1978, your zodiac sign combines the Horse's spirit with the stability of the Earth element.

The 1978 Earth Horse: Energetic & Grounded

Ever asked, "What is the Chinese zodiac for 1978?" Let me tell you—it's the Year of the Earth Horse! If your birthday falls between February 7, 1978 and January 27, 1979, you're one of these lively souls. Earth Horses are like that adventurous friend who always has a plan—energetic, independent, but totally grounded. The Horse is seventh in the zodiac, all about spirit and freedom. Add Earth energy, and you get someone who's not just adventurous—they're reliable, practical, and easy to count on. They'll plan the road trip and make sure everyone gets home safe!

Is 1978 the Year of the Horse for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 7, 1978, your zodiac sign is actually the Fire Snake (from the previous year). Only those born between February 7, 1978 and January 27, 1979 are Earth Horses.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 1978 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
