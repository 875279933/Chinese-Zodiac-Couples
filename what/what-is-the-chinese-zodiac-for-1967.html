What is the Chinese zodiac for 1967?

The 1967 Chinese zodiac is the Fire Goat. If you were born in 1967, your zodiac sign combines the Goat's kindness with the passion of the Fire element.

The 1967 Fire Goat: Gentle & Passionate

Curious about "What is the Chinese zodiac for 1967?" Let me clue you in—it's the Year of the Fire Goat! If your birthday falls between January 30, 1967 and February 17, 1968, you're one of these warm, compassionate souls. Fire Goats are like that creative friend who always has a listening ear—kind, artistic, and just oozing warmth.

Goats are naturally gentle and creative by nature. Throw in the Fire element, and you've got someone who's not just caring—they're deeply passionate, expressive, and they light up every room they walk into!

The True Nature of Fire Goats

What really makes 1967 Fire Goats tick? It's a blend of Goat kindness and Fire passion.

Fire Goats are the warmest people in any room, even when they're not saying much. Regular Goats are already gentle, kind, creative—the ones who remember the small things. Fire Goats take all that softness and add something unexpected: real, visible passion. They're not just nice to be around; they're the ones who actually move you, whether it's with what they say, what they make, or how they love. People born in 1967 have a warmth that lingers long after they've left the room.

What sets Fire Goats apart is the way they hold both feeling and fire. They care deeply, but they don't sit still with it—they turn it into art, into action, into something the rest of us get to experience. They're the friend who throws the dinner party nobody else would have thought of, the artist who makes you see the ordinary differently, the parent who turns a tough year into something the family will talk about forever. They also have a generosity that catches people off guard.

The flip side? Fire Goats feel everything, and that takes a toll. They take criticism too personally, hold on to hurt longer than they should, and have a hard time putting their own needs on the list. They can be too easily influenced by the mood of the room, and they sometimes give so much they forget to keep some for themselves. The real growth for a Fire Goat is learning that being kind to others doesn't have to mean being hard on yourself.

Deeply Kind

Fire Goats care in ways that show up. They bring the soup, they make the call, they remember the thing you mentioned once and never forgot.

Quietly Passionate

Fire Goats don't shout about it, but they love deeply and they care loudly. The people closest to them never have to wonder where they stand.

Genuinely Creative

Fire Goats see the world in their own way. They make art, they cook, they decorate, they fix things—the creativity shows up everywhere, not just in the obvious places.

Feels the Room

Fire Goats know what you're feeling before you do. They pick up the shift, the quiet look, the small change—and they almost always say the right thing about it.

Gives More Than Expected

Fire Goats share what they have—time, money, attention, the good pen. They'd rather give too much than too little, and they don't keep score.

Natural Caretaker

Fire Goats make the people around them feel looked after. They notice who's struggling, they show up, and they make the hard season a little softer.

Love, Work & Money

How Fire Goat traits play out in relationships, careers, and finances.

Fire Goats bring their whole selves to everything. In love, they're tender, devoted, and the kind of partner who actually shows up. At work, they're the people who make the team feel human. With money, they're generous to a fault, which is great until it isn't. The trick is learning that taking care of yourself isn't the same as being selfish.

In Relationships

Fire Goats are devoted, romantic partners who want the real thing. They pair best with Rabbits, Pigs, and Horses. They can struggle with Rats or Oxen who feel too sharp or too cold.

At Work

They shine in creative, people-focused work—the arts, design, writing, teaching, counseling, hospitality, fashion, healthcare. Anywhere feeling matters, Fire Goats are at home.

With Money

Fire Goats are generous first, careful second. They love spending on people and experiences, and they have to be intentional about saving or it never happens.

The Balance

Fire Goats thrive when they remember to take care of themselves, too. The people they love will always be okay—but only if they're okay first.

Wait—Am I Actually a Fire Goat?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if you were born before January 30, 1967, you're actually a Fire Horse from 1966, not a Fire Goat.

This matters! A lot of people born in early January get their zodiac sign wrong. Always double-check your exact birth date to know for sure what is your Chinese zodiac sign.
