What is the Chinese zodiac for 2017?

The 2017 Chinese zodiac is the Fire Rooster. If you were born in 2017, your zodiac sign combines the Rooster's diligence with the passion of the Fire element.

The 2017 Fire Rooster: Confident & Hardworking

Curious about what is the Chinese zodiac for 2017? It's the Year of the Fire Rooster! If you were born between January 28, 2017 and February 15, 2018, you're a Fire Rooster. These are the reliable ones who bring energy to everything they do.

Roosters are the go-getters of the zodiac—hardworking, confident, and always honest. When you add Fire to that mix, you get someone who's not just diligent, but also passionate, enthusiastic, and bursting with energy.

Love & Relationships for 2017 Fire Roosters

How do Fire Roosters born in 2017 approach love and relationships?

Fire Roosters are loyal partners who take relationships seriously. They're caring and protective, always looking out for their loved ones. They believe in building stable, long-lasting connections based on trust.

Is 2017 the Year of the Rooster for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before January 28, 2017, your zodiac sign is actually the Fire Monkey (from the previous year). Only those born between January 28, 2017 and February 15, 2018 are Fire Roosters.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early January 2017 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in February. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
