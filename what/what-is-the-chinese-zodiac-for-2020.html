What is the Chinese zodiac for 2020?

The 2020 Chinese zodiac is the Metal Rat—a clever, determined sign that's full of potential.

2020 Metal Rats: Small but Mighty

Curious what is the Chinese zodiac for 2020? It's the Year of the Metal Rat! If your little one was born between January 25, 2020 and February 11, 2021, they're a Metal Rat. These kids are sharp, curious, and already showing signs of big ambition.

Rats are the clever starters of the zodiac—quick-witted, resourceful, and always one step ahead. Add Metal to that mix, and you get a child who's not just smart, but determined. When they set their minds to something, they don't stop until they achieve it.

Love & Relationships

How will your Metal Rat grow to approach relationships?

Metal Rats are loyal, loving partners. They value trust and commitment, and they'll always be there for the people they care about. They might be a bit private at first, but once they open up, they're incredibly devoted.

Who do they connect with best? Dragons, Monkeys, and Oxen appreciate their intelligence and drive. Horses or Goats might find their intensity overwhelming at times.

Career & Finances

What paths will your Metal Rat thrive in as they grow up?

Important: Check the Exact Date!

Remember, the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar. So if your child was born before January 25, 2020, they're actually an Earth Pig from 2019, not a Metal Rat.

This is a common mix-up, especially for January babies! Always double-check the lunar dates to be sure what is your child's Chinese zodiac sign.
