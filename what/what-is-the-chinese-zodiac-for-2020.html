So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 2020? It's the Year of the Metal Rat! If your birthday falls between January 25, 2020 and February 11, 2021, you belong to one of the most strategic and ambitious signs in the zodiac. Metal Rats are the kind of people who see the play before anyone else, and they usually have a plan to win it.

Rats are known for their cleverness, charm, and quick thinking. They figure out how things work, they adapt fast, and they know how to make an opportunity out of almost anything. Add Metal to the mix and you get a Rat that's even sharper, more focused, and more determined. Metal Rats don't just think fast—they finish what they start.

The True Nature of Metal Rats

What really drives 2020 Metal Rats? It's Rat cleverness sharpened by Metal focus.

Metal Rats don't sit around waiting for things to happen—they make things happen. Rats on their own are already quick, curious, and resourceful. They look for the easiest path to the best result, and they usually find it. The Metal element turns up the discipline. Metal Rats don't just come up with great ideas—they actually execute them, often better than anyone expected.

They're confident without being loud about it. Metal Rats know what they're capable of, and they don't need to prove it. They let the work speak, and the work usually speaks for itself.

The challenge? Metal Rats can be too hard on themselves—and sometimes on the people around them. They hold themselves to a high bar, and they don't always give themselves credit for how far they've come. The growth edge is learning to ease up, celebrate the wins, and let other people in on the journey.

Wait—Am I Actually a Metal Rat?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if you were born before January 25, 2020, you're actually an Earth Pig from 2019, not a Metal Rat.

2020: A Year That Shaped the World

Five moments that defined the Year of the Metal Rat.

January 25, 2020
Year of the Metal Rat Begins
The lunar new year ushered in the Metal Rat, kicking off a new 12-year zodiac cycle. Celebrations in cities from Hong Kong to San Francisco rang in a year associated with ambition, renewal, and quick thinking.
March 11, 2020
COVID-19 Declared a Pandemic
The World Health Organization officially declared COVID-19 a global pandemic. Within weeks, countries around the world went into lockdown, reshaping work, school, travel, and daily life for billions of people.
May 25, 2020
George Floyd Killed in Minneapolis
The killing of George Floyd by a police officer in Minneapolis set off nationwide and global protests against police brutality. The movement became one of the largest civil rights mobilizations in U.S. history.
November 3, 2020
Biden Wins the U.S. Presidency
Joe Biden defeated incumbent Donald Trump to win the U.S. presidency, with Kamala Harris becoming the first woman and first Black and South Asian American to be elected vice president.
December 11, 2020
First COVID Vaccine Approved in U.S.
The FDA granted emergency use authorization to Pfizer-BioNTech's COVID-19 vaccine, the first to be approved in the United States. The rollout marked the beginning of the end of the pandemic's most acute phase.
