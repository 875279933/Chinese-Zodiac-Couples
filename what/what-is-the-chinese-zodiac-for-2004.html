What is the Chinese zodiac for 2004?

The 2004 Chinese zodiac is the Wood Monkey. If you were born in 2004, your zodiac sign combines the Monkey's cleverness with the growth of the Wood element.

The 2004 Wood Monkey: Intelligent & Adaptable

So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 2004? It's the Year of the Wood Monkey! If you were born between January 22, 2004 and February 8, 2005, you're a Wood Monkey. This combination makes for a personality that's both clever and versatile.

The Monkey is the ninth animal in the Chinese zodiac cycle, known for being intelligent, curious, and playful. When you add Wood to the mix, you get someone who's not just smart—they're also adaptable, creative, and full of potential.

How do Wood Monkeys born in 2004 approach love and relationships?

Is 2004 the Year of the Monkey for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before January 22, 2004, your zodiac sign is actually the Water Goat (from the previous year). Only those born between January 22, 2004 and February 8, 2005 are Wood Monkeys.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early January 2004 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in February. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
