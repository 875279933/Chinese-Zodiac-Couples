What is the Chinese zodiac for 2004?

The 2004 Chinese zodiac is the Wood Monkey. If you were born in 2004, your zodiac sign combines the Monkey's cleverness with the growth of the Wood element.

The 2004 Wood Monkey: Intelligent & Adaptable

So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 2004? It's the Year of the Wood Monkey! If you were born between January 22, 2004 and February 8, 2005, you're a Wood Monkey. This combination makes for a personality that's both clever and versatile.

The Monkey is the ninth animal in the Chinese zodiac cycle, known for being intelligent, curious, and playful. When you add Wood to the mix, you get someone who's not just smart—they're also adaptable, creative, and full of potential.

The True Nature of Wood Monkeys

What really makes 2004 Wood Monkeys tick? It's Monkey cleverness grown out with Wood heart.

Wood Monkeys are the people who make the room feel a little smarter. Regular Monkeys are already sharp—curious, quick, the kind of person who can read the room in the first five minutes and then make a joke about it. Wood Monkeys take all of that and add a layer underneath. They have the same fast brain, the same way of catching the thing nobody else sees, the same instinct to try the new thing before the new thing becomes obvious—but they also bring the steady, the curious, the kind of follow-through that turns the clever idea into the thing that actually grows. People born in 2004 have a kind of presence that makes the people around them feel a little more awake.

What sets Wood Monkeys apart is the way they mix clever with care. They don't just see the move—they want to know why the move matters. They're the friend who can quote the obscure documentary and still remember the small thing the people they love mentioned three weeks ago, the coworker who turns the chaotic project into the plan that actually lands, the partner who shows up with the joke and the listening ear. They have a real gift for making the people around them feel like life is a little more interesting than it was five minutes ago.

The flip side? Wood Monkeys can be a little too much. They're quick, they're loud, they can be a little too sure of themselves, and they can be a little too quick to move on when the people they love are still catching up. They can confuse being clever with being right—and they can be a little too quick to play the joker when the room actually needs something else. The real growth for a Wood Monkey is learning that not every room needs the punchline, and that the people they love sometimes need the quiet version of them.

Wait—Am I Actually a Wood Monkey?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. The 2004 Wood Monkey year starts on January 22, 2004. So if you were born in early January 2004, you're actually a Water Goat from 2003, not a Wood Monkey.

This matters! A lot of people born in early January get their zodiac sign wrong. Always double-check your exact birth date to know for sure what is your Chinese zodiac sign.

What Happened in 2004

A few of the moments Americans still talk about from the year the Wood Monkeys were born.

January
The Mars Rovers Land on Mars
Spirit landed on Mars on January 3, 2004, and Opportunity followed on January 25. The two rovers spent years sending back photos that every 2004 baby grew up seeing in their textbooks.
April
"The Passion of the Christ" Becomes a Cultural Moment
Mel Gibson's "The Passion of the Christ" opened in February and became a major cultural conversation in 2004—one of those movies a generation of 2004 babies' parents either loved or didn't.
July
"The Bourne Supremacy" and "I, Robot" Hit Theaters
The summer of 2004 had Bourne Supremacy in July, I, Robot also in July, and Spider-Man 2 in June—the kind of big summer for movies 2004 babies' parents still talk about.
September
"Friends" Airs Its Final Episode
After 10 seasons, the last episode of Friends aired on May 6, 2004—and the country spent a week saying goodbye to the show that had been a constant in living rooms since 1994.
November
George W. Bush Wins Re-Election
On November 2, 2004, George W. Bush won a second term over John Kerry. The race went down to Ohio, and the country didn't know the result until the next morning.
