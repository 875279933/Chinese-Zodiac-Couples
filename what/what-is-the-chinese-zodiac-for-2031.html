What is the Chinese zodiac for 2031?

The 2031 Chinese zodiac is the Metal Pig. If you were born in 2031, your zodiac sign combines the Pig's kindness with the strength of the Metal element.

The 2031 Metal Pig: Kind & Generous

So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 2031? It's the Year of the Metal Pig! If you were born between January 23, 2031 and February 10, 2032, you're a Metal Pig. This combination makes for a personality that's both caring and resolute.

The Pig is the twelfth animal in the Chinese zodiac cycle, known for being kind, generous, and optimistic. When you add Metal to the mix, you get someone who's not just warm—they're also strong, determined, and dependable.

Love & Relationships for 2031 Metal Pigs

How do Metal Pigs born in 2031 approach love and relationships?

Metal Pigs are loving partners who value connection. They're affectionate and supportive, always making their loved ones feel cherished. They believe in building relationships based on kindness and mutual respect.

Is 2031 the Year of the Pig for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before January 23, 2031, your zodiac sign is actually the Metal Dog (from the previous year). Only those born between January 23, 2031 and February 10, 2032 are Metal Pigs.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early January 2031 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in February. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
