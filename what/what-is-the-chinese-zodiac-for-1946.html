What is the Chinese zodiac for 1946?

The 1946 Chinese zodiac is the Fire Dog. If you were born in 1946, your zodiac sign combines the Dog's loyalty with the passion of the Fire element.

The 1946 Fire Dog: Loyal & Passionate

So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 1946? It's the Year of the Fire Dog! If you were born between January 22, 1946 and February 9, 1947, you're a Fire Dog. This combination makes for a personality that's both devoted and full of warmth.

The Dog is the eleventh animal in the Chinese zodiac cycle, known for being loyal, honest, and protective. When you add Fire to the mix, you get someone who's not just faithful—they're also passionate, energetic, and deeply caring.

The True Nature of Fire Dogs

What really makes 1946 Fire Dogs tick? It's a blend of Dog loyalty and Fire passion.

The Fire Dog is the most expressive version of an already devoted sign. Dogs are the zodiac's guardians—loyal, honest, and wired to protect the people they love. Fire lights up all that steadiness. It gives Dogs warmth they sometimes hide, energy they sometimes lack, and a willingness to fight for what they believe in that other Dogs only feel quietly.

Most Dogs are cautious creatures. They watch, they wait, they size you up before they trust you. Fire Dogs still do that—but once they're in, they're all in. They love hard, defend harder, and throw themselves into causes, relationships, and work with a passion that can surprise people who thought Dogs were just steady and reliable.

The flip side? Fire Dogs can burn hot. They'll snap when they see injustice, hold grudges longer than they should, and sometimes confuse their strong feelings with being right. Their loyalty is a gift, but it can turn into stubbornness when they refuse to see the flaws in people they love. The lesson? Channel that fire, don't let it channel you. Pick your fights, forgive faster, and remember that being loyal doesn't mean being blind.

Love, Work & Money

How Fire Dog traits play out in relationships, careers, and finances.

Fire Dogs bring loyalty and passion to everything they do. In love, they're devoted partners who'll fight for the relationship. At work, they're the colleagues who have your back no matter what. The trick for them is learning to cool down when their fire runs hot, forgive faster, and not let their protective instincts turn into control.

With Money

Fire Dogs are responsible savers who build security steadily. Fire adds generosity—they'll give freely to people they love and causes they believe in, sometimes too freely.

The Balance

The same fire that fuels Fire Dogs can also burn them out. They thrive when they learn to cool down, let go of grudges, and trust that not every battle is theirs to fight.

Is 1946 the Year of the Dog for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. The 1946 Chinese zodiac year starts on January 22, 1946, so anyone born before that date is actually a Wood Rooster (from the previous year). Only those born between January 22, 1946 and February 9, 1947 are Fire Dogs.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early January 1946 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in February. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!

Five moments that defined the Year of the Fire Dog.

Year of the Fire Dog Begins

At Westminster College in Missouri, Churchill warned that an "iron curtain" had descended across Europe, framing the Cold War that would define the next 45 years.

Italians Vote Out Their King
In a national referendum, Italians narrowly voted to abolish the monarchy and become a republic, ending the House of Savoy's 85-year reign.
July 4, 1946
Philippines Gains Independence
The United States granted independence to the Philippines, ending nearly 50 years of American colonial rule and recognizing the new Republic of the Philippines.
October 16, 1946
Nuremberg Executions
Ten top Nazi leaders were hanged at Nuremberg after the war crimes tribunal found them guilty of crimes against humanity, crimes against peace, and waging aggressive war.
