What is the Chinese zodiac for 1987?

The 1987 Chinese zodiac is the Fire Rabbit. If you were born in 1987, your zodiac sign combines the Rabbit's gentleness with the passion of the Fire element.

The 1987 Fire Rabbit: Warm & Creative

Ever asked what is the Chinese zodiac for 1987? Let me fill you in—it's the Year of the Fire Rabbit! If your birthday falls between January 29, 1987 and February 16, 1988, you're one of these charming souls. Fire Rabbits are like that artistic friend who lights up any gathering—gentle, creative, and full of warmth that makes everyone feel at ease. The Rabbit sits fourth in the zodiac cycle, known for kindness and artistry. Add Fire energy to that mix, and you get someone who's not just caring—they're warm, vibrant, and amazing at expressing themselves. These are the friends who'll paint your portrait for your birthday or write you a heartfelt poem just because.

The True Nature of Fire Rabbits

What really makes 1987 Fire Rabbits tick? It's a blend of Rabbit warmth and Fire sparkle.

Fire Rabbits are the people who walk into a room and make it a little easier to be in. Regular Rabbits are warm, kind, the kind of person who makes everyone else feel like they belong. Fire Rabbits take all of that and add a current of creative spark. They notice the small things, they remember the small things, and they have a way of turning an ordinary evening into something the people they love will talk about for years. People born in 1987 have a kind of emotional warmth that you can feel across a crowded room.

What sets Fire Rabbits apart is the way they mix kindness with creativity. They don't just show up with the practical thing—they show up with the thoughtful thing, the one that says they actually get you. They're the friend who picks the perfect gift without being told what to get, the coworker who quietly makes the office a better place, the partner who turns a Tuesday night into a memory. They have a real gift for noticing the details that other people walk past, and they put that gift to use constantly.

The flip side? Fire Rabbits feel a lot, and not always on their own schedule. They can take on other people's moods without meaning to, they have a hard time saying no when the ask comes from someone they care about, and they sometimes disappear into the background when they should be stepping forward. The real growth for a Fire Rabbit is learning that their kindness doesn't have to come at the cost of their own needs—and that being seen isn't a selfish thing.

Wait—Am I Actually a Fire Rabbit?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. The 1987 Fire Rabbit year starts on January 29, 1987. So if you were born in early January 1987, you're actually a Fire Tiger from 1986, not a Fire Rabbit.

This matters! A lot of people born in early January get their zodiac sign wrong. Always double-check your exact birth date to know for sure what is your Chinese zodiac sign.

What Happened in 1987

A few of the moments Americans still talk about from the year the Fire Rabbits were born.

March
"The Simpsons" Premieres on "The Tracey Ullman Show"
The Simpsons shorts debuted on April 19, kicking off the run that would turn Springfield into the longest-running American animated family of all time.
June
Reagan's "Tear Down This Wall" Speech
In front of the Brandenburg Gate, President Reagan told Mikhail Gorbachev to "tear down this wall." It aired on every network that night.
August
Andy Warhol Passes Away
The pop art icon died on February 22 at 58, closing the chapter he had opened in 1962 with the Campbell's Soup Cans. It was the kind of loss that made front pages for weeks.
October
Black Monday on Wall Street
The Dow dropped 22.6 percent on October 19, 1987—the biggest one-day percentage drop in modern market history, and a story 1987 babies grew up hearing about from their parents.
December
"Dirty Dancing" and "Fatal Attraction" Hit Theaters
Two of the most-quoted movies of the decade—Patrick Swayze and Glenn Close—landed within a year of each other and defined a year at the multiplex.
