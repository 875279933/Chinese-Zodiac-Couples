What is the Chinese zodiac for 1987?

The 1987 Chinese zodiac is the Fire Rabbit. If you were born in 1987, your zodiac sign combines the Rabbit's gentleness with the passion of the Fire element.

The 1987 Fire Rabbit: Warm & Creative

Ever asked what is the Chinese zodiac for 1987? Let me fill you in—it's the Year of the Fire Rabbit! If your birthday falls between January 29, 1987 and February 16, 1988, you're one of these charming souls. The Rabbit sits fourth in the zodiac cycle, known for kindness and artistry. Add Fire energy to that mix, and you get someone who's not just caring—they're warm, vibrant, and amazing at expressing themselves. These are the friends who'll paint your portrait for your birthday or write you a heartfelt poem just because.

Is 1987 the Year of the Rabbit for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. The 1987 Chinese zodiac year starts on January 29, 1987, so anyone born before that date is actually a Fire Tiger (from the previous year). Only those born between January 29, 1987 and February 16, 1988 are Fire Rabbits.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early January 1987 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in February. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
