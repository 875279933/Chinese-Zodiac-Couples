What is the Chinese zodiac for 2023?

The 2023 Chinese zodiac is the Water Rabbit—a gentle, intuitive sign that's full of creativity.

2023 Water Rabbits: Gentle Souls with Creative Hearts

So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 2023? It's the Year of the Water Rabbit! If your little one was born between January 22, 2023 and February 9, 2024, they're a Water Rabbit. These kids are kind, sensitive, and have a natural artistic streak.

Rabbits are known for being gentle, kind, and peace-loving. Add Water to the mix, and you get a child who's not just sweet—they're deeply intuitive and empathetic. They have a way of understanding how others feel, which makes them wonderful friends.

Here are the key traits that define 2023 Water Rabbits—great for understanding your child.

Love & Relationships

How will your Water Rabbit approach relationships as they grow?

Water Rabbits are loving, supportive partners. They value harmony and will do anything to keep their relationships peaceful. They're thoughtful listeners who always have your back.

Who do they connect with best? Goats, Pigs, and Dogs appreciate their gentle nature. Roosters or Rats might find their sensitivity overwhelming at times.

Career & Finances

What paths will your Water Rabbit thrive in as they grow up?

Best Careers

Water Rabbits excel in creative, caring roles—art, design, counseling, writing. Anywhere they can express themselves and help others.

Money Habits

They're cautious with money and prefer security. The Water element makes them wise planners who avoid unnecessary risks.

Important: Check the Exact Date!

Remember, the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar. So if your child was born before January 22, 2023, they're actually a Water Tiger from 2022, not a Water Rabbit.

This is a common mix-up, especially for January babies! Always double-check the lunar dates to be sure what is your child's Chinese zodiac sign.
