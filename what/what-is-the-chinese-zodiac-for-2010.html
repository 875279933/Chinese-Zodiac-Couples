What is the Chinese zodiac for 2010?

The 2010 Chinese zodiac is the Metal Tiger. If you were born in 2010, your zodiac sign combines the Tiger's courage with the strength of the Metal element.

The 2010 Metal Tiger: Brave & Strong

Curious what is the Chinese zodiac for 2010? It's the Year of the Metal Tiger! If your birthday falls between February 14, 2010 and February 2, 2011, you're a Metal Tiger. These are the bold, confident ones who aren't afraid to take charge.

Tigers are the natural leaders of the zodiac—brave, self-assured, and magnetic. When you add Metal to that mix, you get someone who's not just courageous, but also strong-willed, decisive, and totally powerful in everything they do.

Is 2010 the Year of the Tiger for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 14, 2010, your zodiac sign is actually the Earth Ox (from the previous year). Only those born between February 14, 2010 and February 2, 2011 are Metal Tigers.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 2010 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
