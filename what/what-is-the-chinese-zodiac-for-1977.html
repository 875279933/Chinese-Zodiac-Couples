Is 1977 the Year of the Snake for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 18, 1977, your zodiac sign is actually the Fire Dragon (from the previous year). Only those born between February 18, 1977 and February 6, 1978 are Fire Snakes.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 1977 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
