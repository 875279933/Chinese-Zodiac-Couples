Is 1956 the Year of the Monkey for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. The 1956 Chinese zodiac year starts on January 31, 1956, so anyone born before that date is actually a Wood Goat (from the previous year). Only those born between January 31, 1956 and February 17, 1957 are Fire Monkeys.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early January 1956 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in February. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
