Wood Snakes love with intensity but keep the feelings private. Wood Snakes risk running themselves into the ground before they ask for help. The improvement comes from learning to delegate and from scheduling downtime the way they schedule meetings. They don't need to be in every conversation to still be in charge of the outcome.

Born in early 1965? Check Before You Claim Wood Snake

The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, so the 1965 year of the chinese zodiac actually starts on Feb 2, 1965. Anyone born before that date is a Wood Dragon from 1964, not the Wood Snake.

This is one of the most common mix-ups in the chinese zodiac, because the lunar new year falls in late January in 1965, earlier than the more familiar February dates. Always double-check the lunar date before settling on a sign. The right year matters more than the right month when it comes to figuring out the year of the chinese zodiac you belong to.
