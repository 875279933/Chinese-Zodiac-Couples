Is 1965 the Year of the Snake for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. The 1965 Chinese zodiac year starts on January 21, 1965, so anyone born before that date is actually a Wood Dragon (from the previous year). Only those born between January 21, 1965 and February 8, 1966 are Wood Snakes.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early January 1965 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in February. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
