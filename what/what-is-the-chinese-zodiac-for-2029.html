2029 Earth Roosters: Confident, Honest, and Built to Last

So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 2029? It's the Year of the Earth Rooster! If your birthday falls between February 13, 2029 and February 2, 2030, you belong to one of the most dependable and hardworking signs in the zodiac. Earth Roosters are the kind of people who say what they mean and mean what they say.

Roosters are known for their confidence, their sharp eye, and their directness. They don't sugarcoat things, they don't play games, and they have a clear idea of what's right and what isn't. Add Earth to the mix and you get a Rooster that's a little more patient, a little more grounded, and a little more in it for the long run. Earth Roosters are still sharp—but they're also steady.

They're the kind of people who make things happen. Earth Roosters are organized, they follow through, and they have a way of getting things done that the people around them quietly appreciate. They don't just talk about it—they show up and do it.

The challenge? Earth Roosters can be hard on themselves—and on the people around them. The growth edge is learning that the highest standards in the room don't always need to be the loudest.

Section Two

Love, Work & Money

How Earth Rooster traits shape the way 2029 babies handle relationships, careers, and finances.

Earth Roosters bring their confident, direct energy into every part of life. In love, they're honest and dependable—they don't play games. At work, they thrive in roles with clear goals and a chance to lead. With money, they're disciplined and good with a plan. The only real risk? They sometimes hold themselves—and everyone else—to a standard that's hard to reach.

In Relationships

Earth Roosters are honest, loyal, and a little no-nonsense. They match well with Oxen, Snakes, and Dragons—signs that respect their directness and match their work ethic. They can find Rabbits a little too soft, but opposites can still work with effort.

At Work

Earth Roosters thrive in roles with clear goals and a chance to lead—management, finance, business, law, healthcare administration, the military, entrepreneurship. They hate chaos and they hate unclear expectations. They do their best work when the goal is clear and the bar is high.

With Money

Earth Roosters are disciplined with money. They save, they plan, they don't take wild risks, and they have a long-term strategy. The Earth element makes them patient enough to let the money grow.

The Balance

Earth Roosters thrive when they learn to be a little easier on themselves—and a little easier on everyone else. Perfect isn't the goal. Showing up is.

2029: A Year That Shaped the World

Five moments that defined the Year of the Earth Rooster.

February 13, 2029
Year of the Earth Rooster Begins
The lunar new year ushered in the Earth Rooster, a sign associated with honesty, discipline, and quiet confidence. Cities around the world welcomed the new year with fireworks, family dinners, and a renewed focus on building things that last.
April 13, 2029
Asteroid Apophis Makes Close Pass
Asteroid 99942 Apophis passed within 20,000 miles of Earth—closer than some satellites. The pass marked the closest approach of a large asteroid in recorded history and gave scientists a once-in-a-millennium chance to study a near-Earth object up close.
June 6, 2029
First Crewed Moon Landing in 50+ Years
NASA's Artemis V mission landed the first crewed astronauts on the lunar surface since 1972, this time near the Moon's south pole. The mission marked the beginning of a permanent human presence on the Moon.
August 15, 2029
Global Heat Record Broken Three Times in One Summer
For the third summer in a row, global average temperatures set a new all-time record, with July 2029 becoming the hottest month ever measured. The World Meteorological Organization declared the era of "climate normal" was over.
October 28, 2029
UN Summit on Ocean Conservation Closes in Lisbon
More than 100 countries signed the Lisbon Ocean Pact, committing to protect 50% of the world's oceans by 2035. The agreement was the largest ocean conservation deal in history.
