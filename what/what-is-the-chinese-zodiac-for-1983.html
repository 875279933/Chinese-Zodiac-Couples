What is the Chinese zodiac for 1983?

The 1983 Chinese zodiac is the Water Pig. If you were born in 1983, your zodiac sign combines the Pig's kindness with the sensitivity of the Water element.

The 1983 Water Pig: Kind & Compassionate

Ever asked, "What is the Chinese zodiac for 1983?" Let me fill you in—it's the Year of the Water Pig! If your birthday falls between February 13, 1983 and February 1, 1984, you're one of these kind-hearted souls. Water Pigs are like that warm friend who always shares—generous, optimistic, and totally genuine. The Pig is last in the zodiac, all about kindness and loyalty. Add Water energy, and you get someone who's not just caring—they're compassionate, intuitive, and great at making people feel loved. They'll bake cookies for the whole neighborhood!

The True Nature of Water Pigs

What really makes 1983 Water Pigs tick? It's a blend of Pig generosity and Water warmth.

Water Pigs are the people who make the room feel like home the moment they walk into it. Regular Pigs are warm, generous, the kind of person who shows up with the thing you didn't know you needed. Water Pigs take all of that and add a current of emotional depth that runs underneath everything they do. They feel things in a big way, and they don't apologize for it. People born in 1983 have a kind of emotional generosity that's pretty hard to fake, and pretty hard to miss when it's in front of you.

What sets Water Pigs apart is the way they mix kindness with real feeling. They don't do the polite, surface-level version of caring—they go all the way in, even when the situation doesn't really call for it. They're the friend who brings the soup when you're sick and stays to make sure you actually ate it, the coworker who notices the off day and checks in without making it a thing, the partner who can read the mood in a heartbeat and shows up with the right thing at the right time. They make the people they love feel like the most important people in the world.

The flip side? Water Pigs feel everything, and not always on their own schedule. They take on other people's problems like they're theirs to fix, they have a hard time saying no, and they can over-give in a way that leaves them running on empty. The real growth for a Water Pig is learning that being kind to the people they love includes being kind to themselves—and that they don't have to pour from an empty cup.

Wait—Am I Actually a Water Pig?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if you were born before February 13, 1983, you're actually a Water Dog from 1982, not a Water Pig.

This matters! A lot of people born in early February get their zodiac sign wrong. Always double-check your exact birth date to know for sure what is your Chinese zodiac sign.
