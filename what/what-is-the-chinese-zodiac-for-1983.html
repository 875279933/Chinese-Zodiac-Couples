What is the Chinese zodiac for 1983?

The 1983 Chinese zodiac is the Water Pig. If you were born in 1983, your zodiac sign combines the Pig's kindness with the sensitivity of the Water element.

The 1983 Water Pig: Kind & Compassionate

Ever asked, "What is the Chinese zodiac for 1983?" Let me fill you in—it's the Year of the Water Pig! If your birthday falls between February 13, 1983 and February 1, 1984, you're one of these kind-hearted souls. Water Pigs are like that warm friend who always shares—generous, optimistic, and totally genuine. The Pig is last in the zodiac, all about kindness and loyalty. Add Water energy, and you get someone who's not just caring—they're compassionate, intuitive, and great at making people feel loved. They'll bake cookies for the whole neighborhood!

Is 1983 the Year of the Pig for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 13, 1983, your zodiac sign is actually the Water Dog (from the previous year). Only those born between February 13, 1983 and February 1, 1984 are Water Pigs.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 1983 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
