What is the Chinese zodiac for 2021?

The 2021 Chinese zodiac is the Metal Ox. If you were born in 2021, your zodiac sign combines the Ox's reliability with the strength of the Metal element.

The 2021 Metal Ox: Dependable & Hardworking

So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 2021? It's the Year of the Metal Ox! If you were born between February 12, 2021 and January 31, 2022, you're a Metal Ox. This combination makes for a personality that's both strong and trustworthy.

The Ox is the second animal in the Chinese zodiac cycle, known for being dependable, hardworking, and steady. When you add Metal to the mix, you get someone who's not just reliable—they're also strong, disciplined, and determined.

Love & Relationships for 2021 Metal Oxen

How do Metal Oxen born in 2021 approach love and relationships?

Metal Oxen are loyal partners who value stability. They're devoted and protective, always showing their love through actions rather than words. They believe in building long-lasting relationships based on trust and commitment.

When it comes to compatibility, Metal Oxen tend to get along best with Roosters, Snakes, and Rats. These signs appreciate the Ox's reliability. They may need to be mindful with Horses or Goats.

Is 2021 the Year of the Ox for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 12, 2021, your zodiac sign is actually the Metal Rat (from the previous year). Only those born between February 12, 2021 and January 31, 2022 are Metal Oxen.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 2021 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
