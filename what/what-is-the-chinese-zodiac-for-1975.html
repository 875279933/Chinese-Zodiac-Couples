What is the Chinese zodiac for 1975?

The 1975 Chinese zodiac is the Wood Rabbit. If you were born in 1975, your zodiac sign combines the Rabbit's grace with the growth of the Wood element.

The 1975 Wood Rabbit: Gentle & Creative

Wondering "What is the Chinese zodiac for 1975?" Let me tell you—it's the Year of the Wood Rabbit! If your birthday falls between February 11, 1975 and January 30, 1976, you're one of these wonderfully gentle souls. Wood Rabbits are like that artistic friend who lights up every room they enter—charming, creative, and oozing with grace.

The Rabbit sits fourth in the zodiac, all about gentleness and creativity. Add Wood energy to that, and you get someone who's not just graceful—they're wildly imaginative, vibrant, and they bring beauty to everything they touch.

The True Nature of Wood Rabbits

What really makes 1975 Wood Rabbits tick? It's a blend of Rabbit grace and Wood growth.

Wood Rabbits are the people you want to sit next to at a dinner party. Regular Rabbits are already kind, easy to be around, somehow make the world feel a little softer just by being in it. Wood Rabbits take all that grace and add something else: real, visible growth. They don't just stay gentle—they get better at it, year after year, in a way that doesn't really need to announce itself. People born in 1975 have a quiet kind of beauty that other people feel, even when they can't put words to it.

What sets Wood Rabbits apart is the way they mix softness with substance. They care about people, they care about the work, and they care about doing things the right way. They're the friend who makes a hard day lighter, the coworker who makes the office feel less like an office, the partner whose presence is the reason the house feels like a home. They have a kind of taste—food, design, music, whatever it is—that other people quietly look to.

The flip side? Wood Rabbits can be too careful. They avoid conflict when the conflict is needed, hold back when they should speak up, and sometimes trade being happy for being safe. They take criticism too personally and have a hard time putting their own needs on the list. The real growth for a Wood Rabbit is learning that being kind to others doesn't have to mean disappearing yourself.

Love, Work & Money

How Wood Rabbit traits play out in relationships, careers, and finances.

Wood Rabbits are warm, easy to love, and a little better at taking care of everyone else than themselves. In love, they're tender, devoted, and the kind of partner who actually shows up. At work, they're the people who make the team feel human. With money, they're careful in a way that doesn't feel stingy. The trick is learning that taking care of yourself isn't the same as being selfish.

In Relationships

Wood Rabbits are devoted, romantic partners who want the real thing. They pair best with Goats, Pigs, and Dogs. They can struggle with Roosters or Rats who feel too sharp or too direct.

At Work

They shine in creative, people-focused work—the arts, design, writing, teaching, counseling, hospitality, fashion, healthcare, beauty. Anywhere feeling matters, Wood Rabbits are at home.

With Money

Wood Rabbits are careful, thoughtful with money. They save without making a show of it, and they spend on the things that actually matter to them.

The Balance

Wood Rabbits thrive when they remember to put themselves on the list. The people they love will always be okay—but only if the Rabbit is okay first.

Wait—Am I Actually a Wood Rabbit?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if you were born before February 11, 1975, you're actually a Wood Tiger from 1974, not a Wood Rabbit.

This matters! A lot of people born in early February get their zodiac sign wrong. Always double-check your exact birth date to know for sure what is your Chinese zodiac sign.
