Born in early 1960? Check Before You Claim Metal Rat

The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, so the 1960 year of the chinese zodiac actually starts on Jan 28, 1960. Anyone born before that date is an Earth Pig from 1959, not the Metal Rat.

This is one of the most common mix-ups in the chinese zodiac, because the lunar new year falls in late January in 1960, earlier than the more familiar February dates. Always double-check the lunar date before settling on a sign. The right year matters more than the right month when it comes to figuring out the year of the chinese zodiac you belong to.
