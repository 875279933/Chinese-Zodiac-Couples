Is 1960 the Year of the Rat for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 17, 1960, your zodiac sign is actually the Earth Pig (from the previous year). Only those born between February 17, 1960 and February 4, 1961 are Metal Rats.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 1960 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
