Is 1963 the Year of the Rabbit for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 13, 1963, your zodiac sign is actually the Water Tiger (from the previous year). Only those born between February 13, 1963 and January 31, 1964 are Water Rabbits.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 1963 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
