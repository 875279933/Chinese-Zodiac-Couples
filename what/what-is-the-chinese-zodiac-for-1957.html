The 1957 Chinese zodiac is the Fire Rooster. If you were born in 1957, your zodiac sign combines the Rooster's confidence with the passion of the Fire element.

Is 1957 the Year of the Rooster for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 18, 1957, your zodiac sign is actually the Fire Monkey (from the previous year). Only those born between February 18, 1957 and February 7, 1958 are Fire Roosters.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 1957 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
