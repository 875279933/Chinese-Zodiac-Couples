Love, Work, Money & Balance

How the Fire Rooster personality shows up across the four areas of life that shape anyone born in 1957.

In Relationships

Fire Roosters are loyal and attentive once they commit, but they take a long time to open up. They prefer a small inner circle to a wide social circle. Oxen, Dragons, and Monkeys tend to match the Fire Rooster's pace best. They show love through steady acts, not big speeches.

At Work

Fire Roosters fit best in roles that reward careful thinking—finance, research, operations, engineering, agriculture, craftsmanship, accounting. They are reliable, consistent, and great at finding the flaw in a plan before it goes live. They dislike flashy workplaces and prefer stable teams.

Fire Roosters are conservative with cash and patient with investments. They build savings rather than chase trends, and they have a strong sense of value. The risk to watch is over-caution—sitting on a strong idea for too long while opportunities move on.
