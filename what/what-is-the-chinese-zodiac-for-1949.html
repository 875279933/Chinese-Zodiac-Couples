The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, so the 1949 year of the chinese zodiac actually starts on January 29, 1949. Anyone born before that date is an Earth Rat from 1948, not an Earth Ox.

This catches a lot of people. Always double-check the lunar date before settling on a sign. The right year matters more than the right month when figuring out which year of the chinese zodiac you belong to.

Al Capp's Li'l Abner became the first daily comic strip published in color in U.S. newspapers, running in the New York Mirror and others.
