Love, Work, Money & Balance

How the Metal Rabbit personality shows up across the four areas of life that shape anyone born in 1951.

Metal Rabbits are attentive partners who show love through small acts—remembering a preference, fixing something before it broke, leaving a note. They need a partner who respects their quiet side and does not push for constant noise. Goats, Dogs, and Pigs tend to match the Metal Rabbit's pace. They take commitment seriously and dislike games.

Born in January 1951? Check Before You Claim Metal Rabbit

The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, so the 1951 year of the chinese zodiac actually starts on February 6, 1951. Anyone born before that date is a Metal Tiger from 1950, not a Metal Rabbit.

This trips up a lot of people. Always double-check the lunar date before settling on a sign. The right year matters more than the right month when figuring out which year of the chinese zodiac you belong to.
