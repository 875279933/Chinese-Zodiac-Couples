2019 Earth Pigs: Generous, Warm, and Easy to Be Around

So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 2019? It's the Year of the Earth Pig! If your birthday falls between February 5, 2019 and January 24, 2020, you belong to one of the most easygoing and good-hearted signs in the zodiac. Earth Pigs are the kind of people who make everyone around them feel a little more relaxed.

Pigs are known for their kindness, generosity, and love of life. They care about the people around them, they don't hold grudges, and they make time for the good stuff. Add Earth to the mix and you get a Pig that's a little more grounded, a little more reliable, and a little more focused on building things that last. Earth Pigs don't just enjoy life—they share it with the people they love.

Section OneThe True Nature of Earth Pigs

What really drives 2019 Earth Pigs? It's Pig warmth with Earth stability.

Earth Pigs are the kind of people everyone feels comfortable around. They don't bring drama, they don't play games, and they don't pretend to be something they're not. Pigs on their own are already warm, generous, and good-natured. They love a good meal, a good friend, and a good laugh. The Earth element adds a layer of groundedness that keeps all that warmth from being scattered. Earth Pigs know what matters, and they put their energy there.

They're generous in a way that doesn't keep score. Earth Pigs give what they can—whether it's time, money, attention, or help—without expecting anything back. They make the people in their life feel valued, and they don't even realize how much it means.

The challenge? Earth Pigs sometimes trust too easily. They want to see the good in people, and they sometimes miss the red flags. The growth edge is learning that generosity doesn't have to mean being walked on—and that saying no, sometimes, is part of taking care of themselves.

Section TwoLove, Work & Money

How Earth Pig traits shape the way 2019 babies handle relationships, careers, and finances.

Earth Pigs bring their warm, easy energy into every part of life. In love, they're devoted partners who create a real sense of home. At work, they thrive in roles that let them help and connect. With money, they're careful and they enjoy the good things in life. The only real risk? They sometimes give too much—to the wrong people, for the wrong reasons—and forget to save some for themselves.

01

In Relationships

Earth Pigs are warm, loyal, and deeply devoted. They match well with Rabbits, Goats, and Tigers—signs that share their warmth and don't bring unnecessary drama. They can find Snakes a little too guarded, but opposites can still attract.

02

At Work

Earth Pigs thrive in roles that let them help, create, and connect—teaching, hospitality, food, healthcare, the arts, counseling, community work, design. They work best in environments where the culture is as important as the output, and they make great teammates because they lift everyone up.

03

With Money

Earth Pigs are careful with cash but love the good things. They save for what matters, they spend on quality, and they don't believe in suffering for the sake of it. The Earth element keeps them from going overboard either way.

04

The Balance

Earth Pigs thrive when they remember that generosity has limits. Giving is good, but giving until they're empty isn't. Saying no sometimes, and putting themselves first occasionally, is what keeps them able to keep giving long-term.

Wait—Am I Actually an Earth Pig?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if you were born before February 5, 2019, you're actually an Earth Dog from 2018, not an Earth Pig.

This matters! People born in January and early February often get their zodiac sign wrong. Always check the exact lunar dates to know for sure what is your Chinese zodiac sign.
