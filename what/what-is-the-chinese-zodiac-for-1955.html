What is the Chinese zodiac for 1955?

The 1955 Chinese zodiac is the Wood Goat. If you were born in 1955, your zodiac sign combines the Goat's kindness with the growth of the Wood element.

The 1955 Wood Goat: Gentle & Artistic

Ever asked, "What is the Chinese zodiac for 1955?" Let me share—it's the Year of the Wood Goat! If your birthday falls between February 12, 1955 and January 30, 1956, you're part of this gentle group. Wood Goats have this beautiful mix of compassion and creativity that makes them wonderful to be around. The Goat is eighth in the zodiac cycle—naturally gentle, artistic, and kind-hearted. Add Wood to that? You get someone who's not just caring—they're imaginative, nurturing, and always growing.

Is 1955 the Year of the Goat for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 12, 1955, your zodiac sign is actually the Wood Horse (from the previous year). Only those born between February 12, 1955 and January 30, 1956 are Wood Goats.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 1955 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
