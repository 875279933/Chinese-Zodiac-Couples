The 1989 Chinese zodiac is the Earth Snake. If you were born in 1989, your zodiac sign combines the Snake's wisdom with the stability of the Earth element.

The 1989 Earth Snake: Wise & Practical

Ever asked what is the Chinese zodiac for 1989? Let me tell you—it's the Year of the Earth Snake! If your birthday falls between February 6, 1989 and January 26, 1990, you're one of these wise souls. The Snake is the sixth animal in the zodiac cycle, known for wisdom and grace. Add Earth energy to that mix, and you get someone who's not just clever—they're reliable, down-to-earth, and amazing at seeing the big picture. These are the friends who'll help you map out your career path or plan your dream vacation like it's second nature!

Is 1989 the Year of the Snake for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 6, 1989, your zodiac sign is actually the Earth Dragon (from the previous year). Only those born between February 6, 1989 and January 26, 1990 are Earth Snakes.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 1989 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
