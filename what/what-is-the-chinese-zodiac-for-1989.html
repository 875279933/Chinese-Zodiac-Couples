What is the Chinese zodiac for 1989?

The 1989 Chinese zodiac is the Earth Snake. If you were born in 1989, your zodiac sign combines the Snake's wisdom with the stability of the Earth element.

The 1989 Earth Snake: Wise & Practical

Ever asked what is the Chinese zodiac for 1989? Let me tell you—it's the Year of the Earth Snake! If your birthday falls between February 6, 1989 and January 26, 1990, you're one of these wise souls. Earth Snakes are like that thoughtful friend who always has the best advice when you're stuck—intuitive, practical, and totally grounded in reality. The Snake is the sixth animal in the zodiac cycle, known for wisdom and grace. Add Earth energy to that mix, and you get someone who's not just clever—they're reliable, down-to-earth, and amazing at seeing the big picture. These are the friends who'll help you map out your career path or plan your dream vacation like it's second nature!

The True Nature of Earth Snakes

What really makes 1989 Earth Snakes tick? It's a blend of Snake wisdom and Earth steadiness.

Earth Snakes are the people in the room who are already three moves ahead—and somehow still calm about it. Regular Snakes are sharp, intuitive, the kind of person who reads a situation before the rest of the room has even looked up from their phone. Earth Snakes take all of that and anchor it. They have the same insight, the same quiet confidence, the same instinct for what comes next—but they don't rush. They wait, they watch, and they move at exactly the right moment. People born in 1989 have a kind of grounded wisdom that makes the people around them feel like they're in good hands.

What sets Earth Snakes apart is the way they mix smarts with stability. They don't just see the answer—they actually have the patience to wait for it. They're the friend you call when you can't tell if someone is bluffing, the coworker who somehow already knew the meeting was going to go sideways, the partner who reads the room before you even walk in. They have a real gift for seeing the long game—and they have the kind of discipline it takes to actually play it.

The flip side? Earth Snakes can be hard to read, and that can be hard on the people who love them. They keep things to themselves, they hold their cards close, and they sometimes mistake being quiet for being safe. They can be slow to open up, slow to trust, and slow to let the people who care about them see what's really going on. The real growth for an Earth Snake is learning that the people worth letting in don't need the polished version of you—and that letting yourself be seen isn't the same as losing the upper hand.

Wait—Am I Actually an Earth Snake?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if you were born before February 6, 1989, you're actually an Earth Dragon from 1988, not an Earth Snake.

This matters! A lot of people born in early February get their zodiac sign wrong. Always double-check your exact birth date to know for sure what is your Chinese zodiac sign.

A few of the moments Americans still talk about from the year the Earth Snakes were born.

The tanker ran aground in Alaska's Prince William Sound on March 24, dumping 11 million gallons of crude and turning the spill into a national story for months.

October
The San Francisco Earthquake
The Loma Prieta quake hit on October 17 just before the World Series, killing 63 people, collapsing a section of the Bay Bridge, and pausing the A's vs. Giants game live on TV.
November
The Berlin Wall Falls
On November 9, East and West Berliners climbed the wall together. The story ran on every American network for weeks, and it was the kind of moment '89 babies grew up hearing about in history class.
