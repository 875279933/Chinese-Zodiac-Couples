Love, Work, Money & Balance

How the Water Dragon personality shows up across the four areas of life that shape anyone born in 1952.

In Relationships

Water Dragons love with intensity and creativity. They plan surprises, send the right message at the right time, and remember the small detail. They need a partner who gives them space to dream. Roosters, Monkeys, and Rats tend to match the Water Dragon's pace. They get bored by routine and refreshed by a partner who keeps up.

At Work

Water Dragons thrive in roles that reward original thinking—advertising, film, design, founding, sales leadership, architecture, strategy. Water Dragons swing between inspired bets and quiet saving. The Water element softens the Dragon's usual risk, so they tend to be more careful than people expect. The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, so the 1952 year of the chinese zodiac actually starts on January 27, 1952. Anyone born before that date is a Metal Rabbit from 1951, not a Water Dragon.

This trips up a lot of people. Always double-check the lunar date before settling on a sign. The right year matters more than the right month when figuring out which year of the chinese zodiac you belong to.
