What is the Chinese zodiac for 2024?

The 2024 Chinese zodiac is the Wood Dragon—a powerful sign that combines the Dragon's ambition with Wood's growth energy.

2024 Wood Dragons: Born to Lead

So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 2024? It's the Year of the Wood Dragon! If your little one is born between February 10, 2024 and January 28, 2025, they're a Wood Dragon. This is a sign of power, creativity, and boundless potential.

Dragons are the royalty of the zodiac—confident, charismatic, and born leaders. When you add the Wood element, you get someone who's not just ambitious—they're also nurturing, creative, and focused on growth. Think of a dragon tending a garden that grows into something magnificent.

How will your Wood Dragon approach relationships as they grow?

Wood Dragons are passionate partners who love grand gestures. They're generous and protective, always making their loved ones feel special. But they also need partners who can keep up with their energy and ambition.

Who do they connect with best? Roosters, Monkeys, and Rats appreciate their drive and charisma. Dogs or Goats might find their intensity a bit overwhelming—balance is key.

Important: Check the Exact Date!

Remember, the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if your baby is born before February 10, 2024, they're actually a Water Rabbit from 2023, not a Wood Dragon.

This is a common point of confusion—especially for February babies! Always check the lunar dates to be sure what is your child's Chinese zodiac sign.
