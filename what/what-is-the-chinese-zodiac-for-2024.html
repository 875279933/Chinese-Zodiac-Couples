2024: A Year That Shaped the World

Five moments that defined the Year of the Wood Dragon.

February 10, 2024
Year of the Wood Dragon Begins
The lunar new year ushered in the Wood Dragon, a sign associated with ambition, growth, and big changes. The dragon is considered the luckiest of the zodiac animals, and the year kicked off with high hopes for fresh starts and bold new projects.
April 8, 2024
Total Solar Eclipse Crosses the U.S.
A rare total solar eclipse swept across North America, from Mexico through the United States to Canada, plunging millions of people into midday darkness. It was the last total solar eclipse visible from the contiguous U.S. until 2044.
June 28, 2024
Boeing Starliner Crew Launched
Boeing's Starliner spacecraft carried two NASA astronauts to the International Space Station on its first crewed test flight. The mission was a major milestone for Boeing's commercial crew program, though the astronauts would remain on the ISS much longer than planned after thruster and helium leak issues were discovered.
July 26, 2024
Paris Olympics Open
The 2024 Summer Olympics opened in Paris with a striking ceremony along the Seine. The Games featured Simone Biles' triumphant return to gymnastics, Léon Marchand's swimming sweep, and a men's basketball run by a revitalized Team USA.
November 13, 2024
Boeing Starliner Crew Returns Home
NASA astronauts Butch Wilmore and Suni Williams finally returned to Earth aboard a SpaceX Crew Dragon after an extended stay on the ISS caused by Boeing Starliner helium leaks. The mission highlighted the ongoing shift toward commercial spaceflight partnerships.
