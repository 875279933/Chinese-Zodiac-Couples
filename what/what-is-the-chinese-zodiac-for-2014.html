What is the Chinese zodiac for 2014?

The 2014 Chinese zodiac is the Wood Horse. If you were born in 2014, your zodiac sign combines the Horse's energy with the growth of the Wood element.

The 2014 Wood Horse: Energetic & Independent

Ever asked what is the Chinese zodiac for 2014? It's the Year of the Wood Horse! If you were born between January 31, 2014 and February 18, 2015, you're a Wood Horse. These are the free spirits who combine energy with a drive to grow.

Horses are the adventurers of the zodiac—energetic, independent, and always ready for the next journey. When you add Wood to that mix, you get someone who's not just active, but also growth-oriented, creative, and bursting with vitality.

Love & Relationships for 2014 Wood Horses

How do Wood Horses born in 2014 approach love and relationships?

Wood Horses are passionate lovers who bring excitement to relationships. They're romantic and affectionate, always keeping the spark alive. They believe in living in the moment and enjoying every second together.

Is 2014 the Year of the Horse for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before January 31, 2014, your zodiac sign is actually the Water Snake (from the previous year). Only those born between January 31, 2014 and February 18, 2015 are Wood Horses.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early January 2014 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in February. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
