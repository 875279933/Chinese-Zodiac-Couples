What is the Chinese zodiac for 2018?

The 2018 Chinese zodiac is the Earth Dog. If you were born in 2018, your zodiac sign combines the Dog's loyalty with the stability of the Earth element.

The 2018 Earth Dog: Loyal & Grounded

Wondering what is the Chinese zodiac for 2018? Let me share—it's the Year of the Earth Dog! If your birthday is between February 16, 2018 and February 4, 2019, you're an Earth Dog. These are the faithful, reliable ones you can always count on.

Dogs are the loyal companions of the zodiac—protective, honest, and always there for you. When you combine that with Earth, you get someone who's not just devoted, but also grounded, practical, and totally dependable.

Love & Relationships for 2018 Earth Dogs

How do Earth Dogs born in 2018 approach love and relationships?

Is 2018 the Year of the Dog for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 16, 2018, your zodiac sign is actually the Fire Rooster (from the previous year). Only those born between February 16, 2018 and February 4, 2019 are Earth Dogs.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 2018 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
