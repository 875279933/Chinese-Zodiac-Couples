What is the Chinese zodiac for 2018?

If you were born in 2018, your Chinese zodiac sign is the Earth Dog—a loyal, steady mix of honesty and care.

2018 Earth Dogs: Loyal, Honest, and Always There

So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 2018? It's the Year of the Earth Dog! If your birthday falls between February 16, 2018 and February 4, 2019, you belong to one of the most loyal and dependable signs in the zodiac. Earth Dogs are the kind of people who show up for the people they love—no matter what.

Dogs are known for their honesty, loyalty, and sense of fairness. They care about doing the right thing, and they don't let the people they love down. Add Earth to the mix and you get a Dog that's even more grounded, even more reliable, and even more willing to put in the work. Earth Dogs don't just say they'll be there—they are there, every single time.

The True Nature of Earth Dogs

What really drives 2018 Earth Dogs? It's Dog loyalty with Earth stability.

Earth Dogs are the kind of people everyone wants in their corner. They don't just show up for the fun parts—they show up for the hard parts, the awkward parts, the parts where most other people disappear. Dogs on their own are already loyal and protective. They feel responsible for the people they care about, and they take that responsibility seriously. The Earth element makes that loyalty even more solid. Earth Dogs don't just care about their people—they show it, consistently, over years.

They're honest in a way that feels safe. Earth Dogs tell the truth, but they do it with care. They don't tear people down—they just say what needs to be said, kindly, and they keep it moving.

The challenge? Earth Dogs carry too much sometimes. They take on the worries of the people they love and forget to set any of it down. The growth edge is learning that they can be there for others without taking on all the weight—and that asking for help is just as brave as giving it.

Steadfastly Loyal

Earth Dogs don't leave. They're the friend who calls, the partner who shows up, the colleague who remembers the promise they made three months ago. Loyalty isn't a word for them—it's a way of life.

Protective Without Being Controlling

Earth Dogs stand up for the people they love. They speak up when something feels off, and they don't let the people in their life get pushed around. It's quiet strength, not showy strength.

Honest in the Best Way

Earth Dogs tell the truth—but they do it with care. They don't use honesty as a weapon. They just say what needs to be said, kindly, and they trust the people they love to handle it.

Dependably Steady

Earth Dogs are the calm in any room. They don't get swept up in drama, and they don't flake when things get hard. People lean on them because they've earned it.

Deeply Caring

Earth Dogs notice when someone is off. They check in, they listen, they remember the small things. The care they show is the kind that makes people feel safe being themselves.

Strongly Fair-Minded

Earth Dogs care about doing the right thing. They stand up for fairness, they call out unfairness, and they don't play favorites. People trust their judgment because it usually is fair.

Love, Work & Money

How Earth Dog traits shape the way 2018 babies handle relationships, careers, and finances.

Earth Dogs bring their warm, steady energy into every part of life. In love, they're devoted partners who show up. At work, they thrive in roles that let them help, support, and protect. With money, they're careful and grounded. The only real risk? They sometimes take on everyone else's problems and forget to take care of their own.

In Relationships

Earth Dogs are loyal, devoted, and deeply committed once they trust someone. They match well with Rabbits, Tigers, and Horses—signs that appreciate their steadiness and bring some spark. They can find Monkeys a little too unpredictable, but opposites can still work.

At Work

Earth Dogs thrive in roles where they can help and protect—medicine, law, social work, teaching, counseling, human resources, public service, emergency response. They do their best work when the mission matters more than the spotlight, and they shine in teams that share their values.

With Money

Earth Dogs are careful with cash. They save, they plan, and they don't take big risks. The Earth element gives them a long view—money is for security and the people they care about, not for showing off.

The Balance

Earth Dogs thrive when they let other people carry some weight. They don't have to be the one who holds it all together. Asking for help, saying no, and putting themselves first sometimes isn't weak—it's the only way to keep showing up for the long run.

Wait—Am I Actually an Earth Dog?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if you were born before February 16, 2018, you're actually a Fire Rooster from 2017, not an Earth Dog.

This matters! People born in January and early February often get their zodiac sign wrong. Always check the exact lunar dates to know for sure what is your Chinese zodiac sign.

2018: A Year That Shaped the World

Five moments that defined the Year of the Earth Dog.

February 16, 2018
Year of the Earth Dog Begins
The lunar new year ushered in the Earth Dog, with lantern festivals and family gatherings in cities around the world. The Dog is associated with loyalty and protection—fitting for a year marked by both global tension and collective action.
February 14, 2018
Parkland School Shooting
A gunman killed 17 people at Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School in Parkland, Florida. The tragedy sparked a wave of student-led activism, including the March for Our Lives rally in Washington, D.C., that drew nearly 800,000 protesters.
June 12, 2018
Trump-Kim Summit in Singapore
U.S. President Donald Trump and North Korean leader Kim Jong-un met in Singapore—the first ever meeting between sitting leaders of their two countries. The summit produced a vague denuclearization commitment but no detailed agreement.
July 15, 2018
France Wins the World Cup
France beat Croatia 4-2 in the 2018 FIFA World Cup Final in Moscow, claiming its second World Cup title. The tournament broke viewership records and saw underdog Croatia reach the final for the first time.
October 2, 2018
Khashoggi Killing
Saudi journalist Jamal Khashoggi was killed inside the Saudi consulate in Istanbul. The murder drew global condemnation, strained Saudi Arabia's relationships with Western governments, and put a renewed focus on press freedom around the world.
