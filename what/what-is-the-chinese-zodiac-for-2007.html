What is the Chinese zodiac for 2007?

The 2007 Chinese zodiac is the Fire Pig. If you were born in 2007, your zodiac sign combines the Pig's kindness with the warmth of the Fire element.

The 2007 Fire Pig: Kind & Optimistic

Curious about what is the Chinese zodiac for 2007? It's the Year of the Fire Pig! If you were born between February 18, 2007 and February 6, 2008, you're a Fire Pig. These folks have such a warm, loving vibe—they're like the sunshine in any room.

Pigs are the sweethearts of the zodiac—kind, giving, and always looking on the bright side. Add a little Fire to that mix, and you've got someone who's not just compassionate, but also radiates positivity and brings so much joy to everyone around them.

Love & Relationships for 2007 Fire Pigs

How do Fire Pigs born in 2007 approach love and relationships?

Fire Pigs are loving partners who value happiness. They're affectionate and supportive, always making their loved ones feel cherished. They believe in creating joyful, harmonious relationships.

Is 2007 the Year of the Pig for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 18, 2007, your zodiac sign is actually the Fire Dog (from the previous year). Only those born between February 18, 2007 and February 6, 2008 are Fire Pigs.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 2007 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
