What is the Chinese zodiac for 2007?

The 2007 Chinese zodiac is the Fire Pig. If you were born in 2007, your zodiac sign combines the Pig's kindness with the warmth of the Fire element.

The 2007 Fire Pig: Kind & Optimistic

Curious about what is the Chinese zodiac for 2007? It's the Year of the Fire Pig! If you were born between February 18, 2007 and February 6, 2008, you're a Fire Pig. These folks have such a warm, loving vibe—they're like the sunshine in any room.

Pigs are the sweethearts of the zodiac—kind, giving, and always looking on the bright side. Add a little Fire to that mix, and you've got someone who's not just compassionate, but also radiates positivity and brings so much joy to everyone around them.

The True Nature of Fire Pigs

What really makes 2007 Fire Pigs tick? It's Pig warmth lit up with Fire enthusiasm.

Fire Pigs are the people who make the room feel a little more like the room is worth being in. Regular Pigs are already generous—warm, kind, the kind of person who shows up with the good thing and stays for the long talk. Fire Pigs take all of that and add a layer underneath. They have the same soft heart, the same way of putting the people they love first, the same instinct to make the ordinary afternoon feel like the small celebration—but they also bring the spark, the energy, the kind of enthusiasm that turns the kind gesture into the moment the people around them remember. People born in 2007 have a kind of presence that makes the people around them feel a little more welcome.

What sets Fire Pigs apart is the way they mix generous with alive. They don't just give—they bring the whole thing. They're the friend who shows up with the food and the stories, the coworker who turns the rough meeting into the moment the team actually feels something, the partner who plans the small surprise and the big trip with the same kind of energy. They have a real gift for making the people they love feel like the life they're living is the life worth living, even when the life is a little messy in the middle.

The flip side? Fire Pigs can be a little too much. They can be a little too quick to say yes, a little too quick to spend the money, a little too quick to put the people's needs in front of their own. They can confuse being generous with being okay—and they can be a little too hard on themselves when the candle they lit for the people they love doesn't leave enough for their own. The real growth for a Fire Pig is learning that the people they love would rather see them take care of themselves, and that the best gift is the one that doesn't run them down.

Wait—Am I Actually a Fire Pig?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. The 2007 Fire Pig year starts on February 18, 2007. So if you were born in early January 2007, you're actually a Fire Dog from 2006, not a Fire Pig.

This matters! A lot of people born in early January get their zodiac sign wrong. Always double-check your exact birth date to know for sure what is your Chinese zodiac sign.
