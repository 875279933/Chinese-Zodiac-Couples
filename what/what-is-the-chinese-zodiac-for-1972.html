Born in early 1972? Check Before You Claim Water Rat

The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, so the 1972 year of the chinese zodiac actually starts on Feb 15, 1972. Anyone born before that date is a Metal Pig from 1971, not the Water Rat.

This is one of the most common mix-ups in the chinese zodiac, because the February lunar start is easy to confuse with the January-to-December calendar. Always double-check the lunar date before settling on a sign. The right year matters more than the right month when it comes to figuring out the year of the chinese zodiac you belong to.
