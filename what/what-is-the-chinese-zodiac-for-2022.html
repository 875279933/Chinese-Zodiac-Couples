What is the Chinese zodiac for 2022?

The 2022 Chinese zodiac is the Water Tiger. If you were born in 2022, your zodiac sign combines the Tiger's courage with the depth of the Water element.

The 2022 Water Tiger: Brave & Intuitive

So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 2022? It's the Year of the Water Tiger! If you were born between February 1, 2022 and January 21, 2023, you're a Water Tiger. This combination makes for a personality that's both bold and empathetic.

The Tiger is the third animal in the Chinese zodiac cycle, known for being brave, confident, and competitive. When you add Water to the mix, you get someone who's not just courageous—they're also intuitive, adaptable, and deeply emotional.

Is 2022 the Year of the Tiger for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 1, 2022, your zodiac sign is actually the Metal Ox (from the previous year). Only those born between February 1, 2022 and January 21, 2023 are Water Tigers.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 2022 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
