2022: A Year That Shaped the World

Five moments that defined the Year of the Water Tiger.

February 1, 2022
Year of the Water Tiger Begins
The lunar new year ushered in the Water Tiger, a sign associated with courage, change, and bold moves. Cities from Beijing to San Francisco welcomed the new year with fireworks, family gatherings, and a sense of optimism after two difficult pandemic years.
February 24, 2022
James Webb Telescope Unfolds
NASA's James Webb Space Telescope completed its final mirror deployment in deep space, a critical step before beginning science operations. The $10 billion observatory, the most powerful ever built, is designed to study the first galaxies that formed after the Big Bang and to image exoplanet atmospheres in unprecedented detail.
September 8, 2022
Queen Elizabeth II Dies
Queen Elizabeth II, the longest-reigning monarch in British history, died at Balmoral at age 96 after 70 years on the throne. Her death marked the end of an era and was followed by the accession of her son Charles III.
November 10, 2022
FTX Collapses
The cryptocurrency exchange FTX, once valued at $32 billion, collapsed into bankruptcy after a sudden run on customer withdrawals. The fall sent shockwaves through the crypto industry and led to fraud charges against founder Sam Bankman-Fried.
December 18, 2022
Argentina Wins the World Cup
Argentina, led by Lionel Messi in what was likely his final World Cup, beat France in a thrilling final that went to penalties. The match was widely regarded as one of the greatest World Cup finals ever played.
