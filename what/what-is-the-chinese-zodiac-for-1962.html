Is 1962 the Year of the Tiger for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. The 1962 Chinese zodiac year starts on January 25, 1962, so anyone born before that date is actually a Metal Ox (from the previous year). Only those born between January 25, 1962 and February 12, 1963 are Water Tigers.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early January 1962 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in February. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
