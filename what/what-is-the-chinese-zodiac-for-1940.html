What is the Chinese zodiac for 1940?

The 1940 Chinese zodiac is the Metal Dragon. If you were born in 1940, your zodiac sign combines the Dragon's power with the strength of the Metal element.

The 1940 Metal Dragon: Powerful & Ambitious

So, what is the Chinese zodiac for 1940? It's the Year of the Metal Dragon! If you were born between January 31, 1940 and February 18, 1941, you're a Metal Dragon. This combination makes for a personality that's both charismatic and determined.

The Dragon is the fifth animal in the Chinese zodiac cycle, known for being powerful, ambitious, and charismatic. When you add Metal to the mix, you get someone who's not just influential—they're also disciplined, strong-willed, and have incredible perseverance.

The True Nature of Metal Dragons

What really makes 1940 Metal Dragons tick? It's a blend of Dragon fire and Metal resolve.

The Metal Dragon is the most intense version of an already intense sign. Dragons are the only mythical creature in the zodiac, and they carry that larger-than-life energy—ambitious, charismatic, and impossible to ignore. Metal sharpens all of it. It gives Dragons a steel core, turning their big dreams into concrete plans and their charm into real authority.

Most Dragons are visionaries who chase the horizon. Metal Dragons actually get there. They've got the discipline to back up their ambition, the focus to see things through, and the confidence to brush off anyone who doubts them. Their yang energy burns hot, and Metal turns that heat into a forge.

The flip side? Metal Dragons can be blunt to the point of harsh. They expect a lot from themselves and even more from the people around them. They can steamroll others without realizing it and struggle to admit when they're wrong. The lesson for Metal Dragons? Soften the edges, listen as well as you lead, and remember that not everyone runs at your pace.

Is 1940 the Year of the Dragon for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. The 1940 Chinese zodiac year starts on January 31, 1940, so anyone born before that date is actually an Earth Rabbit (from the previous year). Only those born between January 31, 1940 and February 18, 1941 are Metal Dragons.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early January 1940 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in February. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
