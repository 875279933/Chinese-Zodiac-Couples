The Metal Dragon: Ambitious, Sharp, Driven

What is the Chinese zodiac for 1940? It's the Year of the Metal Dragon, the fifth stop on the chinese zodiac cycle. The Dragon is the most commanding sign in the 12-year round—visionary, confident, and built for leadership. Add Metal to that, and the Metal Dragon becomes a different kind of powerful: more disciplined, more exacting, and less interested in being liked than in being right.

Anyone with a birthday between February 8, 1940 and January 26, 1941 falls under this sign. They are the people who build a career, raise a family, and stay sharp about both well into their later decades.

Six Things That Define the Metal Dragon

The core traits that shape how 1940 Metal Dragons think, work, and connect with the people around them.

Born to Lead

Metal Dragons don't ask for the room—they take it. They have the vision, the conviction, and the stamina to lead a team through a long, hard project. People follow them because they've earned it, not because they demanded it.

Iron Will

The Metal element gives Dragons something they don't usually have—unbending follow-through. Once a Metal Dragon commits to a path, they stay the course. They are the people who keep training, keep saving, keep showing up when everyone else has quit.

Charismatic

Metal Dragons walk into a room and the air shifts. They have a presence, a self-possession, and a way of speaking that makes people want to listen. They don't need to be loud to be the center of attention.

Relentlessly Driven

Metal Dragons don't do half-measures. They set a high bar, then spend years getting over it. They are the people who turn a small business into an institution, a hobby into a craft, or a project into a category of one.

Sharp and Decisive

Metal Dragons think fast and decide faster. They are the person at the table who has the answer before the rest of the room has finished debating. The risk is that they sometimes decide before they've heard everyone out.

Fiercely Independent

Metal Dragons are not joiners. They will work with a team, but they need to be free to set their own direction. They don't want someone else's map. The world is more interesting to them when they get to choose the route.

Love, Work, Money & Balance

How the Metal Dragon personality shows up across the four areas of life that matter most.

In Relationships

Metal Dragons love with intensity and pride. They want a partner who respects their ambition and matches their loyalty—Roosters, Monkeys, and Rats tend to be their best matches. They are devoted once they commit, but they don't commit quickly, and they don't tolerate feeling small in a relationship.

At Work

Metal Dragons thrive in roles that require vision, judgment, and the discipline to follow through: executive leadership, law, medicine, engineering, finance, and entrepreneurship. They are the people who build institutions, not just careers. They lead by example and expect the same from everyone around them.

With Money

Metal Dragons are confident with money and willing to take calculated risks on big bets. The Metal element gives them patience and structure—they build wealth steadily rather than chasing quick wins. The watch-out is overcommitment to a single position that can hurt if the market turns.

The Balance

Metal Dragons can outwork and out-decide everyone in their orbit, including themselves. The balance improves when they let other people lead sometimes, slow down enough to hear the parts of life that aren't about winning, and accept that not every battle needs their sharpest edge.

Born in January 1940? Check Before You Claim Metal Dragon

The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, so the 1940 year of the chinese zodiac actually starts on February 8, 1940. Anyone born before that date is an Earth Rabbit from 1939, not a Metal Dragon. Each year of the chinese zodiac tracks lunar months, not the January-to-December calendar most of us use day to day.

This catches a lot of people, because most modern American adults think of Lunar New Year as a late-January or February event. The 1940 lunar year happens to start on the early side, in early February. Always double-check the lunar date before settling on a sign. The right year matters more than the right month when it comes to figuring out the year of the chinese zodiac you belong to.
