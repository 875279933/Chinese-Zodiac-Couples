What is the Chinese zodiac for 1994?

The 1994 Chinese zodiac is the Wood Dog. If you were born in 1994, your zodiac sign combines the Dog's loyalty with the warmth of the Wood element.

The 1994 Wood Dog: Loyal & Compassionate

Ever asked what is the Chinese zodiac for 1994? Let me fill you in—it's the Year of the Wood Dog! If your birthday falls between February 10, 1994 and January 30, 1995, you're one of these loyal souls. Wood Dogs are like that friend who'll always have your back no matter what—faithful, nurturing, and totally dedicated to the people they love. The Dog ranks eleventh in the zodiac cycle, known for loyalty, honesty, and protectiveness. Add Wood energy, and you get someone who's not just faithful—they're compassionate, warm, and always looking to grow and help others grow too. These are the friends who'll drive hours to be at your graduation or bring you soup when you're sick.

Love & Relationships for 1994 Wood Dogs

How do Wood Dogs born in 1994 approach love and relationships?

Wood Dogs are devoted partners who value trust above all. They're affectionate and supportive, always there for their loved ones. They believe in building strong, lasting relationships based on mutual respect.

Is 1994 the Year of the Dog for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 10, 1994, your zodiac sign is actually the Water Rooster (from the previous year). Only those born between February 10, 1994 and January 30, 1995 are Wood Dogs.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 1994 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
