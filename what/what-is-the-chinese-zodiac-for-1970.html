Is 1970 the Year of the Dog for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. The 1970 Chinese zodiac year starts on January 27, 1970, so anyone born before that date is actually an Earth Rooster (from the previous year). Only those born between January 27, 1970 and February 14, 1971 are Metal Dogs.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early January 1970 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in February. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
