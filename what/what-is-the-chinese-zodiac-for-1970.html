The core traits that shape how 1970 Metal Dogs think, work, and connect with the people around them.

01

Decisive Under Pressure

Metal Dogs don't freeze when things go sideways. They pick a direction, commit, and adjust on the way. Coworkers lean on them in tight situations because they radiate the kind of calm that comes from already having run the scenario in their head.

02

A Sharp, Quick Mind

The Metal element turns the Dog's natural cleverness into something quicker and harder. They read people fast, spot patterns sooner, and connect two ideas that don't look related to anyone else in the room.

03

Built for the Long Climb

Metal Dogs don't bounce from idea to idea. They set a target years out and work back from it. The ambition is quiet, but it is always on, and it tends to pay off in ways other people don't see coming.

04

Loyal When It Counts

Outside the boardroom, Metal Dogs are fiercely loyal. They keep a small circle and they keep it for decades. Once they call someone a friend, the label sticks, and they show up when it matters.

05

High Standards, Quietly Held

Metal Dogs are harder on themselves than on anyone else. They hold their work to a high bar and rarely explain it. The result is consistency: the people around them know what to expect, and that expectation is rarely missed.

06

Strategic Patience

Metal Dogs will wait years for the right move, as long as the move is right. They don't confuse patience with passivity—they are always watching, always running scenarios, and ready to act when the window opens.

03Love, Work, Money & Balance

How the Metal Dog personality shows up across the four areas of life that shape anyone born in 1970.

In Relationships

Metal Dogs love with intensity but keep the feelings private. They are at their best with partners who respect their need for space and don't push for the kind of constant reassurance that wears them out. Ox, Dragon, and Monkey partners tend to match their pace. They show love through doing things well, planning ahead, and showing up when it matters.

At Work

Metal Dogs thrive in roles that reward sharp thinking and long-term planning: finance, law, surgery, founding, engineering, sales leadership. They don't need the spotlight, but they end up running whatever team they are on. They are the people leadership trusts with the hardest problems because they don't flinch.

With Money

Metal Dogs are careful with money but not cheap. They save with a plan in mind, and they spend on what they care about. The watch-out is taking on too much risk chasing a return that looks too good—Metal Dogs have a hard time walking away from a high-stakes bet, even when they know the odds are bad.

The Balance

Metal Dogs risk running themselves into the ground before they ask for help. The improvement comes from learning to delegate and from scheduling downtime the way they schedule meetings. They don't need to be in every conversation to still be in charge of the outcome.

Born in early 1970? Check Before You Claim Metal Dog

The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, so the 1970 year of the chinese zodiac actually starts on Feb 6, 1970. Anyone born before that date is a Earth Rooster from 1969, not the Metal Dog.

This is one of the most common mix-ups in the chinese zodiac, because the lunar new year falls in late January in 1970, earlier than the more familiar February dates. Always double-check the lunar date before settling on a sign. The right year matters more than the right month when it comes to figuring out the year of the chinese zodiac you belong to.
