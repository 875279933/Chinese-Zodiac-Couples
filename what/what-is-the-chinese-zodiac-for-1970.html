What is the Chinese zodiac for 1970?

The 1970 Chinese zodiac is the Metal Dog. If you were born in 1970, your zodiac sign combines the Dog's loyalty with the strength of the Metal element.

The 1970 Metal Dog: Loyal & Protective

Ever found yourself wondering, "What is the Chinese zodiac for 1970?" Let me tell you—it's the Year of the Metal Dog! If your birthday falls between January 27, 1970 and February 14, 1971, you're one of these incredibly loyal souls. Metal Dogs are like that friend who'll always have your back no matter what—protective, honest to a fault, and deeply caring.

Dogs are naturally loyal and truthful. Add Metal into the equation, and you've got someone who's not just faithful—they're strong, completely trustworthy, and they'll always stand up for what they believe is right!

The True Nature of Metal Dogs

What really makes 1970 Metal Dogs tick? It's a blend of Dog loyalty and Metal resolve.

Metal Dogs are the people you'd want in your corner during a hard year. Regular Dogs are already loyal, honest, and protective—the kind of friend who calls you back, the kind of parent who shows up. Metal Dogs take all that and add something quieter: backbone. They don't just say the right thing, they follow through on it, even when it's hard, even when nobody's watching. People born in 1970 have a kind of steadiness that other people lean on, sometimes without knowing they're doing it.

What sets Metal Dogs apart is the way they hold conviction and care at the same time. They feel things deeply, but they don't broadcast it—they show it in the small choices, the way they listen, the way they keep showing up. They're the friend who notices when something's off and quietly fixes it. The coworker who sticks their neck out for the team. The partner who keeps their promises when it would be easier not to. They have a kind of integrity that other people can feel, even if they couldn't put words to it.

The flip side? Metal Dogs can be too hard on themselves. They hold themselves to a standard they'd never hold anyone else to, and they take it personally when they miss it. They can be too suspicious of people who are a little too friendly, too quick, too shiny. The real growth for a Metal Dog is learning that not everyone is out to get something, and that letting people in isn't the same as letting them hurt you.

How Metal Dog traits play out in relationships, careers, and finances.

Metal Dogs are the kind of people you want on your team—in love, at work, in life. They show up, they keep their word, and they don't really do anything halfway. The trick is learning to be a little easier on themselves, and to trust that the right people will stay without them having to earn it.

At Work

The Balance

Metal Dogs thrive when they learn to ease up on themselves. The standard they keep is high—but the people who love them don't need them to be perfect, just present.

Wait—Am I Actually a Metal Dog?

Here's a common point of confusion: the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian one. So if you were born before January 27, 1970, you're actually an Earth Rooster from 1969, not a Metal Dog.

This matters! A lot of people born in early January get their zodiac sign wrong. Always double-check your exact birth date to know for sure what is your Chinese zodiac sign.
