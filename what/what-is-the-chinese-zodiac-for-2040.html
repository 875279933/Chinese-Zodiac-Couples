What is the Chinese zodiac for 2040?

The 2040 Chinese zodiac is the Metal Monkey. If you were born in 2040, your zodiac sign combines the Monkey's cleverness with the strength of the Metal element.

The 2040 Metal Monkey: Intelligent & Sharp

Curious about what is the Chinese zodiac for 2040? It's the Year of the Metal Monkey! If your birthday lands between February 12, 2040 and January 31, 2041, you're a Metal Monkey through and through. This mix creates a personality that's both incredibly smart and tough as nails.

Is 2040 the Year of the Monkey for Everyone Born That Year?

Not exactly! The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, not the Gregorian calendar. So if you were born before February 12, 2040, your zodiac sign is actually the Earth Goat (from the previous year). Only those born between February 12, 2040 and January 31, 2041 are Metal Monkeys.

This is an important distinction—especially if you've ever wondered why someone born in early February 2040 might have a different zodiac sign than someone born in March. Always check the exact lunar dates to be sure!
