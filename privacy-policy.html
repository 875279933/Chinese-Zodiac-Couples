Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our Site and/or other sites on the Internet. These advertising cookies may include:

  • DoubleClick Cookie (IDE): Set by Google DoubleClick to register and report ad views, and to distinguish unique users. Expires after 13 months in most regions (up to 24 months in some jurisdictions).

For a complete list of advertising cookies set by Google, please refer to Google Cookie Usage.
