Chinese New Year 2021 landed on February 12, 2021, opening the Year of the Metal Ox on the traditional lunisolar calendar. In the 60-year cycle of Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches, this was the Xin (辛) year, the Xin stem paired with the Chou (丑) branch. The Ox holds a set place in the Chinese zodiac, and a Metal Ox year carries its own tone. Each zodiac year brings a different flavor to the Spring Festival, shaping the hopes families carry into the months ahead.

Spring Festival Customs in 2021

The 2021 festival kept the customs families returned to every year. Each one tied the household to a shared habit, from food to decoration to ceremony. These are six of the ones that mattered most in the Metal Ox year.

01

New Clothes

Red was the favorite, but any bright color would do. Children wore what made them happy; adults leaned toward darker, quieter tones. In 2021, a child in a new red coat walking to a relative's house was the picture of the holiday.

02

New Year Shopping

In the days before the festival, the market ran late. Stalls of red paper, dried fruit, fireworks, and cloth crowded the lanes. In 2021, shoppers carried bundles home through streets strung with lanterns.

03

Reunion Dinner

The night before the new year, families gathered around one table. Fish for surplus, dumplings for wealth, long noodles for long life, a chicken left whole to keep the family whole. In 2021, this was the meal people traveled all year to reach.

04

Red Envelopes

Married adults tucked crisp bills into red envelopes and pressed them into children's hands. The amount mattered less than the gesture. In 2021, a child might carry a stack of hongbao home and count them late into the night.

05

Visiting Relatives

The first days of the year were for rounds of visits. Married couples called on the wife's family on the second day. In 2021, the route was planned days ahead, and every stop came with tea, snacks, and good wishes.

06

Lantern Night

On the fifteenth night, lanterns went up. Children carried rabbits and fish made of paper and wire, candlelit from inside. In 2021, the festival closed the way it opened, with light.

Cultural Context of 2021

The 2021 festival opened the Metal Ox year against the backdrop of its time. Here is a look at the cultural and social life families carried into the festival.

Cultural Snapshot: 2016-2025
💰

Mobile Red Envelopes

Red envelopes went to the phone in earnest. Groups of relatives sent money back and forth in a running game through the holiday, and children learned to grab them with a thumb.

📱

Video Reunions

When a family member could not get home, a video call propped on the table brought them into the dinner. The connection dropped now and then, but the face on the screen still got a toast.

🚴

Food Delivery Feasts

In cities, families ordered part of the reunion dinner from restaurants and apps. The kitchen still did the centerpiece dishes, but the table filled out with delivery boxes.

🚆

Travel, Faster

High-speed rail cut the trip home from days to hours. In 2021, the station was still crowded, but the crowds moved through faster, and more people made it home for the eve.

A Story from 2021

The meaning of the 2021 festival often lives less in records than in small memories. Here is one from the Metal Ox year.

A postcard from 2021

Meet Xiao Mei, a letter carrier living in a busy district in Beijing. 2021 was the year Xiao Mei almost did not make it home. The train was late, the platform was crowded, and the journey that should have taken hours stretched into a full day. Xiao Mei dozed standing up, woke to snack sellers pushing through the car, and watched the winter fields pass in the dark. When the train finally pulled in, the streets were already lit with red lanterns and the air smelled like firecrackers. Xiao Mei reached the family door just as the reunion meal was being set out. There was no time to change, no time to rest. Xiao Mei sat down still wearing the coat from the road, and the first bite of the meal was the best Xiao Mei had tasted all year. The Metal Ox year began with a simple reminder: the hardest part of the holiday is getting there, and the rest takes care of itself.

2021 Festival - Quick Answers

The basics on the 2021 festival, in short.

When was Chinese New Year in 2021?

The festival fell on February 12, 2021. It marked the start of the Year of the Metal Ox on the traditional Chinese lunisolar calendar.

What animal is 2021 in the Chinese zodiac?

2021 is the Year of the Ox, specifically the Metal Ox. The Metal Ox year ran from February 12, 2021 to January 31, 2022 on the Gregorian calendar.

How was the festival celebrated in 2021?

In 2021, families marked the festival with reunion dinners, ancestor offerings, firecrackers, red envelopes for children, spring couplets on doorways, and lion and dragon dances. The customs followed centuries-old tradition, with regional differences across China.

What element is 2021?

2021 is a Metal year. In the five-element cycle, Metal years bring their own character to the Ox sign, shaping the tone of the year that began on February 12, 2021.
