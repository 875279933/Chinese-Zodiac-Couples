Lion and Dragon Dances

A lion dance troupe needed two strong dancers and a drummer. A dragon dance needed dozens, each holding a pole that held up the long body. In 2036, such performances drew the biggest crowds of the fair.

02

Visiting Relatives

The first days of the year were for rounds of visits. Married couples called on the wife's family on the second day. In 2036, the route was planned days ahead, and every stop came with tea, snacks, and good wishes.

03

Lantern Night

On the fifteenth night, lanterns went up. Children carried rabbits and fish made of paper and wire, candlelit from inside. In 2036, the festival closed the way it opened, with light.

04

Spring Couplets

Red paper strips went up on every door, black ink brushed in pairs, with a shorter line across the top. Door gods stood guard at the entrance. In 2036, a good calligrapher could fill half a street with orders in the days before the festival.

05

Temple Visits

On the first morning, families walked to the temple with incense. The early arrivals hoped to be first to the altar. In 2036, temple courtyards filled with new clothes and low murmurs before sunrise.

06

Ancestor Offerings

Before the meal, families set places for those who came before. Incense, a cup of wine, a bow. In 2036, the ancestor altar was the quiet center of the loudest week of the year.

Yan Ling, bicycle repairman, a coastal neighborhood in Guangzhou.

Meet Yan Ling, a bicycle repairman living in a coastal neighborhood in Guangzhou. 2036 was the year Yan Ling almost did not make it home. The train was late, the platform was crowded, and the journey that should have taken hours stretched into a full day. Yan Ling dozed standing up, woke to snack sellers pushing through the car, and watched the winter fields pass in the dark. When the train finally pulled in, the streets were already lit with red lanterns and the air smelled like firecrackers. Yan Ling reached the family door just as the reunion meal was being set out. There was no time to change, no time to rest. Yan Ling sat down still wearing the coat from the road, and the first bite of the meal was the best Yan Ling had tasted all year.
