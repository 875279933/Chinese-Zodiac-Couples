Spring Festival Customs in 2024

Chinese New Year customs in 2024 mixed timeless traditions with small, personal touches. Different households emphasized different things, but six practices stood out as central to how the festival was lived that winter. Here is what a typical family's season might have included.

🏯

Temple Visits and Prayers

Temple visits were not only about asking for things. They were also about thankfulness -- for a safe year past, for the health of family members, for enough to eat. Families lit three sticks of incense each, bowed before the main altar, and sometimes left small offerings of fruit or oil. In 2024, the larger temples held special ceremonies throughout the first week.

🙏

Ancestor Worship

Some families visited graves before the holiday, cleaning the stones and leaving offerings. Others held the ceremony at home, around a table with photographs and incense. Either way, the intention was the same: to say thank you. In 2024, the quiet act of remembrance felt more important than any public celebration.

👩

Visiting Relatives and Friends

A holiday visit was not just a chat. It included tea, snacks, the exchange of auspicious phrases, and often a meal. Hosts took pride in having something to offer every guest, even unexpected ones. In 2024, households kept boxes of candies and tins of tea ready at all times during the first week of the year.

🎁

Spring Couplets and Door Gods

Families pasted red paper couplets with auspicious phrases on their doorways, along with images of door gods to protect the household. Writing or buying new couplets before the new year was an essential preparation, and the best calligraphers in the village were always in high demand in 2024.

🍭

New Year Sweets and Snacks

Making the holiday snacks was a task shared by the women of the household. Rolling sesame cookies, pressing rice cakes, stirring the sugar syrup that coated candied fruit -- these were the smells and sounds of preparation. In 2024, a jar of the family's favorite sweets sat on every kitchen table.

🎓

Reunion Dinner

The reunion dinner was where the year's stories were told. Grandparents recalled festivals from their youth, parents quietly counted the blessings of the past twelve months, and children asked for seconds of everything. In 2024, this meal was the emotional center of the holiday.
