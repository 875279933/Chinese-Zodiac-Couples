Spring Festival Customs in 1991

The 1991 festival kept the customs families returned to every year. Each one tied the household to a shared habit, from food to decoration to ceremony. These are six of the ones that mattered most in the Metal Goat year.

🛍

New Year Shopping

In the days before the festival, the market ran late. Stalls of red paper, dried fruit, fireworks, and cloth crowded the lanes. In 1991, shoppers carried bundles home through streets strung with lanterns.

🍲

Reunion Dinner

The night before the new year, families gathered around one table. Fish for surplus, dumplings for wealth, long noodles for long life, a chicken left whole to keep the family whole. In 1991, this was the meal people traveled all year to reach.

🎁

Red Envelopes

Married adults tucked crisp bills into red envelopes and pressed them into children's hands. The amount mattered less than the gesture. In 1991, a child might carry a stack of hongbao home and count them late into the night.

👗

New Clothes

Red was the favorite, but any bright color would do. Children wore what made them happy; adults leaned toward darker, quieter tones. In 1991, a child in a new red coat walking to a relative's house was the picture of the holiday.

🎈

Lantern Night

On the fifteenth night, lanterns went up. Children carried rabbits and fish made of paper and wire, candlelit from inside. In 1991, the festival closed the way it opened, with light.

🎁

Spring Couplets

Red paper strips went up on every door, black ink brushed in pairs, with a shorter line across the top. Door gods stood guard at the entrance. In 1991, a good calligrapher could fill half a street with orders in the days before the festival.

Cultural Context of 1991

The 1991 festival opened the Metal Goat year against the backdrop of its time. Here is a look at the cultural and social life families carried into the festival.

Cultural Snapshot: 1986-1995

Fuller Tables

The reunion dinner in 1991 carried dishes that had been absent for years. Whole fish, a range of braised meats, and store-bought sweets found their way back, and the table could barely hold them all.

New Clothes, New Styles

Ready-made clothes in bright colors replaced the tailored and mended outfits of earlier years. Children in 1991 picked their festival wear from racks, not from a tailor's order.

Fireworks at Midnight

Firecrackers came back in force, and the midnight sky over a city in 1991 was bright and loud for a solid hour. The smoke was thick, but no one minded.

The Gala as Ritual

The television gala settled into a fixed part of the evening. Families timed the dumplings to the midnight countdown, and the two became inseparable.

A Story from 1991

The meaning of the 1991 festival often lives less in records than in small memories. Here is one from the Metal Goat year.

Qiang Li, tailor, a hillside hamlet in Yunnan.

Meet Qiang Li, a tailor living in a hillside hamlet in Yunnan. 1991 was the year Qiang Li almost did not make it home. The train was late, the platform was crowded, and the journey that should have taken hours stretched into a full day. Qiang Li dozed standing up, woke to snack sellers pushing through the car, and watched the winter fields pass in the dark. When the train finally pulled in, the streets were already lit with red lanterns and the air smelled like firecrackers. Qiang Li reached the family door just as the reunion meal was being set out. There was no time to change, no time to rest. Qiang Li sat down still wearing the coat from the road, and the first bite of the meal was the best Qiang Li had tasted all year. The Metal Goat year began with a simple reminder: the hardest part of the holiday is getting there, and the rest takes care of itself.

1991 Festival - Quick Answers

The basics on the 1991 festival, in short.

When was Chinese New Year in 1991?

The festival fell on February 15, 1991. It marked the start of the Year of the Metal Goat on the traditional Chinese lunisolar calendar.

What animal is 1991 in the Chinese zodiac?

1991 is the Year of the Goat, specifically the Metal Goat. The Metal Goat year ran from February 15, 1991 to February 3, 1992 on the Gregorian calendar.

How was the festival celebrated in 1991?

In 1991, families marked the festival with reunion dinners, ancestor offerings, firecrackers, red envelopes for children, spring couplets on doorways, and lion and dragon dances. The customs followed centuries-old tradition, with regional differences across China.

What element is 1991?

1991 is a Metal year. In the five-element cycle, Metal years bring their own character to the Goat sign, shaping the tone of the year that began on February 15, 1991.
