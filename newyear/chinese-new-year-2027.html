Qiang Li, tailor, a hillside hamlet in Yunnan.

Meet Qiang Li, a tailor living in a hillside hamlet in Yunnan. 2027 was the year Qiang Li almost did not make it home. The train was late, the platform was crowded, and the journey that should have taken hours stretched into a full day. Qiang Li dozed standing up, woke to snack sellers pushing through the car, and watched the winter fields pass in the dark. When the train finally pulled in, the streets were already lit with red lanterns and the air smelled like firecrackers. Qiang Li reached the family door just as the reunion meal was being set out. There was no time to change, no time to rest. Qiang Li sat down still wearing the coat from the road, and the first bite of the meal was the best Qiang Li had tasted all year.
