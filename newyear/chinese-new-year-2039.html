Spring Couplets

02

Lion and Dragon Dances

A lion dance troupe needed two strong dancers and a drummer. A dragon dance needed dozens, each holding a pole that held up the long body. In 2039, such performances drew the biggest crowds of the fair.

03

Visiting Relatives

The first days of the year were for rounds of visits. Married couples called on the wife's family on the second day. In 2039, the route was planned days ahead, and every stop came with tea, snacks, and good wishes.

04

Lantern Night

On the fifteenth night, lanterns went up. Children carried rabbits and fish made of paper and wire, candlelit from inside. In 2039, the festival closed the way it opened, with light.

05

Firecrackers

At midnight the sky lit up. Long strings of firecrackers unrolled from rooftops, and children clapped hands over their ears. The noise chased the old year off. In 2039, the smoke hung low over the rooftops and mixed with the smell of cooking oil.

06

Temple Visits

On the first morning, families walked to the temple with incense. The early arrivals hoped to be first to the altar. In 2039, temple courtyards filled with new clothes and low murmurs before sunrise.

Qiang Li, tailor, a hillside hamlet in Yunnan.

Meet Qiang Li, a tailor living in a hillside hamlet in Yunnan. 2039 was the year Qiang Li almost did not make it home. The train was late, the platform was crowded, and the journey that should have taken hours stretched into a full day. Qiang Li dozed standing up, woke to snack sellers pushing through the car, and watched the winter fields pass in the dark. When the train finally pulled in, the streets were already lit with red lanterns and the air smelled like firecrackers. Qiang Li reached the family door just as the reunion meal was being set out. There was no time to change, no time to rest. Qiang Li sat down still wearing the coat from the road, and the first bite of the meal was the best Qiang Li had tasted all year. The Earth Goat year began with a simple reminder: the hardest part of the holiday is getting there, and the rest takes care of itself.
