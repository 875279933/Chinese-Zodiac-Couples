Spring Festival Customs in 2022

The 2022 festival kept the customs families returned to every year. Each one tied the household to a shared habit, from food to decoration to ceremony. These are six of the ones that mattered most in the Water Tiger year.

01

Spring Couplets

Red paper strips went up on every door, black ink brushed in pairs, with a shorter line across the top. Door gods stood guard at the entrance. In 2022, a good calligrapher could fill half a street with orders in the days before the festival.

02

Lion and Dragon Dances

A lion dance troupe needed two strong dancers and a drummer. A dragon dance needed dozens, each holding a pole that held up the long body. In 2022, such performances drew the biggest crowds of the fair.

03

Visiting Relatives

The first days of the year were for rounds of visits. Married couples called on the wife's family on the second day. In 2022, the route was planned days ahead, and every stop came with tea, snacks, and good wishes.

04

Lantern Night

On the fifteenth night, lanterns went up. Children carried rabbits and fish made of paper and wire, candlelit from inside. In 2022, the festival closed the way it opened, with light.

05

Firecrackers

At midnight the sky lit up. Long strings of firecrackers unrolled from rooftops, and children clapped hands over their ears. The noise chased the old year off. In 2022, the smoke hung low over the rooftops and mixed with the smell of cooking oil.

06

Temple Visits

On the first morning, families walked to the temple with incense. The early arrivals hoped to be first to the altar. In 2022, temple courtyards filled with new clothes and low murmurs before sunrise.

A Story from 2022

The meaning of the 2022 festival often lives less in records than in small memories. Here is one from the Water Tiger year.

A postcard from 2022

Meet Rong Hua, a potter living in a farming village in Sichuan. 2022 was supposed to be an ordinary Chinese New Year, but it became one Rong Hua would not forget. It started with a small disaster: the kitchen stove broke on New Year's Eve morning, right when the reunion meal was meant to be cooked. The house went quiet. Then neighbors stepped in. One lent a portable stove, another sent a pot of steaming rice, and a shopkeeper kept her door open an extra hour so the family could buy a replacement. By late afternoon the kitchen worked again, and by evening the table was full. The meal that year tasted better, maybe because it almost did not happen. Rong Hua took a simple lesson from the Water Tiger year: good fortune often looks like neighbors looking out for each other. After midnight, with firecrackers rolling down the street, Rong Hua wrote a line in a notebook: remember the stove.

When was Chinese New Year in 2022?

What animal is 2022 in the Chinese zodiac?

In 2022, families marked the festival with reunion dinners, ancestor offerings, firecrackers, red envelopes for children, spring couplets on doorways, and lion and dragon dances. The customs followed centuries-old tradition, with regional differences across China.
