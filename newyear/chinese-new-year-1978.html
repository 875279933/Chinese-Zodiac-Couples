Festival Positioning

1978 marked the move into the Year of the Earth Horse, the 7th position in the 12-year zodiac cycle.

Chinese New Year 1978 landed on February 7, 1978, opening the Year of the Earth Horse on the traditional lunisolar calendar. In the 60-year cycle of Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches, this was the Wu (戊) year, the Wu stem paired with the Wu (午) branch.

The Horse holds a set place in the Chinese zodiac, and a Earth Horse year carries its own tone. Each zodiac year brings a different flavor to the Spring Festival, shaping the hopes families carry into the months ahead.

Spring Festival Customs in 1978

The 1978 festival kept the customs families returned to every year. Each one tied the household to a shared habit, from food to decoration to ceremony. These are six of the ones that mattered most in the Earth Horse year.

01

Spring Couplets

Red paper strips went up on every door, black ink brushed in pairs, with a shorter line across the top. Door gods stood guard at the entrance. In 1978, a good calligrapher could fill half a street with orders in the days before the festival.

02

Lion and Dragon Dances

A lion dance troupe needed two strong dancers and a drummer. A dragon dance needed dozens, each holding a pole that held up the long body. In 1978, such performances drew the biggest crowds of the fair.

03

Visiting Relatives

The first days of the year were for rounds of visits. Married couples called on the wife's family on the second day. In 1978, the route was planned days ahead, and every stop came with tea, snacks, and good wishes.

04

Lantern Night

On the fifteenth night, lanterns went up. Children carried rabbits and fish made of paper and wire, candlelit from inside. In 1978, the festival closed the way it opened, with light.

05

Firecrackers

At midnight the sky lit up. Long strings of firecrackers unrolled from rooftops, and children clapped hands over their ears. The noise chased the old year off. In 1978, the smoke hung low over the rooftops and mixed with the smell of cooking oil.

06

Temple Visits

On the first morning, families walked to the temple with incense. The early arrivals hoped to be first to the altar. In 1978, temple courtyards filled with new clothes and low murmurs before sunrise.

Core Meaning

The 1978 festival brought the Earth Horse year, with its own blend of meaning. Each year in the Chinese lunisolar calendar carries its own character, and the Earth Horse year that began on February 7, 1978 set the tone for the months ahead. For families, it carried a set of steady wishes: health, a full table, safety, and a home in good order. Read more on the Year of the Horse.

The Horse Character

The Horse loves movement and company. Energetic, open, and quick to travel. A Horse year pulls people out of the house and into the street.

🌿

Symbols on the Table

Abundance in Chinese tradition shows up through symbols. A fish on the table stands for having more than enough. Dumplings stand for wealth. Tangerines stand for good fortune. In 1978, these small signs were everywhere.

🏔

The Earth Element

Earth years steady. They suit saving, building, and laying foundations that will hold for years.

👪

Home and Heritage

At its center, Chinese New Year is about coming together. The festival gives families a chance to honor the people who came before, mend the ties that frayed, and reset the household before the year turns.

Cultural Snapshot: 1977-1985
📺

The Television Gala

From 1983 on, the Spring Festival Gala on television gave families a new reason to stay up past midnight. The set ran for hours, and even those who talked through it kept it on, glancing up for the skits and songs.

🛍

Returning Markets

Markets filled again with fireworks, dried fruit, and bolts of red cloth. Shoppers who had made do for years suddenly had choices, and the lanes in 1978 were loud with bargaining.

🪒

New Appliances

A family that had saved for a television or a sewing machine often brought it home just before the festival, so that it could be shown off to visiting relatives. The appliance became part of the celebration.

🚇

Travel Home

As work sent people farther afield, the trip home for the new year grew longer and more crowded. The train station in 1978 was a sea of bundles and sleeping children, all heading the same direction.

A Story from 1978

The meaning of the 1978 festival often lives less in records than in small memories. Here is one from the Earth Horse year.

Meet Fen Fang, a teacher living in a port alley in Quanzhou. 1978 was the year Fen Fang finally understood what Chinese New Year was for. Until then, Fen Fang had cared mostly about new clothes, red envelopes, and a few days off. But that winter, the elder relative who usually hosted the reunion dinner fell ill. Without a host, the family briefly wondered whether the dinner would happen at all. Then, quietly, people stepped up. An aunt offered her kitchen. An uncle took over the shopping. A cousin, normally shy, found an old calligraphy set and wrote the spring couplets. By New Year's Eve the family was gathered around a different table in a different room, but everything important was the same. The dishes carried their old meanings, the altar was set with care, and the room stayed warm. For Fen Fang, the Earth Horse year was a lesson: tradition lives in people, not in places.

1978 Festival - Quick Answers

The basics on the 1978 festival, in short.

When was Chinese New Year in 1978?

The festival fell on February 7, 1978. It marked the start of the Year of the Earth Horse on the traditional Chinese lunisolar calendar.

What animal is 1978 in the Chinese zodiac?

1978 is the Year of the Horse, specifically the Earth Horse. The Earth Horse year ran from February 7, 1978 to January 27, 1979 on the Gregorian calendar.

How was the festival celebrated in 1978?

In 1978, families marked the festival with reunion dinners, ancestor offerings, firecrackers, red envelopes for children, spring couplets on doorways, and lion and dragon dances. The customs followed centuries-old tradition, with regional differences across China.

What element is 1978?

1978 is a Earth year. In the five-element cycle, Earth years bring their own character to the Horse sign, shaping the tone of the year that began on February 7, 1978.
