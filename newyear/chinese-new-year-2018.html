Festival Positioning

2018 marked the move into the Year of the Earth Dog, the 11th position in the 12-year zodiac cycle.

Chinese New Year 2018 landed on February 16, 2018, opening the Year of the Earth Dog on the traditional lunisolar calendar. In the 60-year cycle of Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches, this was the Wu (戊) year, the Wu stem paired with the Xu (戌) branch.

The Dog holds a set place in the Chinese zodiac, and a Earth Dog year carries its own tone. Each zodiac year brings a different flavor to the Spring Festival, shaping the hopes families carry into the months ahead.

Spring Festival Customs in 2018

The 2018 festival kept the customs families returned to every year. Each one tied the household to a shared habit, from food to decoration to ceremony. These are six of the ones that mattered most in the Earth Dog year.

🛍

New Year Shopping

In the days before the festival, the market ran late. Stalls of red paper, dried fruit, fireworks, and cloth crowded the lanes. In 2018, shoppers carried bundles home through streets strung with lanterns.

🍲

Reunion Dinner

The night before the new year, families gathered around one table. Fish for surplus, dumplings for wealth, long noodles for long life, a chicken left whole to keep the family whole. In 2018, this was the meal people traveled all year to reach.

🎁

Red Envelopes

Married adults tucked crisp bills into red envelopes and pressed them into children's hands. The amount mattered less than the gesture. In 2018, a child might carry a stack of hongbao home and count them late into the night.

👗

New Clothes

Red was the favorite, but any bright color would do. Children wore what made them happy; adults leaned toward darker, quieter tones. In 2018, a child in a new red coat walking to a relative's house was the picture of the holiday.

🎈

Lantern Night

On the fifteenth night, lanterns went up. Children carried rabbits and fish made of paper and wire, candlelit from inside. In 2018, the festival closed the way it opened, with light.

🎁

Spring Couplets

Red paper strips went up on every door, black ink brushed in pairs, with a shorter line across the top. Door gods stood guard at the entrance. In 2018, a good calligrapher could fill half a street with orders in the days before the festival.

Core Meaning

The 2018 festival brought the Earth Dog year, with its own blend of meaning. Each year in the Chinese lunisolar calendar carries its own character, and the Earth Dog year that began on February 16, 2018 set the tone for the months ahead. For families, it carried a set of steady wishes: health, a full table, safety, and a home in good order. Read more on the Year of the Dog.

🐕

The Dog Character

The Dog stands for loyalty and fair play. Watchful, honest, and steady with friends. A Dog year asks people to look after the people who look after them.

🌿

Symbols on the Table

Abundance in Chinese tradition shows up through symbols. A fish on the table stands for having more than enough. Dumplings stand for wealth. Tangerines stand for good fortune. In 2018, these small signs were everywhere.

🏔

The Earth Element

Earth years steady. They suit saving, building, and laying foundations that will hold for years.

👪

Home and Heritage

At its center, Chinese New Year is about coming together. The festival gives families a chance to honor the people who came before, mend the ties that frayed, and reset the household before the year turns.

Cultural Context of 2018

The 2018 festival opened the Earth Dog year against the backdrop of its time. Here is a look at the cultural and social life families carried into the festival.

Cultural Snapshot: 2016-2025
💰

Mobile Red Envelopes

Red envelopes went to the phone in earnest. Groups of relatives sent money back and forth in a running game through the holiday, and children learned to grab them with a thumb.

📱

Video Reunions

When a family member could not get home, a video call propped on the table brought them into the dinner. The connection dropped now and then, but the face on the screen still got a toast.

🚴

Food Delivery Feasts

In cities, families ordered part of the reunion dinner from restaurants and apps. The kitchen still did the centerpiece dishes, but the table filled out with delivery boxes.

🚆

Travel, Faster

High-speed rail cut the trip home from days to hours. In 2018, the station was still crowded, but the crowds moved through faster, and more people made it home for the eve.

A Story from 2018

The meaning of the 2018 festival often lives less in records than in small memories. Here is one from the Earth Dog year.

Meet Jian Guo, a farmer living in a mining settlement in Shanxi. 2018 was the year Jian Guo almost did not make it home. The train was late, the platform was crowded, and the journey that should have taken hours stretched into a full day. Jian Guo dozed standing up, woke to snack sellers pushing through the car, and watched the winter fields pass in the dark. When the train finally pulled in, the streets were already lit with red lanterns and the air smelled like firecrackers. Jian Guo reached the family door just as the reunion meal was being set out. There was no time to change, no time to rest. Jian Guo sat down still wearing the coat from the road, and the first bite of the meal was the best Jian Guo had tasted all year. The Earth Dog year began with a simple reminder: the hardest part of the holiday is getting there, and the rest takes care of itself.

2018 Festival - Quick Answers

The basics on the 2018 festival, in short.

When was Chinese New Year in 2018?

The festival fell on February 16, 2018. It marked the start of the Year of the Earth Dog on the traditional Chinese lunisolar calendar.

What animal is 2018 in the Chinese zodiac?

2018 is the Year of the Dog, specifically the Earth Dog. The Earth Dog year ran from February 16, 2018 to February 4, 2019 on the Gregorian calendar.

How was the festival celebrated in 2018?

In 2018, families marked the festival with reunion dinners, ancestor offerings, firecrackers, red envelopes for children, spring couplets on doorways, and lion and dragon dances. The customs followed centuries-old tradition, with regional differences across China.

What element is 2018?

2018 is a Earth year. In the five-element cycle, Earth years bring their own character to the Dog sign, shaping the tone of the year that began on February 16, 2018.
