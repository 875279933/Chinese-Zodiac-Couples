Festival Positioning

1970 marked the move into the Year of the Metal Dog, the 11th position in the 12-year zodiac cycle.

Chinese New Year 1970 landed on February 6, 1970, opening the Year of the Metal Dog on the traditional lunisolar calendar. In the 60-year cycle of Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches, this was the Geng (庚) year, the Geng stem paired with the Xu (戌) branch.

The Dog holds a set place in the Chinese zodiac, and a Metal Dog year carries its own tone. Each zodiac year brings a different flavor to the Spring Festival, shaping the hopes families carry into the months ahead.

Spring Festival Customs in 1970

The 1970 festival kept the customs families returned to every year. Each one tied the household to a shared habit, from food to decoration to ceremony. These are six of the ones that mattered most in the Metal Dog year.

  • 🪐

    Lion and Dragon Dances

    A lion dance troupe needed two strong dancers and a drummer. A dragon dance needed dozens, each holding a pole that held up the long body. In 1970, such performances drew the biggest crowds of the fair.

  • 👪

    Visiting Relatives

    The first days of the year were for rounds of visits. Married couples called on the wife's family on the second day. In 1970, the route was planned days ahead, and every stop came with tea, snacks, and good wishes.

  • 🎈

    Lantern Night

    On the fifteenth night, lanterns went up. Children carried rabbits and fish made of paper and wire, candlelit from inside. In 1970, the festival closed the way it opened, with light.

  • 🎁

    Spring Couplets

    Red paper strips went up on every door, black ink brushed in pairs, with a shorter line across the top. Door gods stood guard at the entrance. In 1970, a good calligrapher could fill half a street with orders in the days before the festival.

  • 🏯

    Temple Visits

    On the first morning, families walked to the temple with incense. The early arrivals hoped to be first to the altar. In 1970, temple courtyards filled with new clothes and low murmurs before sunrise.

  • 🔥

    Ancestor Offerings

    Before the meal, families set places for those who came before. Incense, a cup of wine, a bow. In 1970, the ancestor altar was the quiet center of the loudest week of the year.

The 1970 festival brought the Metal Dog year, with its own blend of meaning. Each year in the Chinese lunisolar calendar carries its own character, and the Metal Dog year that began on February 6, 1970 set the tone for the months ahead. For families, it carried a set of steady wishes: health, a full table, safety, and a home in good order. Read more on the Year of the Dog.

The Dog Character

The Dog stands for loyalty and fair play. Watchful, honest, and steady with friends. A Dog year asks people to look after the people who look after them.

🌿

Symbols on the Table

Abundance in Chinese tradition shows up through symbols. A fish on the table stands for having more than enough. Dumplings stand for wealth. Tangerines stand for good fortune. In 1970, these small signs were everywhere.

⚔

The Metal Element

Metal years sharpen. They cut away what is wasted and leave what is solid and useful.

👪

Home and Heritage

At its center, Chinese New Year is about coming together. The festival gives families a chance to honor the people who came before, mend the ties that frayed, and reset the household before the year turns.

Cultural Context of 1970

The 1970 festival opened the Metal Dog year against the backdrop of its time. Here is a look at the cultural and social life families carried into the festival.

Cultural Snapshot: 1966-1976
✂

Paper Cuttings

Families kept the decorations going with scissors and red paper. Window flowers of the zodiac animal, the character for fortune turned upside down, and simple lucky phrases went up on every pane.

🏯

Quiet Temple Visits

Temple visits thinned out, but a few families still came at dawn to light a stick of incense. They stayed only a few minutes, yet those few minutes kept an old habit alive for the next generation.

🍲

Homemade Dumplings

The reunion meal leaned on what a household could make itself. Dumplings, rolled and folded around the table, became the center of the dinner, and the folding was the part children remembered.

✉

Letters Home

For those sent far away, a letter written before the eve was the closest thing to being there. Families read them aloud at the table and saved them in a box for the next year.

A Story from 1970

The meaning of the 1970 festival often lives less in records than in small memories. Here is one from the Metal Dog year.

Meet Jian Guo, a farmer living in a mining settlement in Shanxi. 1970 was the year Jian Guo almost did not make it home. The train was late, the platform was crowded, and the journey that should have taken hours stretched into a full day. Jian Guo dozed standing up, woke to snack sellers pushing through the car, and watched the winter fields pass in the dark. When the train finally pulled in, the streets were already lit with red lanterns and the air smelled like firecrackers. Jian Guo reached the family door just as the reunion meal was being set out. There was no time to change, no time to rest. Jian Guo sat down still wearing the coat from the road, and the first bite of the meal was the best Jian Guo had tasted all year. The Metal Dog year began with a simple reminder: the hardest part of the holiday is getting there, and the rest takes care of itself.

1970 Festival - Quick Answers

The basics on the 1970 festival, in short.

When was Chinese New Year in 1970?

The festival fell on February 6, 1970. It marked the start of the Year of the Metal Dog on the traditional Chinese lunisolar calendar.

What animal is 1970 in the Chinese zodiac?

1970 is the Year of the Dog, specifically the Metal Dog. The Metal Dog year ran from February 6, 1970 to January 26, 1971 on the Gregorian calendar.

How was the festival celebrated in 1970?

In 1970, families marked the festival with reunion dinners, ancestor offerings, firecrackers, red envelopes for children, spring couplets on doorways, and lion and dragon dances. The customs followed centuries-old tradition, with regional differences across China.

What element is 1970?

1970 is a Metal year. In the five-element cycle, Metal years bring their own character to the Dog sign, shaping the tone of the year that began on February 6, 1970.
