2. Understanding Chinese Zodiac Compatibility

Chinese zodiac compatibility draws from ancient principles of Earthly Branches and Five Elements. For the Dog, key relationship types define compatibility:

  • Six Harmony (六合): The Dog and Rabbit form the most perfect union, representing ultimate harmony.
  • Three Harmony (三合): The Dog, Tiger, and Horse create a powerful Fire alliance.
  • Clash (冲): The Dog and Dragon represent direct opposition, creating conflicts.
  • Harm (害): The Dog and Rooster face subtle harm that undermines trust.
