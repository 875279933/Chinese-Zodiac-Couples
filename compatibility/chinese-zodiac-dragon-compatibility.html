2. Understanding Chinese Zodiac Compatibility

Chinese zodiac compatibility draws from ancient principles of Earthly Branches and Five Elements. For the Dragon, key relationship types define compatibility:

  • Six Harmony (六合): The Dragon and Rooster form the most perfect union, representing ultimate harmony and mutual support.
  • Three Harmony (三合): The Dragon, Monkey, and Rat create a powerful Water alliance that brings success.
  • Clash (冲): The Dragon and Dog represent direct opposition, creating intense conflicts.
  • Harm (害): The Dragon and Rabbit face subtle harm that erodes trust over time.
