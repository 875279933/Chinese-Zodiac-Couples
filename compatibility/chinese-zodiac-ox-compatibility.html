About the Ox Zodiac Sign

People born in the Year of the Ox (牛) are celebrated for their unwavering determination, reliability, and strong work ethic. They approach life with steady patience and build success through consistent effort rather than shortcuts. In relationships, Oxen are devoted partners who value tradition, loyalty, and long-term commitment. Their grounded nature provides a solid foundation for lasting love.

2. Understanding Chinese Zodiac Compatibility

Chinese zodiac compatibility draws from ancient wisdom about how animal signs interact through various cosmic relationships. For the Ox, four key relationship types define compatibility:

  • Six Harmony (六合): The Ox and Rat form the most perfect union, representing ultimate harmony and mutual support.
  • Three Harmony (三合): The Ox, Snake, and Rooster create a powerful alliance that brings stability and prosperity.
  • Clash (冲): The Ox and Goat represent direct opposition, creating natural conflicts.
  • Harm (害): The Ox and Horse create subtle harm, leading to mutual hindrance.
