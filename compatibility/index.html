Chinese Zodiac Compatibility

A full 12-sign chinese zodiac compatibility chart, the five-element rules that decide each match, and a year-by-year guide from 1936 through 2042. Pick your sign, see your best and worst match, and read why the pairing works.

Chinese zodiac compatibility is a matching system built on three layers: the 12-year animal cycle, the 10-year element cycle, and a set of branch relationships called San He, Liu He, Liu Chong, and Liu Hai. The animal answers who you match with. The element answers how the match feels. The branch rules answer why some pairs click and others push apart.

Each birth year carries one animal and one element. The pair of the two is what the rest of this page calls a compatibility profile. Two people with the same animal but different elements can have very different matches. Two people with different animals but the same element can still feel at home. The chart below puts all 144 pairs on one page so the pattern is easy to read.

The system comes from the same place as the lunar calendar and the 60-year Stem-Branch cycle. It is not a rule. It is a pattern that Chinese families have used for centuries to read friendships, marriages, and business ties. Use it as a map, not a verdict.

Why Chinese Zodiac Compatibility Works: The Five Elements and Branch Rules

The animal decides the shape of the match. The element decides the texture. Both come from one 60-year cycle that has been tracked in China for over two thousand years.

Best · Triangle

Three-Harmony (San He)

Three animals 4 years apart form a triangle. Any two animals in the same triangle carry the strongest compatibility. The four triangles are:

  • WaterRat · Dragon · Monkey
  • MetalOx · Snake · Rooster
  • FireTiger · Horse · Dog
  • WoodRabbit · Goat · Pig
Best · One-on-One

Six-Harmony (Liu He)

Two animals 6 years apart form a hidden pair. These six couples are the closest one-on-one matches in the system. The hidden stem adds depth, not just surface chemistry.

  • Sub-bornRat + Ox
  • Hidden unionTiger + Pig
  • Loyal pairRabbit + Dog
  • Radiant pairDragon + Rooster
  • Quiet bondSnake + Monkey
  • Warm pairHorse + Goat
Avoid · Direct Conflict

Six-Conflict (Liu Chong)

Two animals that sit 6 apart in the 12-cycle are wired to push against each other. The match score for these pairs is the lowest of the six common relationships. They are not impossible, but they need more work.

  • ClashRat ↔ Horse
  • ClashOx ↔ Goat
  • ClashTiger ↔ Monkey
  • ClashRabbit ↔ Rooster
  • ClashDragon ↔ Dog
  • ClashSnake ↔ Pig
Avoid · Hidden Harm

Six-Harm (Liu Hai)

The six-harm pairs do not fight like the conflicts. They drain each other slowly, often without knowing why. The match score for these pairs is just above conflict. A friendship survives; a marriage often does not.

  • HarmRat ↔ Goat
  • HarmOx ↔ Horse
  • HarmTiger ↔ Dog
  • HarmRabbit ↔ Dragon
  • HarmSnake ↔ Pig
  • HarmMonkey ↔ Rooster

The Five Elements and Chinese Zodiac Compatibility

Each birth year also carries one of the five elements: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water. The element shifts with the last digit of the year and decides the feel of every pair.

WoodYears ending 4 · 5
FireYears ending 6 · 7
EarthYears ending 8 · 9
MetalYears ending 0 · 1
WaterYears ending 2 · 3

The elements move in two cycles. The generating cycle (Wood feeds Fire, Fire creates Earth, Earth bears Metal, Metal collects Water, Water nourishes Wood) creates smooth matches. Pairs on this cycle feel supportive, like older and younger siblings. The controlling cycle (Wood parts Earth, Earth absorbs Water, Water quenches Fire, Fire melts Metal, Metal cuts Wood) creates friction. Pairs on this cycle can still match, but they tend to push each other into growth.

Chinese Zodiac Compatibility FAQ

Short answers to the questions people search most about the 12 animal signs, drawn from the same animal-element system used on this site.

What is chinese zodiac compatibility?
The match is a layered system built on the 12-year animal cycle and the 10-year element cycle. Each birth year carries one animal and one element. The match between two people is read by looking at how those animals and elements line up across the three-harmony, six-harmony, six-conflict, and six-harm rules, plus the generating and controlling cycles of the five elements.
How is the chinese zodiac compatibility score calculated?
The score on this page is a 0 to 100 blend of three inputs. The first is the branch relationship (three-harmony, six-harmony, neutral, six-harm, six-conflict). The second is the element relationship (generating, neutral, controlling). The third is the yin-yang polarity of the stem. 90 to 100 is a best match, 65 to 89 is a good match, 40 to 64 is average, and 15 to 39 is a pairing that usually needs extra work.
Which chinese zodiac signs are most compatible with each other?
The six-harmony pairs are the closest one-on-one matches: Rat-Ox, Tiger-Pig, Rabbit-Dog, Dragon-Rooster, Snake-Monkey, Horse-Goat. The three-harmony triangles are the next strongest: Rat-Dragon-Monkey (Water), Ox-Snake-Rooster (Metal), Tiger-Horse-Dog (Fire), Rabbit-Goat-Pig (Wood).
Which chinese zodiac signs should not be together?
The six-conflict pairs are the roughest: Rat-Horse, Ox-Goat, Tiger-Monkey, Rabbit-Rooster, Dragon-Dog, Snake-Pig. The six-harm pairs drain slowly rather than fight: Rat-Goat, Ox-Horse, Tiger-Dog, Rabbit-Dragon, Snake-Pig, Monkey-Rooster. None of these is a hard rule, but they tend to bring more arguments and distance.
What is the difference between three-harmony and six-harmony in the 12 animal signs?
Three-harmony is a triangle of three animals 4 years apart. Six-harmony is a pair of two animals 6 years apart. Three-harmony ties carry a shared element and feel stable across long stretches of life. Six-harmony ties feel personal and intimate. The strongest match comes when a six-harmony pair also sits inside a three-harmony triangle, but that is rare.
Does the birth year element change the match?
Yes. A Rat born in 1972 carries Water, a Rat born in 1984 carries Wood, a Rat born in 1996 carries Fire. The animal is the same, but the feel of each Rat shifts with the element. The 1996 Fire Rat, for example, pairs more easily with Fire and Wood signs than the 1972 Water Rat.
Can two people with the same animal sign be compatible?
Yes, with a caveat. Same-element pairs feel easy and familiar, like a soft mirror. Clashing element pairs (a Water Rat with a Fire Rat) feel like a tense mirror. Same-animal and same-element pairs are the smoothest match of that group. Same-animal and clashing element pairs are the roughest.
Is the animal-pair match the same for love and business?
The animal rules are the same, but the weight shifts. Six-harmony and three-harmony pairs tend to last longer in love. Six-conflict pairs can still work as business partners because the friction fuels drive. Earth and Metal signs often build stronger business ties, while Fire and Wood signs often build stronger creative teams.
What is the worst match between the 12 animal signs?
The harshest matches are the six-conflict pairs joined with a controlling element. Rat with Horse, Ox with Goat, Tiger with Monkey, Rabbit with Rooster, Dragon with Dog, Snake with Pig. None is a rule, but each tends to bring the most arguments, distance, and clashing priorities of any animal pair.
What role does the five elements play in the match?
The five elements are the second layer. The animal decides the shape of the match, but the element decides the texture. Pairs on the generating cycle (Wood-Fire, Fire-Earth, Earth-Metal, Metal-Water, Water-Wood) feel supportive. Pairs on the controlling cycle (Wood-Earth, Earth-Water, Water-Fire, Fire-Metal, Metal-Wood) feel tense. Same-element pairs feel familiar.
How does the chart handle birth months?
The chart on this page uses the lunar year, which begins on Chinese New Year (late January to mid-February). If you were born in January or early February, your sign is set by the lunar new year, not the Gregorian calendar. Each year page on this site lists the exact Chinese New Year date so you can check.
