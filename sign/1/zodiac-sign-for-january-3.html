A Capricorn Story — Eli, January 3

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Eli OConnell was 30, a dog walker in Providence, and the person their friends called when they needed to make an actual decision. They were weirdly good with directions -- steady, responsible, and quietly funny once you had known them long enough. Eli kept a real, paper planner because the phone version, in their opinion, lacked the commitment required by a good Tuesday.

Later that afternoon, Eli sat in their car in a grocery store parking lot for 17 minutes and thought about nothing in particular. Sometimes a person just needs 17 minutes. They had a running list of things they wanted to do before they were 40: learn to bake sourdough, visit the coast, find that one jacket they lost in 2017, call their dad more. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Eli was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

By the time Eli got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47, which is a fine time for an adult to go to bed and nobody gets to say otherwise. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, is a different sign entirely.
