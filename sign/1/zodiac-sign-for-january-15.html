A January 15 Reader Story

A short story about someone born on this day

Profile Vignette

Hadley Patterson was 29, a coffee roaster in Kansas City, and the person friends called when they needed a real decision made. They were surprisingly patient -- steady, reliable, and got funnier the longer you knew them. Hadley kept a paper planner. The phone version, in their view, did not hold up.

Later that afternoon, Hadley sat in their car in a grocery store parking lot for 17 minutes and thought about nothing in particular. Sometimes a person just needs 17 minutes. They had a running list of things they wanted to do before they were 39: learn to bake sourdough, visit the coast, find that one jacket they lost in 2017, call their dad more. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Hadley was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

They told themselves they would take the day off next year, which is what they told themselves every year, and which they had never once actually done. Hadley was, for better and for worse, the kind of person who showed up. Who checked the work email on a Sunday. Who answered the phone when their mom called, even when they were in the middle of something. It was, they thought, probably fine. They were 29. The Capricorn in them knew exactly how much of the plan to stick to and exactly how much to wing.
