A Aquarius Story — Luna, January 23

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Luna Goodwin was 35, a bookstore manager in Austin, and the friend who would recommend a podcast at 11 p.m. and then not text back for three days. They were unreasonably loyal -- opinionated about things nobody else cared about and weirdly helpful when something was actually wrong. Their apartment had a stack of library books on the nightstand and a stack of books about a subject they had recently become, temporarily, obsessed with.

On their birthday, Luna got up early, made coffee, and stood at the window for about 10 minutes doing absolutely nothing. This was a ritual, even though they would never call it that out loud. Their roommate -- the one who had been in and out of four different jobs in two years -- left a cupcake on the counter with a single, slightly misspelled note. Luna ate it standing up and got to work 12 minutes early, because 12 is a good round number and because that's just the kind of person they were.

When their phone died at 9:30 p.m., instead of panicking, Luna found a charger -- eventually -- and spent the rest of the night looking at old pictures. Not the ones on Instagram. The ones from 2014, stored in a folder nobody opens anymore. They smiled at a few, laughed at one, and deleted exactly zero. Being a Aquarius, Luna thought, was less about the big, loud traits everyone talked about and more about the small, quiet ones: the things you save, the people you call first, the night you stay home and feel, for 15 very specific minutes, completely caught up.
