A short story about someone born on this day

Profile Vignette

Liam Goodwin was 36, a graphic designer in Raleigh, and the person friends called when they needed a real decision made. They were terrible at texting back -- steady, reliable, and got funnier the longer you knew them. Liam kept a paper planner. The phone version, in their view, did not hold up.

On their birthday, Liam got up early, made coffee, and stood at the window for about 10 minutes doing absolutely nothing. This was a ritual, even though they would never call it that out loud. Their roommate -- the one who had been in and out of four different jobs in two years -- left a cupcake on the counter with a single, slightly misspelled note. Liam ate it standing up and got to work 12 minutes early, because 12 is a good round number and because that's just the kind of person they were.

They told themselves they would take the day off next year, which is what they told themselves every year, and which they had never once actually done. Liam was, for better and for worse, the kind of person who showed up. Who checked the work email on a Sunday. Who answered the phone when their mom called, even when they were in the middle of something. It was, they thought, probably fine. They were 36. The Capricorn in them knew exactly how much of the plan to stick to and exactly how much to wing.
