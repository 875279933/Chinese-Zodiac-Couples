A Capricorn Story — Mia, January 16

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Mia Lindquist was 32, a software engineer in Austin, and the person their friends called when they needed to make an actual decision. They were secretly funny -- steady, responsible, and quietly funny once you had known them long enough. Mia kept a real, paper planner because the phone version, in their opinion, lacked the commitment required by a good Tuesday.

Their closest friend had been trying to plan a surprise for three weeks, and Mia had been quietly pretending not to know about it for 20 days. They liked surprises in theory and hated them in practice, which is why they had, very gently, texted their friend the exact outfit they were planning to wear. Not to control things, they told themselves. Just to make the pictures turn out. When they walked in the door, everyone yelled. Mia yelled back, which is, in their opinion, how birthdays are supposed to go.

They told themselves they would take the day off next year, which is what they told themselves every year, and which they had never once actually done. Mia was, for better and for worse, the kind of person who showed up. Who checked the work email on a Sunday. Who answered the phone when their mom called, even when they were in the middle of something. It was, they thought, probably fine. They were 32. The Capricorn in them knew exactly how much of the plan to stick to and exactly how much to wing.
