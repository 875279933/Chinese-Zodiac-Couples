An Aquarius Story — A Reader, January 20

A short story about someone born on this day

Profile Vignette

Sadie Vogel was 46, a high school coach in Boston, and the friend who would recommend a podcast at 11 p.m. and then not text back for three days. They were unexpectedly romantic -- opinionated about things nobody else cared about and weirdly helpful when something was actually wrong. Their apartment had a kettle that is almost always on and a stack of books about a subject they had recently become, temporarily, obsessed with.

Later that afternoon, Sadie sat in their car in a grocery store parking lot for 17 minutes and thought about nothing in particular. Sometimes a person just needs 17 minutes. They had a running list of things they wanted to do before they were 56: learn to bake sourdough, visit the coast, find that one jacket they lost in 2017, call their dad more. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Sadie was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

By the time Sadie got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, sits in a different sign.
