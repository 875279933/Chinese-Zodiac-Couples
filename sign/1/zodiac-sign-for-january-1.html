A Capricorn Story 鈥?A Reader, January 1

A short story about someone born on this day

Chloe Zimmerman was 45, a architect in Kansas City, and the person friends called when they needed a real decision made. They were terrible at texting back -- steady, reliable, and got funnier the longer you knew them. Chloe kept a paper planner. The phone app, in their view, did not hold up.

On their birthday, Chloe got up early, made coffee, and stood at the window for about 10 minutes doing absolutely nothing. This was a ritual, even though they would never call it that out loud. Their roommate -- the one who had been in and out of four different jobs in two years -- left a cupcake on the counter with a single, slightly misspelled note. Chloe ate it standing up and got to work 12 minutes early, because 12 is a good round number and because that's just the kind of person they were.

By the time Chloe got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, sits in a different sign.
