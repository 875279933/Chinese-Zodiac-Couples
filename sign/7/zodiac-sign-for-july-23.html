A Leo Story — Wyatt, July 23

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Wyatt Quinlan was 38, a dentist in Portland, and the unofficial social secretary of a friend group that had somehow stayed intact since college. They were quietly stubborn and the first person anyone called when they needed a speech, a toast, or someone to tell the waiter it was, in fact, their birthday. Wyatt genuinely did not care about being the center of attention, which is exactly why the center of attention kept finding them.

Their closest friend had been trying to plan a surprise for three weeks, and Wyatt had been quietly pretending not to know about it for 20 days. They liked surprises in theory and hated them in practice, which is why they had, very gently, texted their friend the exact outfit they were planning to wear. Not to control things, they told themselves. Just to make the pictures turn out. When they walked in the door, everyone yelled. Wyatt yelled back, which is, in their opinion, how birthdays are supposed to go.

By the time Wyatt got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47, which is a fine time for an adult to go to bed and nobody gets to say otherwise. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, is a different sign entirely.
