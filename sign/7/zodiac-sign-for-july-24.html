A Leo Story — Wren Castellanos, July 24

A short, personal read on this day

Reader Story
Wren Castellanos, on turning a year older

Wren Castellanos was 32, worked as a surfboard shaper in Miami, and had, in the last year, taken up pottery, birdwatching, and a half-finished attempt at Portuguese. None of them stuck. Wren was unreasonably loyal, and their texts came in paragraphs at 9 a.m. and silence for two days after. They didn't mean anything by it. They were just off somewhere else. By the time Wren got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47, which is a fine time for an adult to go to bed and nobody gets to say otherwise. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow was a different kind of day, and that was fine.
