A Taurus Story — Jordan, May 1

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Jordan Underwood was 39 and had lived in the same Baltimore apartment for 11 years. They worked as a graphic designer, and they liked it. Not in a dramatic way. In a 'I know where the good parking is and my coworker brings donuts on Fridays' kind of way. Jordan was quietly competitive, and their apartment had a record player that gets used more than any appliance, which told you almost everything you needed to know.

Later that afternoon, Jordan sat in their car in a grocery store parking lot for 17 minutes and thought about nothing in particular. Sometimes a person just needs 17 minutes. They had a running list of things they wanted to do before they were 49: learn to bake sourdough, visit the coast, find that one jacket they lost in 2017, call their dad more. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Jordan was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

When their phone died at 9:30 p.m., instead of panicking, Jordan found a charger -- eventually -- and spent the rest of the night looking at old pictures. Not the ones on Instagram. The ones from 2014, stored in a folder nobody opens anymore. They smiled at a few, laughed at one, and deleted exactly zero. Being a Taurus, Jordan thought, was less about the big, loud traits everyone talked about and more about the small, quiet ones: the things you save, the people you call first, the night you stay home and feel, for 15 very specific minutes, completely caught up.
