A Gemini Story — Quinn, May 27

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Quinn Nelson was 44, worked as a veterinary technician in Tucson, and had, in the last 12 months alone, taken up pottery, birdwatching, competitive trivia, and a short-lived attempt to learn Portuguese. None of them stuck. Quinn was a creature of habit, and their texts came in paragraphs at 9 a.m. and silence for two days after. They didn't mean anything by it. They were just off somewhere else.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Quinn spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on May 27, were not among them. This felt, in its own quiet way, like progress.

They told themselves they would take the day off next year, which is what they told themselves every year, and which they had never once actually done. Quinn was, for better and for worse, the kind of person who showed up. Who checked the work email on a Sunday. Who answered the phone when their mom called, even when they were in the middle of something. It was, they thought, probably fine. They were 44. The Gemini in them knew exactly how much of the plan to stick to and exactly how much to wing.
