A Taurus Story — Emma, May 13

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Emma Vogel was 38 and had lived in the same Raleigh apartment for 11 years. They worked as a veterinary technician, and they liked it. Not in a dramatic way. In a 'I know where the good parking is and my coworker brings donuts on Fridays' kind of way. Emma was unexpectedly romantic, and their apartment had a folded blanket on the back of the couch, which told you almost everything you needed to know.

Their closest friend had been trying to plan a surprise for three weeks, and Emma had been quietly pretending not to know about it for 20 days. They liked surprises in theory and hated them in practice, which is why they had, very gently, texted their friend the exact outfit they were planning to wear. Not to control things, they told themselves. Just to make the pictures turn out. When they walked in the door, everyone yelled. Emma yelled back, which is, in their opinion, how birthdays are supposed to go.

By the time Emma got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47, which is a fine time for an adult to go to bed and nobody gets to say otherwise. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, is a different sign entirely.
