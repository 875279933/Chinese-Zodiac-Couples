A Taurus Story — Jake, May 9

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Jake Lindquist was 46 and had lived in the same Boston apartment for 11 years. They worked as a barista, and they liked it. Not in a dramatic way. In a 'I know where the good parking is and my coworker brings donuts on Fridays' kind of way. Jake was unreasonably loyal, and their apartment had a stack of library books on the nightstand, which told you almost everything you needed to know.

On their birthday, Jake got up early, made coffee, and stood at the window for about 10 minutes doing absolutely nothing. This was a ritual, even though they would never call it that out loud. Their roommate -- the one who had been in and out of four different jobs in two years -- left a cupcake on the counter with a single, slightly misspelled note. Jake ate it standing up and got to work 12 minutes early, because 12 is a good round number and because that's just the kind of person they were.

They told themselves they would take the day off next year, which is what they told themselves every year, and which they had never once actually done. Jake was, for better and for worse, the kind of person who showed up. Who checked the work email on a Sunday. Who answered the phone when their mom called, even when they were in the middle of something. It was, they thought, probably fine. They were 46. The Taurus in them knew exactly how much of the plan to stick to and exactly how much to wing.
