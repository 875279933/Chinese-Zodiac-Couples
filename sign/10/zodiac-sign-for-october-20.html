A Libra Story — Clara, October 20

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Clara Fernandez was 39, a bike mechanic in Phoenix, and the only person in their friend group who could pick a restaurant without drama. They were weirdly good with directions, and their apartment had lighting so intentional that people always mentioned it. Clara had a folded blanket on the back of the couch, because a Libra without small, nice things is basically just a person with an opinion.

Their closest friend had been trying to plan a surprise for three weeks, and Clara had been quietly pretending not to know about it for 20 days. They liked surprises in theory and hated them in practice, which is why they had, very gently, texted their friend the exact outfit they were planning to wear. Not to control things, they told themselves. Just to make the pictures turn out. When they walked in the door, everyone yelled. Clara yelled back, which is, in their opinion, how birthdays are supposed to go.

By the time Clara got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47, which is a fine time for an adult to go to bed and nobody gets to say otherwise. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, is a different sign entirely.
