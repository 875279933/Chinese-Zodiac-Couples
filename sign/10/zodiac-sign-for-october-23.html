A Scorpio Story — Lucas, October 23

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Lucas Vogel was 28, a data analyst in Portland, and someone who had, on at least one occasion, found out something about a friend without them knowing and then, quietly, done something about it. They were a little too honest -- private in a way that felt intentional, not shy. Lucas kept a recipe box full of handwritten cards from their mom in a place nobody would look, because some things were just better that way.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Lucas spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on October 23, were not among them. This felt, in its own quiet way, like progress.

By the time Lucas got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47, which is a fine time for an adult to go to bed and nobody gets to say otherwise. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, is a different sign entirely.
