A Scorpio Story — Chloe, October 29

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Chloe Vogel was 27, a dentist in Philadelphia, and someone who had, on at least one occasion, found out something about a friend without them knowing and then, quietly, done something about it. They were the friend who brings the snacks -- private in a way that felt intentional, not shy. Chloe kept a window sill full of plants, some thriving and some clearly struggling in a place nobody would look, because some things were just better that way.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Chloe spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on October 29, were not among them. This felt, in its own quiet way, like progress.

Before falling asleep, Chloe wrote down one thing in a notebook they kept by the bed. They had been doing this, off and on, since they were 19. Sometimes the thing was big. Usually it was small. On October 29, it was small: a good sandwich, an honest compliment from a coworker, a text from an old friend they had been meaning to call. That was the thing about birthdays, Chloe thought. You expect a big moment and you usually end up with three or four small ones that, eventually, turn out to be the point.
