A Scorpio Story — Hannah, October 30

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Hannah Quinlan was 31, a data analyst in Portland, and someone who had, on at least one occasion, found out something about a friend without them knowing and then, quietly, done something about it. They were unexpectedly romantic -- private in a way that felt intentional, not shy. Hannah kept a window sill full of plants, some thriving and some clearly struggling in a place nobody would look, because some things were just better that way.

Later that afternoon, Hannah sat in their car in a grocery store parking lot for 17 minutes and thought about nothing in particular. Sometimes a person just needs 17 minutes. They had a running list of things they wanted to do before they were 41: learn to bake sourdough, visit the coast, find that one jacket they lost in 2017, call their dad more. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Hannah was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

Before falling asleep, Hannah wrote down one thing in a notebook they kept by the bed. They had been doing this, off and on, since they were 19. Sometimes the thing was big. Usually it was small. On October 30, it was small: a good sandwich, an honest compliment from a coworker, a text from an old friend they had been meaning to call. That was the thing about birthdays, Hannah thought. You expect a big moment and you usually end up with three or four small ones that, eventually, turn out to be the point.
