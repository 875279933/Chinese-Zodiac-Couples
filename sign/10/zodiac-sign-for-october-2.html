A Libra Story — Brendan, October 2

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Brendan Delgado was 41, a high school coach in Richmond, and the only person in their friend group who could pick a restaurant without drama. They were surprisingly patient, and their apartment had lighting so intentional that people always mentioned it. Brendan had a magnet from every state they've visited, because a Libra without small, nice things is basically just a person with an opinion.

Later that afternoon, Brendan sat in their car in a grocery store parking lot for 17 minutes and thought about nothing in particular. Sometimes a person just needs 17 minutes. They had a running list of things they wanted to do before they were 51: learn to bake sourdough, visit the coast, find that one jacket they lost in 2017, call their dad more. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Brendan was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

By the time Brendan got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47, which is a fine time for an adult to go to bed and nobody gets to say otherwise. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, is a different sign entirely.
