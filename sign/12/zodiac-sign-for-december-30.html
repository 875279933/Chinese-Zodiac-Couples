A Capricorn Story — Hollis Trent, December 30

A short, personal read for this birthday

H
Hollis TrentBorn December 30 · Capricorn

Hollis Trent was 36, a luthier in Indianapolis, and the kind of person who had, on at least one occasion, refused to finish a repair because the instrument, in their words, "had been mistreated, and the customer needed to hear that". They were opinionated in a way that some customers loved and others didn't come back from, and they kept a folder of every instrument they had ever built, with a single sentence written about each one, in their own hand, because some things were just better that way.

On their birthday, Hollis Trent spent the morning in the shop, the afternoon on a long walk, and the evening with the same friends they had been having dinner with for almost 15 years. They had a running list of things they wanted to get to before the next decade: finish the commission, take the trip they keep talking about, fix the kitchen. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Hollis Trent was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

When their phone died at 9:30 p.m., instead of panicking, Hollis Trent found a charger -- eventually -- and spent the rest of the night looking at old pictures. Not the ones on Instagram. The ones from 2014, stored in a folder nobody opens anymore. They smiled at a few, laughed at one, and deleted exactly zero. A Capricorn, Hollis thought, was someone who could spend six months on a single piece of wood and call it patience, not perfectionism
