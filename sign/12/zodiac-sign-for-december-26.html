A Capricorn Story — Nora, December 26

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Nora Turnbull was 25, a software engineer in Boston, and the person their friends called when they needed to make an actual decision. They were unreasonably loyal -- steady, responsible, and quietly funny once you had known them long enough. Nora kept a real, paper planner because the phone version, in their opinion, lacked the commitment required by a good Tuesday.

On their birthday, Nora got up early, made coffee, and stood at the window for about 10 minutes doing absolutely nothing. This was a ritual, even though they would never call it that out loud. Their roommate -- the one who had been in and out of four different jobs in two years -- left a cupcake on the counter with a single, slightly misspelled note. Nora ate it standing up and got to work 12 minutes early, because 12 is a good round number and because that's just the kind of person they were.

By the time Nora got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47, which is a fine time for an adult to go to bed and nobody gets to say otherwise. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, is a different sign entirely.
