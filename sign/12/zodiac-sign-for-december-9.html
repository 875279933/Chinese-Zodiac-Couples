A Sagittarius Story — Beatrix Holloway, December 9

A short, personal read for this birthday

B
Beatrix HollowayBorn December 9 · Sagittarius

Beatrix Holloway was 24, a potter in Asheville, and the kind of person who had, on at least one occasion, been told they were "a lot" by someone who later became a regular customer. They were direct in a way that was, in the right hands, a kind of kindness, and the reason half their friend group had a story that started with 'Okay so Beatrix Holloway said we should...'.

On their birthday, Beatrix Holloway sat on the porch in the morning with coffee they had forgotten they had made. They had a running list of things they wanted to get to before the next decade: get the kiln fixed, take a real day off, finish the mug commission for the cafe downtown. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Beatrix Holloway was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

When their phone died at 9:30 p.m., instead of panicking, Beatrix Holloway found a charger -- eventually -- and spent the rest of the night looking at old pictures. Not the ones on Instagram. The ones from 2014, stored in a folder nobody opens anymore. They smiled at a few, laughed at one, and deleted exactly zero. A Sagittarius, Beatrix thought, was someone who made what they wanted and let the rest of the world figure it out
