A Capricorn Story — Augustus Pryor, December 28

A short, personal read for this birthday

A
Augustus PryorBorn December 28 · Capricorn

Augustus Pryor was 48, a high school football coach in St. Louis, and the kind of person who had, on at least one occasion, benched a star player for attitude and been right, which had cost them about a season of polite arguments from parents. They were patient with kids, less patient with adults, and they kept a clipboard from their first year of coaching, in a drawer at home, with the names of every player who had ever quit the team written on the back in pencil, because some things were just better that way.

On their birthday, Augustus Pryor spent the morning at practice, the afternoon on the field with the underclassmen, and the evening watching film that, frankly, could have waited. They had a running list of things they wanted to get to before the next decade: finish the season strong, take the wife to the lake, fix the porch. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Augustus Pryor was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

When their phone died at 9:30 p.m., instead of panicking, Augustus Pryor found a charger -- eventually -- and spent the rest of the night looking at old pictures. Not the ones on Instagram. The ones from 2014, stored in a folder nobody opens anymore. They smiled at a few, laughed at one, and deleted exactly zero. A Capricorn, Augustus thought, was someone who could spend 20 years on the same field and still, somehow, look forward to the next season
