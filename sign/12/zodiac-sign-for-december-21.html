A Sagittarius Story — Ella, December 21

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Ella Nelson was 29, a dentist in Milwaukee, and the kind of person who would take a three-hour detour on a drive home just to see a roadside attraction they had already seen. They were a creature of habit -- loud, honest, and the reason half their friend group had a story that started with 'Okay so Ella said we should...'.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Ella spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on December 21, were not among them. This felt, in its own quiet way, like progress.

They told themselves they would take the day off next year, which is what they told themselves every year, and which they had never once actually done. Ella was, for better and for worse, the kind of person who showed up. Who checked the work email on a Sunday. Who answered the phone when their mom called, even when they were in the middle of something. It was, they thought, probably fine. They were 29. The Sagittarius in them knew exactly how much of the plan to stick to and exactly how much to wing.
