A Sagittarius Story — Eli, December 15

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Eli Nelson was 30, a data analyst in Austin, and the kind of person who would take a three-hour detour on a drive home just to see a roadside attraction they had already seen. They were the friend who brings the snacks -- loud, honest, and the reason half their friend group had a story that started with 'Okay so Eli said we should...'.

On their birthday, Eli got up early, made coffee, and stood at the window for about 10 minutes doing absolutely nothing. This was a ritual, even though they would never call it that out loud. Their roommate -- the one who had been in and out of four different jobs in two years -- left a cupcake on the counter with a single, slightly misspelled note. Eli ate it standing up and got to work 12 minutes early, because 12 is a good round number and because that's just the kind of person they were.

When their phone died at 9:30 p.m., instead of panicking, Eli found a charger -- eventually -- and spent the rest of the night looking at old pictures. Not the ones on Instagram. The ones from 2014, stored in a folder nobody opens anymore. They smiled at a few, laughed at one, and deleted exactly zero. Being a Sagittarius, Eli thought, was less about the big, loud traits everyone talked about and more about the small, quiet ones: the things you save, the people you call first, the night you stay home and feel, for 15 very specific minutes, completely caught up.
