They were patient in interviews, ruthless in writeups, and the reason half their friend group had a story that started with 'Okay so Imogen Sato said we should...'.

On their birthday, Imogen Sato spent a Sunday in the park reading a novel and, in the middle of it, realized they hadn't thought about work once. They had a running list of things they wanted to get to before the next decade: finish the dissertation, visit the family in Osaka, replace the broken chair. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Imogen Sato was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

The ones from 2014, stored in a folder nobody opens anymore. They smiled at a few, laughed at one, and deleted exactly zero.
