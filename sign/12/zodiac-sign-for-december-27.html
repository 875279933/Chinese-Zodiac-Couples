A Capricorn Story — Wren, December 27

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Wren OConnell was 28, a dog walker in Salt Lake City, and the person their friends called when they needed to make an actual decision. They were terrible at texting back -- steady, responsible, and quietly funny once you had known them long enough. Wren kept a real, paper planner because the phone version, in their opinion, lacked the commitment required by a good Tuesday.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Wren spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on December 27, were not among them. This felt, in its own quiet way, like progress.

By the time Wren got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47, which is a fine time for an adult to go to bed and nobody gets to say otherwise. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, is a different sign entirely.
