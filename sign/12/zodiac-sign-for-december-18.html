A Sagittarius Story — Bram Holcomb, December 18

A short, personal read for this birthday

B
Bram Holcomb
Born December 18 · Sagittarius

Bram Holcomb was 43, a auto designer in Detroit, and the kind of person who had, on at least one occasion, sketched a curve on a napkin in a diner that ended up on a production car. They were private in a way that made people assume they were easy to know, when in fact the opposite was true, and the reason half their friend group had a story that started with 'Okay so Bram Holcomb said we should...'.

On their birthday, Bram Holcomb spent an hour in the garage on a Sunday, doing nothing in particular, which was, they had come to understand, a form of rest. They had a running list of things they wanted to get to before the next decade: finish the side project, fix the basement, take the trip to the UP. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Bram Holcomb was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

When their phone died at 9:30 p.m., instead of panicking, Bram Holcomb found a charger -- eventually -- and spent the rest of the night looking at old pictures. Not the ones on Instagram. The ones from 2014, stored in a folder nobody opens anymore. They smiled at a few, laughed at one, and deleted exactly zero. A Sagittarius, Bram thought, was someone who could sketch the shape of a thing three years before anyone else saw it
