A short, personal read for this birthday

I
Idris Halvorsen
Born December 16 · Sagittarius

Idris Halvorsen was 47, a rancher in Bismarck, and the kind of person who had, on at least one occasion, predicted a hard winter in late September and been right. They were patient in a way that city people sometimes mistook for slow, and the reason half their friend group had a story that started with 'Okay so Idris Halvorsen said we should...'.

On their birthday, Idris Halvorsen spent the afternoon moving cattle across a frozen pasture and called it, quietly, a perfect day. They had a running list of things they wanted to get to before the next decade: fix the fence by the south ridge, take the wife to see her sister, replace the roof on the barn. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Idris Halvorsen was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

When their phone died at 9:30 p.m., instead of panicking, Idris Halvorsen found a charger -- eventually -- and spent the rest of the night looking at old pictures. Not the ones on Instagram. The ones from 2014, stored in a folder nobody opens anymore. They smiled at a few, laughed at one, and deleted exactly zero. A Sagittarius, Idris thought, was someone who could outlast a bad winter and somehow still be in a good mood by spring
