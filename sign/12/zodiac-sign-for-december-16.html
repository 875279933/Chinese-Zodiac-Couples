A Sagittarius Story — Caleb, December 16

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Caleb Iverson was 33, a physical therapist in Raleigh, and the kind of person who would take a three-hour detour on a drive home just to see a roadside attraction they had already seen. They were a little too honest -- loud, honest, and the reason half their friend group had a story that started with 'Okay so Caleb said we should...'.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Caleb spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on December 16, were not among them. This felt, in its own quiet way, like progress.

Before falling asleep, Caleb wrote down one thing in a notebook they kept by the bed. They had been doing this, off and on, since they were 19. Sometimes the thing was big. Usually it was small. On December 16, it was small: a good sandwich, an honest compliment from a coworker, a text from an old friend they had been meaning to call. That was the thing about birthdays, Caleb thought. You expect a big moment and you usually end up with three or four small ones that, eventually, turn out to be the point.
