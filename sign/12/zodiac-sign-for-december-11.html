A Sagittarius Story — Iris, December 11

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Iris Calloway was 37, a architect in Detroit, and the kind of person who would take a three-hour detour on a drive home just to see a roadside attraction they had already seen. They were quietly competitive -- loud, honest, and the reason half their friend group had a story that started with 'Okay so Iris said we should...'.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Iris spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on December 11, were not among them. This felt, in its own quiet way, like progress.

By the time Iris got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47, which is a fine time for an adult to go to bed and nobody gets to say otherwise. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, is a different sign entirely.
