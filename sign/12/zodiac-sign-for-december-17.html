A Sagittarius Story — Magdalene Cole, December 17

A short, personal read for this birthday

M
Magdalene Cole
Born December 17 · Sagittarius

Magdalene Cole was 32, a midwife in Burlington, and the kind of person who had, on at least one occasion, known a labor was going to be long before anyone else in the room. They were calm in a way that was, in this line of work, the actual job, and the reason half their friend group had a story that started with 'Okay so Magdalene Cole said we should...'.

On their birthday, Magdalene Cole walked the same lake loop they had walked every week for six years and noticed, for the first time, a heron on the far shore. They had a running list of things they wanted to get to before the next decade: finish the certification, plant the garden, take the trip they keep talking about. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Magdalene Cole was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

When their phone died at 9:30 p.m., instead of panicking, Magdalene Cole found a charger -- eventually -- and spent the rest of the night looking at old pictures. Not the ones on Instagram. The ones from 2014, stored in a folder nobody opens anymore. They smiled at a few, laughed at one, and deleted exactly zero. A Sagittarius, Magdalene thought, was someone whose work happened in the smallest hours, when most people were asleep
