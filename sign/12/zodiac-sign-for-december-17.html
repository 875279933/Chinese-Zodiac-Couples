A Sagittarius Story — Liam, December 17

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Liam Delgado was 37, a construction foreman in Seattle, and the kind of person who would take a three-hour detour on a drive home just to see a roadside attraction they had already seen. They were quietly stubborn -- loud, honest, and the reason half their friend group had a story that started with 'Okay so Liam said we should...'.

Later that afternoon, Liam sat in their car in a grocery store parking lot for 17 minutes and thought about nothing in particular. Sometimes a person just needs 17 minutes. They had a running list of things they wanted to do before they were 47: learn to bake sourdough, visit the coast, find that one jacket they lost in 2017, call their dad more. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Liam was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

Before falling asleep, Liam wrote down one thing in a notebook they kept by the bed. They had been doing this, off and on, since they were 19. Sometimes the thing was big. Usually it was small. On December 17, it was small: a good sandwich, an honest compliment from a coworker, a text from an old friend they had been meaning to call. That was the thing about birthdays, Liam thought. You expect a big moment and you usually end up with three or four small ones that, eventually, turn out to be the point.
