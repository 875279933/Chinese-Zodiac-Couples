A Aries Story — Ella, March 27

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Ella Ashford turned 36 on a Tuesday and, being an Aries, had already made and abandoned three birthday plans before 9 a.m. They worked as a photographer in Portland, where they were known for finishing things early and arguing with the copy machine on principle. Ella was a creature of habit -- the kind of person who would drive 45 minutes out of the way to try a sandwich someone mentioned, then leave an honest one-star review when it was mid.

On their birthday, Ella got up early, made coffee, and stood at the window for about 10 minutes doing absolutely nothing. This was a ritual, even though they would never call it that out loud. Their roommate -- the one who had been in and out of four different jobs in two years -- left a cupcake on the counter with a single, slightly misspelled note. Ella ate it standing up and got to work 12 minutes early, because 12 is a good round number and because that's just the kind of person they were.

By the time Ella got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47, which is a fine time for an adult to go to bed and nobody gets to say otherwise. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, is a different sign entirely.
