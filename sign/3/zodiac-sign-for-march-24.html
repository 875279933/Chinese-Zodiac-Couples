A Aries Story — Dylan, March 24

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Dylan Patterson turned 25 on a Tuesday and, being an Aries, had already made and abandoned three birthday plans before 9 a.m. They worked as a barista in Phoenix, where they were known for finishing things early and arguing with the copy machine on principle. Dylan was quietly competitive -- the kind of person who would drive 45 minutes out of the way to try a sandwich someone mentioned, then leave an honest one-star review when it was mid.

Later that afternoon, Dylan sat in their car in a grocery store parking lot for 17 minutes and thought about nothing in particular. Sometimes a person just needs 17 minutes. They had a running list of things they wanted to do before they were 35: learn to bake sourdough, visit the coast, find that one jacket they lost in 2017, call their dad more. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Dylan was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

They told themselves they would take the day off next year, which is what they told themselves every year, and which they had never once actually done. Dylan was, for better and for worse, the kind of person who showed up. Who checked the work email on a Sunday. Who answered the phone when their mom called, even when they were in the middle of something. It was, they thought, probably fine. They were 25. The Aries in them knew exactly how much of the plan to stick to and exactly how much to wing.
