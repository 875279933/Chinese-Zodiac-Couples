A Pisces Story — Grace, March 19

A short story about someone born on this day, kept deliberately unsentimental

Grace Jeffries was 29, a editor in Philadelphia, and the kind of person who would cry at a dog food commercial and then pretend they didn't. They were weirdly good with directions — the friend who always had a playlist for the mood you didn't know you were in. Grace kept a framed photo from a 2012 trip because their aunt had given it to them when they were 12, and that was, honestly, reason enough.

Later that afternoon, Grace sat in their car in a grocery store parking lot for 17 minutes and thought about nothing in particular. Sometimes a person just needs 17 minutes. They had a running list of things they wanted to do before they were 39: learn to bake sourdough, visit the coast, find that one jacket they lost in 2017, call their dad more. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Grace was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

Before falling asleep, Grace wrote down one thing in a notebook they kept by the bed. They had been doing this, off and on, since they were 19. Sometimes the thing was big. Usually it was small. On March 19, it was small: a good sandwich, an honest compliment from a coworker, a text from an old friend they had been meaning to call. That was the thing about birthdays, Grace thought. You expect a big moment and you usually end up with three or four small ones that, eventually, turn out to be the point.
