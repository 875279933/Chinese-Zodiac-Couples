What Is the Zodiac Sign for March 26?

A reading from the first sign of the zodiac.

By the time the calendar reaches March 26, the sun has been in Aries for about a week. Aries is the first sign of the zodiac, associated with initiative, courage, and a kind of impatience that, more often than not, gets things done. A March 26 birthday sits well past the start of the season, but still close enough to the equinox that the energy reads as fresh, forward-looking, and slightly restless.

It's the kind of Aries that knows exactly what they want and is already three steps into figuring out how to get it. They are the friend who suggests the trip, books the table, and sends the playlist, all before anyone else has put their coat on.

An Aries Story — Brendan, March 26

A short story about someone born on this day, kept deliberately unsentimental

Brendan Fernandez turned 32 on a Tuesday and, being an Aries, had already made and abandoned three birthday plans before 9 a.m. They worked as a editor in Philadelphia, where they were known for finishing things early and arguing with the copy machine on principle. Brendan was weirdly good with directions -- the kind of person who would drive 45 minutes out of the way to try a sandwich someone mentioned, then leave an honest one-star review when it was mid.

On their birthday, Brendan got up early, made coffee, and stood at the window for about 10 minutes doing absolutely nothing. This was a ritual, even though they would never call it that out loud. Their roommate -- the one who had been in and out of four different jobs in two years -- left a cupcake on the counter with a single, slightly misspelled note. Brendan ate it standing up and got to work 12 minutes early, because 12 is a good round number and because that's just the kind of person they were.

By the time Brendan got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, sits in a different sign.
