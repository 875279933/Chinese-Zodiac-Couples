An Aries Story — Liam, March 23

A short story about someone born on this day, kept deliberately unsentimental

Liam Turnbull turned 43 on a Tuesday and, being an Aries, had already made and abandoned three birthday plans before 9 a.m. They worked as a elementary school teacher in Savannah, where they were known for finishing things early and arguing with the copy machine on principle. Liam was unreasonably loyal — the kind of person who would drive 45 minutes out of the way to try a sandwich someone mentioned, then leave an honest one-star review when it was mid.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to "have a good one," and Liam spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on March 23, were not among them. It felt, in its own way, like progress.

They told themselves they would take the day off next year, which is what they told themselves every year, and which they had never once actually done. Liam was, for better and for worse, the kind of person who showed up. Who checked the work email on a Sunday. Who answered the phone when their mom called, even when they were in the middle of something. It was, they thought, probably fine. They were 43. The Aries in them knew exactly how much of the plan to stick to and exactly how much to wing.
