A Aries Story — Graham, April 13

Graham · April 13

Aries

Graham Lehrer turned 47 on a Tuesday and, being an Aries, had already made and abandoned three birthday plans before 9 a.m. They worked as a court reporter in St. Paul, where they were known for finishing things early and arguing with the copy machine on principle. Graham was almost too organized -- the kind of person who would drive 45 minutes out of the way to try a sandwich someone mentioned, then leave an honest one-star review when it was mid.

Their closest friend had been trying to plan a surprise for three weeks, and Graham had been quietly pretending not to know about it for 20 days. They liked surprises in theory and hated them in practice, which is why they had, very gently, texted their friend the exact outfit they were planning to wear. Not to control things, they told themselves. Just to make the pictures turn out. When they walked in the door, everyone yelled. Graham yelled back, which is, in their view, how birthdays go.

Before falling asleep, Graham wrote down one thing in a notebook they kept by the bed. They had been doing this, off and on, since they were 19. Sometimes the thing was big. Usually it was small. On April 13, it was small: a good sandwich, an honest compliment from a coworker, a text from an old friend they had been meaning to call. That was the thing about birthdays, Graham thought. You expect a big moment and you usually end up with three or four small ones that, eventually, turn out to be the point.
