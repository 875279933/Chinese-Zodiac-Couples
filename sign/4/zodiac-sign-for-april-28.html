A Taurus Story — Aaron, April 28

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Aaron Ramirez was 43 and had lived in the same St. Louis apartment for 11 years. They worked as a coffee roaster, and they liked it. Not in a dramatic way. In a 'I know where the good parking is and my coworker brings donuts on Fridays' kind of way. Aaron was unexpectedly romantic, and their apartment had a window sill full of plants, some thriving and some clearly struggling, which told you almost everything you needed to know.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Aaron spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on April 28, were not among them. This felt, in its own quiet way, like progress.

Before falling asleep, Aaron wrote down one thing in a notebook they kept by the bed. They had been doing this, off and on, since they were 19. Sometimes the thing was big. Usually it was small. On April 28, it was small: a good sandwich, an honest compliment from a coworker, a text from an old friend they had been meaning to call. That was the thing about birthdays, Aaron thought. You expect a big moment and you usually end up with three or four small ones that, eventually, turn out to be the point.
