A Taurus Story — Aaron, April 28

A short story about someone born on this day
A

Aaron

Born April 28 · Taurus

Ryan Tessler was 38 and had lived in the same Tallahassee apartment for 11 years. They worked as a court mediator, and they liked it. Not in a dramatic way. In a 'I know where the good parking is and my coworker brings donuts on Fridays' kind of way. Ryan was the friend who actually reads the full article, and their apartment had a stack of well-worn paperback mysteries on the nightstand, which told you almost everything you needed to know.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Aaron spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on April 28, were not among them. It felt, in its own way, like progress.

Before falling asleep, Aaron wrote down one thing in a notebook they kept by the bed. They had been doing this, off and on, since they were 19. Sometimes the thing was big. Usually it was small. On April 28, it was small: a good sandwich, an honest compliment from a coworker, a text from an old friend they had been meaning to call. That was the thing about birthdays, Aaron thought. You expect a big moment and you usually end up with three or four small ones that, eventually, turn out to be the point.
