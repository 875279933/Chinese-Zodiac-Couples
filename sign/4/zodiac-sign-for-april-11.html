A Aries Story — Lily, April 11

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Lily Kirkland turned 27 on a Tuesday and, being an Aries, had already made and abandoned three birthday plans before 9 a.m. They worked as a data analyst in Denver, where they were known for finishing things early and arguing with the copy machine on principle. Lily was weirdly good with directions -- the kind of person who would drive 45 minutes out of the way to try a sandwich someone mentioned, then leave an honest one-star review when it was mid.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Lily spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on April 11, were not among them. This felt, in its own quiet way, like progress.

Before falling asleep, Lily wrote down one thing in a notebook they kept by the bed. They had been doing this, off and on, since they were 19. Sometimes the thing was big. Usually it was small. On April 11, it was small: a good sandwich, an honest compliment from a coworker, a text from an old friend they had been meaning to call. That was the thing about birthdays, Lily thought. You expect a big moment and you usually end up with three or four small ones that, eventually, turn out to be the point.
