A Aries Story — Gavin, April 5

A short story about someone born on this day
G
Gavin
Born April 5 · Aries

Gavin Jeffries turned 27 on a Tuesday and, being an Aries, had already made and abandoned three birthday plans before 9 a.m. They worked as a physical therapist in Baltimore, where they were known for finishing things early and arguing with the copy machine on principle. Gavin was unexpectedly romantic -- the kind of person who would drive 45 minutes out of the way to try a sandwich someone mentioned, then leave an honest one-star review when it was mid.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Gavin spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on April 5, were not among them. It felt, in its own way, like progress.

When the phone died at 9:30 p.m., instead of panicking, Gavin found a charger -- eventually -- and spent the rest of the night looking at old pictures. Not the ones on Instagram. The ones from 2014, in a folder nobody opens anymore. They smiled at a few, laughed at one, and did not delete any. Being a Aries, Gavin thought, was less about the loud traits everyone talked about and more about the small, quiet ones: the things you save, the people you call first, the night you stay home and feel, for 15 very specific minutes, completely caught up.
