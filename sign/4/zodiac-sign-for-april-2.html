A Aries Story — Ruby, April 2

A short story about someone born on this day

Ruby
Born April 2 · Aries

Margot Yentes turned 29 on a Tuesday and, being an Aries, had already made and abandoned three birthday plans before 9 a.m. They worked as a youth librarian in Minneapolis, where they were known for finishing things early and arguing with the copy machine on principle. Margot was gently stubborn -- the kind of person who would drive 45 minutes out of the way to try a sandwich someone mentioned, then leave an honest one-star review when it was mid.

Their closest friend had been trying to plan a surprise for three weeks, and Ruby had been quietly pretending not to know about it for 20 days. They liked surprises in theory and hated them in practice, which is why they had, very gently, texted their friend the exact outfit they were planning to wear. Not to control things, they told themselves. Just to make the pictures turn out. When they walked in the door, everyone yelled. Ruby yelled back, which is, in their view, how birthdays go.

By the time Ruby got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, sits in a different sign.
