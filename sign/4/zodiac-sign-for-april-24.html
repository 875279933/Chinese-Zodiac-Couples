Profile No. 24

Clara · April 24 · Taurus

A working sketch, not a verdict

Delia Voss was 36 and had lived in the same Tucson apartment for 11 years. They worked as a pediatric dietitian, and they liked it. Not in a dramatic way. In a 'I know where the good parking is and my coworker brings donuts on Fridays' kind of way. Delia was the friend who brings the snacks, and their apartment had a recipe box full of handwritten cards from their mom, which told you almost everything you needed to know.

Their closest friend had been trying to plan a surprise for three weeks, and Clara had been quietly pretending not to know about it for 20 days. They liked surprises in theory and hated them in practice, which is why they had, very gently, texted their friend the exact outfit they were planning to wear. Not to control things, they told themselves. Just to make the pictures turn out. When they walked in the door, everyone yelled. Clara yelled back, which is, in their view, how birthdays go.

When the phone died at 9:30 p.m., instead of panicking, Clara found a charger -- eventually -- and spent the rest of the night looking at old pictures. Not the ones on Instagram. The ones from 2014, in a folder nobody opens anymore. They smiled at a few, laughed at one, and did not delete any. Being a Taurus, Clara thought, was less about the loud traits everyone talked about and more about the small, quiet ones: the things you save, the people you call first, the night you stay home and feel, for 15 very specific minutes, completely caught up.
