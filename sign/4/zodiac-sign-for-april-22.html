Profile No. 22

Gavin · April 22 · Taurus

A working sketch, not a verdict

Chris Bergstrom was 41 and had lived in the same Spokane apartment for 11 years. They worked as a high school assistant principal, and they liked it. Not in a dramatic way. In a 'I know where the good parking is and my coworker brings donuts on Fridays' kind of way. Chris was the friend who keeps a spare phone charger in their bag, and their apartment had a stack of well-worn paperback mysteries on the nightstand, which told you almost everything you needed to know.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Gavin spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on April 22, were not among them. It felt, in its own way, like progress.

By the time Gavin got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, sits in a different sign.
