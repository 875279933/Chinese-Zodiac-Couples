A Virgo Story — Maren Schaefer, September 7

A short, personal read on this day

Maren Schaefer was 45, worked as a librarian in Madison, and kept a notebook by the bed for things they wanted to remember. None of them were dramatic. Maren was the friend who answered the phone at inconvenient hours, then talked for twenty minutes about something completely unrelated. They didn't mean to do it. It just worked out that way. By the time Maren got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they had opened by mistake. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47, which is a fine time for an adult to go to bed and nobody gets to say otherwise. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow was a different kind of day, and that was fine.
