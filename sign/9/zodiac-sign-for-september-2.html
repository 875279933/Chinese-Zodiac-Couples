A Virgo Story — Camille, September 2

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Camille Merriman was 37, worked as a data analyst in Raleigh, and maintained a color-coded Google Calendar so specific that even their dentist commented on it. They were unreasonably loyal -- the friend who would proofread your resume, tell you the jacket was not the one, and then drive you to the interview anyway. Their apartment had a recipe box full of handwritten cards from their mom, because that was the kind of thing a Virgo would notice and fix.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Camille spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on September 2, were not among them. This felt, in its own quiet way, like progress.

They told themselves they would take the day off next year, which is what they told themselves every year, and which they had never once actually done. Camille was, for better and for worse, the kind of person who showed up. Who checked the work email on a Sunday. Who answered the phone when their mom called, even when they were in the middle of something. It was, they thought, probably fine. They were 37. The Virgo in them knew exactly how much of the plan to stick to and exactly how much to wing.
