A Virgo Story — Devin, September 5

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Devin Esposito was 26, worked as a architect in Boston, and maintained a color-coded Google Calendar so specific that even their dentist commented on it. They were weirdly good with directions -- the friend who would proofread your resume, tell you the jacket was not the one, and then drive you to the interview anyway. Their apartment had a magnet from every state they've visited, because that was the kind of thing a Virgo would notice and fix.

Their closest friend had been trying to plan a surprise for three weeks, and Devin had been quietly pretending not to know about it for 20 days. They liked surprises in theory and hated them in practice, which is why they had, very gently, texted their friend the exact outfit they were planning to wear. Not to control things, they told themselves. Just to make the pictures turn out. When they walked in the door, everyone yelled. Devin yelled back, which is, in their opinion, how birthdays are supposed to go.

They told themselves they would take the day off next year, which is what they told themselves every year, and which they had never once actually done. Devin was, for better and for worse, the kind of person who showed up. Who checked the work email on a Sunday. Who answered the phone when their mom called, even when they were in the middle of something. It was, they thought, probably fine. They were 26. The Virgo in them knew exactly how much of the plan to stick to and exactly how much to wing.
