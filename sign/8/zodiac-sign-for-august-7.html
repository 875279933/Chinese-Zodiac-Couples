A Leo Story — Lila, August 7

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Lila DAmico was 29, a graphic designer in Denver, and the unofficial social secretary of a friend group that had somehow stayed intact since college. They were a little too honest and the first person anyone called when they needed a speech, a toast, or someone to tell the waiter it was, in fact, their birthday. Lila genuinely did not care about being the center of attention, which is exactly why the center of attention kept finding them.

On their birthday, Lila got up early, made coffee, and stood at the window for about 10 minutes doing absolutely nothing. This was a ritual, even though they would never call it that out loud. Their roommate -- the one who had been in and out of four different jobs in two years -- left a cupcake on the counter with a single, slightly misspelled note. Lila ate it standing up and got to work 12 minutes early, because 12 is a good round number and because that's just the kind of person they were.

Before falling asleep, Lila wrote down one thing in a notebook they kept by the bed. They had been doing this, off and on, since they were 19. Sometimes the thing was big. Usually it was small. On August 7, it was small: a good sandwich, an honest compliment from a coworker, a text from an old friend they had been meaning to call. That was the thing about birthdays, Lila thought. You expect a big moment and you usually end up with three or four small ones that, eventually, turn out to be the point.
