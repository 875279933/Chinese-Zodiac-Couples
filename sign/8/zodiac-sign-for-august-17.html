A Leo Story — Aiden, August 17

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Aiden Hartley was 42, a editor in St. Louis, and the unofficial social secretary of a friend group that had somehow stayed intact since college. They were unreasonably loyal and the first person anyone called when they needed a speech, a toast, or someone to tell the waiter it was, in fact, their birthday. Aiden genuinely did not care about being the center of attention, which is exactly why the center of attention kept finding them.

On their birthday, Aiden got up early, made coffee, and stood at the window for about 10 minutes doing absolutely nothing. This was a ritual, even though they would never call it that out loud. Their roommate -- the one who had been in and out of four different jobs in two years -- left a cupcake on the counter with a single, slightly misspelled note. Aiden ate it standing up and got to work 12 minutes early, because 12 is a good round number and because that's just the kind of person they were.

By the time Aiden got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47, which is a fine time for an adult to go to bed and nobody gets to say otherwise. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, is a different sign entirely.
