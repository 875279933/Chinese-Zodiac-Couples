A Virgo Story — Cyrus Whittaker, August 26

A short, personal read on the zodiac sign for August 26

Reader Story · Tucson

Cyrus Whittaker was 46, worked as a telescope maker in Tucson, and had, in the last year, taken up pottery, birdwatching, and a half-finished attempt at Portuguese. None of them stuck. Cyrus was the kind of person who texted back in paragraphs, fell asleep before 11, and always knew where the spare batteries were. By the time Cyrus got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. The birthday itself went the way birthdays in the opening week of Virgo season usually go: low-key, well-organized, with a long walk somewhere, a couple of good laughs, a small gift they did not need but will absolutely use. Before they turned the light off, they wrote down one thing in a notebook they kept by the bed. They had been doing this, off and on, since they were 19. Sometimes the thing was big. Usually it was small. On August 26, it was small: a good sandwich, an honest compliment from a coworker, a text from an old friend they had been meaning to call. That was the part of Virgo that nobody writes the loud essays about, the part that keeps the rest of it honest. Tomorrow was a different kind of day, and that was fine.
