They were opinionated in a way that some customers loved and others didn't come back from -- loud, honest, and the reason half their friend group had a story that started with 'Okay so Hollis Trent said we should...'.

On their birthday, Hollis Trent spent the morning in the shop, the afternoon on a long walk, and the evening with the same friends they had been having dinner with for almost 15 years. They finish the commission, take the trip they keep talking about, fix the kitchen, because that's just the kind of person they were.

When their phone died at 9:30 p.m., instead of panicking, Hollis Trent found a charger -- eventually -- and spent the rest of the night looking at old pictures. Not the ones on Instagram.
