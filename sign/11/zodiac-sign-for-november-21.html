A Scorpio Story — Logan, November 21

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Logan Whitaker was 25, a barista in Denver, and someone who had, on at least one occasion, found out something about a friend without them knowing and then, quietly, done something about it. They were weirdly good with directions -- private in a way that felt intentional, not shy. Logan kept a recipe box full of handwritten cards from their mom in a place nobody would look, because some things were just better that way.

On their birthday, Logan got up early, made coffee, and stood at the window for about 10 minutes doing absolutely nothing. This was a ritual, even though they would never call it that out loud. Their roommate -- the one who had been in and out of four different jobs in two years -- left a cupcake on the counter with a single, slightly misspelled note. Logan ate it standing up and got to work 12 minutes early, because 12 is a good round number and because that's just the kind of person they were.

When their phone died at 9:30 p.m., instead of panicking, Logan found a charger -- eventually -- and spent the rest of the night looking at old pictures. Not the ones on Instagram. The ones from 2014, stored in a folder nobody opens anymore. They smiled at a few, laughed at one, and deleted exactly zero. Being a Scorpio, Logan thought, was less about the big, loud traits everyone talked about and more about the small, quiet ones: the things you save, the people you call first, the night you stay home and feel, for 15 very specific minutes, completely caught up.
