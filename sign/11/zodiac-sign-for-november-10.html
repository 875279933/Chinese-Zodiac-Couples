A Scorpio Story — Hannah, November 10

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Hannah Quinlan was 30, a pastry chef in Cincinnati, and someone who had, on at least one occasion, found out something about a friend without them knowing and then, quietly, done something about it. They were surprisingly patient -- private in a way that felt intentional, not shy. Hannah kept a stack of library books on the nightstand in a place nobody would look, because some things were just better that way.

On their birthday, Hannah got up early, made coffee, and stood at the window for about 10 minutes doing absolutely nothing. This was a ritual, even though they would never call it that out loud. Their roommate -- the one who had been in and out of four different jobs in two years -- left a cupcake on the counter with a single, slightly misspelled note. Hannah ate it standing up and got to work 12 minutes early, because 12 is a good round number and because that's just the kind of person they were.

They told themselves they would take the day off next year, which is what they told themselves every year, and which they had never once actually done. Hannah was, for better and for worse, the kind of person who showed up. Who checked the work email on a Sunday. Who answered the phone when their mom called, even when they were in the middle of something. It was, they thought, probably fine. They were 30. The Scorpio in them knew exactly how much of the plan to stick to and exactly how much to wing.
