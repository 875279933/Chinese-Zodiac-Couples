A Sagittarius Story — Gavin, November 27

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Gavin Yarborough was 46, a elementary school teacher in Raleigh, and the kind of person who would take a three-hour detour on a drive home just to see a roadside attraction they had already seen. They were secretly funny -- loud, honest, and the reason half their friend group had a story that started with 'Okay so Gavin said we should...'.

On their birthday, Gavin got up early, made coffee, and stood at the window for about 10 minutes doing absolutely nothing. This was a ritual, even though they would never call it that out loud. Their roommate -- the one who had been in and out of four different jobs in two years -- left a cupcake on the counter with a single, slightly misspelled note. Gavin ate it standing up and got to work 12 minutes early, because 12 is a good round number and because that's just the kind of person they were.

They told themselves they would take the day off next year, which is what they told themselves every year, and which they had never once actually done. Gavin was, for better and for worse, the kind of person who showed up. Who checked the work email on a Sunday. Who answered the phone when their mom called, even when they were in the middle of something. It was, they thought, probably fine. They were 46. The Sagittarius in them knew exactly how much of the plan to stick to and exactly how much to wing.
