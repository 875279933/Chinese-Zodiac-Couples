A Sagittarius Story — Delia, November 25

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Delia Calloway was 39, a freelance writer in Kansas City, and the kind of person who would take a three-hour detour on a drive home just to see a roadside attraction they had already seen. They were unreasonably loyal -- loud, honest, and the reason half their friend group had a story that started with 'Okay so Delia said we should...'.

Their closest friend had been trying to plan a surprise for three weeks, and Delia had been quietly pretending not to know about it for 20 days. They liked surprises in theory and hated them in practice, which is why they had, very gently, texted their friend the exact outfit they were planning to wear. Not to control things, they told themselves. Just to make the pictures turn out. When they walked in the door, everyone yelled. Delia yelled back, which is, in their opinion, how birthdays are supposed to go.

Before falling asleep, Delia wrote down one thing in a notebook they kept by the bed. They had been doing this, off and on, since they were 19. Sometimes the thing was big. Usually it was small. On November 25, it was small: a good sandwich, an honest compliment from a coworker, a text from an old friend they had been meaning to call. That was the thing about birthdays, Delia thought. You expect a big moment and you usually end up with three or four small ones that, eventually, turn out to be the point.
