A Scorpio Story — Natalie, November 11

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Natalie Lindquist was 33, a coffee roaster in Philadelphia, and someone who had, on at least one occasion, found out something about a friend without them knowing and then, quietly, done something about it. They were secretly funny -- private in a way that felt intentional, not shy. Natalie kept a stack of library books on the nightstand in a place nobody would look, because some things were just better that way.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Natalie spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on November 11, were not among them. This felt, in its own quiet way, like progress.

They told themselves they would take the day off next year, which is what they told themselves every year, and which they had never once actually done. Natalie was, for better and for worse, the kind of person who showed up. Who checked the work email on a Sunday. Who answered the phone when their mom called, even when they were in the middle of something. It was, they thought, probably fine. They were 33. The Scorpio in them knew exactly how much of the plan to stick to and exactly how much to wing.
