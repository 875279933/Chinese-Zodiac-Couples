A Sagittarius Story — Jake, November 22

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Jake Ramirez was 28, a bartender in Baltimore, and the kind of person who would take a three-hour detour on a drive home just to see a roadside attraction they had already seen. They were unexpectedly romantic -- loud, honest, and the reason half their friend group had a story that started with 'Okay so Jake said we should...'.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Jake spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on November 22, were not among them. This felt, in its own quiet way, like progress.

When their phone died at 9:30 p.m., instead of panicking, Jake found a charger -- eventually -- and spent the rest of the night looking at old pictures. Not the ones on Instagram. The ones from 2014, stored in a folder nobody opens anymore. They smiled at a few, laughed at one, and deleted exactly zero. Being a Sagittarius, Jake thought, was less about the big, loud traits everyone talked about and more about the small, quiet ones: the things you save, the people you call first, the night you stay home and feel, for 15 very specific minutes, completely caught up.
