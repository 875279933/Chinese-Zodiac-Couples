A Scorpio Story — Delia, November 8

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Delia Colombo was 45, a small-business owner in Savannah, and someone who had, on at least one occasion, found out something about a friend without them knowing and then, quietly, done something about it. They were a little too honest -- private in a way that felt intentional, not shy. Delia kept a kettle that is almost always on in a place nobody would look, because some things were just better that way.

Their closest friend had been trying to plan a surprise for three weeks, and Delia had been quietly pretending not to know about it for 20 days. They liked surprises in theory and hated them in practice, which is why they had, very gently, texted their friend the exact outfit they were planning to wear. Not to control things, they told themselves. Just to make the pictures turn out. When they walked in the door, everyone yelled. Delia yelled back, which is, in their opinion, how birthdays are supposed to go.

Before falling asleep, Delia wrote down one thing in a notebook they kept by the bed. They had been doing this, off and on, since they were 19. Sometimes the thing was big. Usually it was small. On November 8, it was small: a good sandwich, an honest compliment from a coworker, a text from an old friend they had been meaning to call. That was the thing about birthdays, Delia thought. You expect a big moment and you usually end up with three or four small ones that, eventually, turn out to be the point.
