Wesley Tao
Born November 13 · Scorpio

Wesley Tao was 35, a civil engineer in Denver, and someone who had, on at least one occasion, caught a structural mistake in a drawing that had been signed off by three other people. They were quietly certain about the things that mattered to them -- private in a way that felt intentional, not shy. Wesley Tao kept a drafting pencil they had used since college in a coffee can on their desk, because some things were just better that way.

Later that afternoon, Wesley Tao spent a Sunday on the couch with the dog, doing absolutely nothing, and not feeling guilty about it, which was the real achievement. Sometimes a person just needs the time. They had a running list of things they wanted to do before they hit the next decade: finish the deck, take the kids to Yellowstone, finally read the book on the nightstand. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Wesley Tao was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

When their phone died at 9:30 p.m., instead of panicking, Wesley Tao found a charger -- eventually -- and spent the rest of the night looking at old pictures. Not the ones on Instagram. The ones from 2014, stored in a folder nobody opens anymore. They smiled at a few, laughed at one, and deleted exactly zero. A Scorpio, Wesley thought, was someone who measured twice and, in life, also twice
