A Aquarius Story — Parker, February 7

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Parker Quinlan was 26, a veterinary technician in Seattle, and the friend who would recommend a podcast at 11 p.m. and then not text back for three days. They were surprisingly patient -- opinionated about things nobody else cared about and weirdly helpful when something was actually wrong. Their apartment had a magnet from every state they've visited and a stack of books about a subject they had recently become, temporarily, obsessed with.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Parker spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on February 7, were not among them. This felt, in its own quiet way, like progress.

They told themselves they would take the day off next year, which is what they told themselves every year, and which they had never once actually done. Parker was, for better and for worse, the kind of person who showed up. Who checked the work email on a Sunday. Who answered the phone when their mom called, even when they were in the middle of something. It was, they thought, probably fine. They were 26. The Aquarius in them knew exactly how much of the plan to stick to and exactly how much to wing.
