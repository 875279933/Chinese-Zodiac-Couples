A Aquarius Story — Oscar, February 14

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Oscar Merriman was 29, a veterinary technician in San Diego, and the friend who would recommend a podcast at 11 p.m. and then not text back for three days. They were a little too honest -- opinionated about things nobody else cared about and weirdly helpful when something was actually wrong. Their apartment had a record player that gets used more than any appliance and a stack of books about a subject they had recently become, temporarily, obsessed with.

Later that afternoon, Oscar sat in their car in a grocery store parking lot for 17 minutes and thought about nothing in particular. Sometimes a person just needs 17 minutes. They had a running list of things they wanted to do before they were 39: learn to bake sourdough, visit the coast, find that one jacket they lost in 2017, call their dad more. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Oscar was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

By the time Oscar got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47, which is a fine time for an adult to go to bed and nobody gets to say otherwise. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, is a different sign entirely.
