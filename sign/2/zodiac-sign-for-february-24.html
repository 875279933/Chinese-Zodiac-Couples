A Pisces Story — Hadley, February 24

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Hadley Yarborough was 43, a dog walker in Richmond, and the kind of person who would cry at a dog food commercial and then pretend they didn't. They were secretly funny -- the friend who always had a playlist for the mood you didn't know you were in. Hadley kept a folded blanket on the back of the couch because their aunt had given it to them when they were 12, and that was, honestly, reason enough.

Later that afternoon, Hadley sat in their car in a grocery store parking lot for 17 minutes and thought about nothing in particular. Sometimes a person just needs 17 minutes. They had a running list of things they wanted to do before they were 53: learn to bake sourdough, visit the coast, find that one jacket they lost in 2017, call their dad more. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Hadley was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

Before falling asleep, Hadley wrote down one thing in a notebook they kept by the bed. They had been doing this, off and on, since they were 19. Sometimes the thing was big. Usually it was small. On February 24, it was small: a good sandwich, an honest compliment from a coworker, a text from an old friend they had been meaning to call. That was the thing about birthdays, Hadley thought. You expect a big moment and you usually end up with three or four small ones that, eventually, turn out to be the point.
