A Gemini Story — Luna, June 10

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Luna Fitzgerald was 31, worked as a small-business owner in San Diego, and had, in the last 12 months alone, taken up pottery, birdwatching, competitive trivia, and a short-lived attempt to learn Portuguese. None of them stuck. Luna was secretly funny, and their texts came in paragraphs at 9 a.m. and silence for two days after. They didn't mean anything by it. They were just off somewhere else.

A stranger at the coffee shop told them to 'have a good one,' and Luna spent the next hour wondering why that phrase was so much better than most things people actually say. They worked through lunch, walked around the block afterward, and had a very honest conversation with themselves about whether they could pull off wearing a hat. They decided, eventually, that some people are just hat people and that they, on June 10, were not among them. This felt, in its own quiet way, like progress.

By the time Luna got home that evening, their phone had 11 texts, one missed call from their sister, and a spam email they opened by accident. They ate leftovers for dinner, watched one episode of something they had already seen, and went to bed at 10:47, which is a fine time for an adult to go to bed and nobody gets to say otherwise. Before they turned the light off, they thought about the year ahead: a trip they might take, a conversation they had been putting off, a playlist they still hadn't finished. Then they turned the light off. Tomorrow, after all, is a different sign entirely.
