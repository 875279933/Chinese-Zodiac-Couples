A Gemini Story — Brynn, June 16

A short read about someone born on this exact day

Brynn Fitzgerald was 31, worked as a chef in Tucson, and had, in the last 12 months alone, taken up pottery, birdwatching, competitive trivia, and a short-lived attempt to learn Portuguese. None of them stuck. Brynn was surprisingly patient, and their texts came in paragraphs at 9 a.m. and silence for two days after. They didn't mean anything by it. They were just off somewhere else.

Later that afternoon, Brynn sat in their car in a grocery store parking lot for 17 minutes and thought about nothing in particular. Sometimes a person just needs 17 minutes. They had a running list of things they wanted to do before they were 41: learn to bake sourdough, visit the coast, find that one jacket they lost in 2017, call their dad more. The list was mostly aspirational and, as far as Brynn was concerned, that was the correct way for a list to be.

Before falling asleep, Brynn wrote down one thing in a notebook they kept by the bed. They had been doing this, off and on, since they were 19. Sometimes the thing was big. Usually it was small. On June 16, it was small: a good sandwich, an honest compliment from a coworker, a text from an old friend they had been meaning to call. That was the thing about birthdays, Brynn thought. You expect a big moment and you usually end up with three or four small ones that, eventually, turn out to be the point.
