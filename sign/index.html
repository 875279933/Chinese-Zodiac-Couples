What Is a Zodiac Sign?

How the sun's position on your birth date sets your zodiac sign, and why two signs share a single month.

Your zodiac sign is set by where the sun sat in the sky on the day you were born. The year is divided into twelve equal segments, and each one is named after a constellation. People have tracked these segments for thousands of years to look for patterns in personality, behavior, and relationships.

Each month actually spans two zodiac signs. The transition usually lands between the 19th and 23rd of the month. If you were born in that window, you may notice you carry traits from both adjacent signs. The standard ranges on this page cover the vast majority of birth dates, and the zodiac sign you find here will match the standard Western reference.

Use the month-by-month list below to identify your zodiac sign, then move on to a full zodiac sign personality profile or check your compatibility with other zodiac signs for a deeper read. The sign is the starting label. The trait, relationship, and life-pattern reads are where the zodiac sign system gets useful.

How the Zodiac Sign Calendar Works

Why the zodiac sign date ranges exist, where the 12 signs come from, and what to do with your sign once you have it. A short reference on the Western calendar and the next steps for a deeper read.

The Western zodiac calendar is built on a 12-month cycle. As Earth orbits the sun, the sun appears to pass in front of twelve constellations in sequence. The constellation behind the sun on your birth date is your zodiac sign.

The date ranges shift slightly from year to year because the constellations are fixed but the calendar is not. If you were born within a day or two of a boundary, the sign technically depends on the exact time, year, and your location. For most purposes, the standard ranges on this page give the right answer.

Once you know your sign, the next step is usually a full zodiac sign personality profile or a compatibility read against another sign. Both are quick ways to turn a birth date into a working picture of how a person tends to operate in relationships, work, and daily life. The zodiac sign lookup here is the entry point. The personality and compatibility pages are where the read gets specific.

Zodiac Sign Lookup — Frequently Asked Questions

Zodiac sign lookup covers the standard Western 12-sign system. The date ranges used here match the most common reference, and the sign each date points to is the one used across the personality and compatibility pages on this site. For readers working with a different zodiac sign system, the same birth date may map to a different label.

How do I find my zodiac sign by date?

Your zodiac sign is set by the sun's position on your birth date. Each month covers two signs, with the transition landing between the 19th and 23rd. Use the month sections on this page to match your birthday to its zodiac sign.

What are the 12 zodiac signs and their dates?

The 12 zodiac signs are: Aries (Mar 21 - Apr 19), Taurus (Apr 20 - May 20), Gemini (May 21 - Jun 20), Cancer (Jun 21 - Jul 22), Leo (Jul 23 - Aug 22), Virgo (Aug 23 - Sep 22), Libra (Sep 23 - Oct 22), Scorpio (Oct 23 - Nov 21), Sagittarius (Nov 22 - Dec 21), Capricorn (Dec 22 - Jan 19), Aquarius (Jan 20 - Feb 18), Pisces (Feb 19 - Mar 20).

What if my birthday falls on a zodiac cusp?

If your birthday lands on a transition date between two signs, you may show traits from both. The exact cutoff shifts slightly by year, so cross-check your specific birth date against the ranges listed on this page.

Can my zodiac sign change?

No. Your zodiac sign is fixed by your birth date. People born on a cusp often relate to qualities of the adjacent sign, but the official sign assignment does not change.
