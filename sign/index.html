A quick guide to understanding how your birth date determines your zodiac sign and what it says about you.

Your zodiac sign comes down to where the sun was sitting on the day you were born. The zodiac calendar breaks the year into twelve chunks, each ruled by a different sign. People have been using this system for centuries to get a handle on personality, relationships, and life patterns.

How the Zodiac Sign Calendar Works

Understanding the zodiac date ranges and why your sign matters.

The zodiac calendar is based on the sun's position throughout the year. As Earth orbits the sun, it passes through twelve different constellations — whichever constellation the sun was in when you were born becomes your zodiac sign.

Each sign covers roughly a month, but the exact dates shift slightly from year to year. That's why if you were born right on the edge of two signs, it's worth checking a specific zodiac calendar for your birth year to be sure.

Knowing your zodiac sign is a starting point. A lot of people go on to explore their full zodiac personality profile or check their compatibility with other signs to get a deeper understanding of themselves and their relationships.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find my zodiac sign by date?

Your zodiac sign is determined by your birth date. Each month covers two signs, with the transition typically occurring between the 19th and 23rd. Check the month sections on this page to find your exact sign based on your birthday.

What are the 12 zodiac signs and their dates?

The 12 zodiac signs are: Aries (Mar 21 - Apr 19), Taurus (Apr 20 - May 20), Gemini (May 21 - Jun 20), Cancer (Jun 21 - Jul 22), Leo (Jul 23 - Aug 22), Virgo (Aug 23 - Sep 22), Libra (Sep 23 - Oct 22), Scorpio (Oct 23 - Nov 21), Sagittarius (Nov 22 - Dec 21), Capricorn (Dec 22 - Jan 19), Aquarius (Jan 20 - Feb 18), Pisces (Feb 19 - Mar 20).

What if my birthday falls on a zodiac cusp?

If your birthday falls on a transition date between two signs, you may exhibit traits from both signs. The exact transition can vary slightly by year, so check your specific birth date against the ranges provided on this page.

Can my zodiac sign change?

No, your zodiac sign is fixed based on your birth date and doesn't change. However, if you were born near a cusp date, you might relate to qualities of both neighboring signs throughout your life.
