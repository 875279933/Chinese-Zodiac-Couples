Cookie Policy

What cookies we use and how you can manage them.

Last updated: May 2026

ChineseZodiacCouples.com uses cookies to improve your experience on our Site. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use, and how you can control them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, understand how you use the site, and in some cases, deliver relevant content or advertising.

2. Cookies We Use

2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the basic functionality of the Site. They enable core features such as page navigation and cannot be disabled without affecting site performance.

2.2 Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our Site. These cookies collect anonymized data such as:

  • Number of visitors and page views
  • Time spent on the Site and specific pages
  • Referral sources (how you found us)
  • Device and browser information

This data helps us improve our content and user experience. Google Analytics cookies include _ga, _gid, and _gat. For more details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

2.3 Advertising Cookies (if applicable)

We may use Google AdSense to display ads on our Site. These services may set cookies to serve ads based on your browsing patterns across the web. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or About Ads.

2.4 Specific Cookies We Use

Below is a list of the main cookies set by ChineseZodiacCouples.com and the third-party services we use. This list is reviewed regularly and updated as needed.

Google Analytics Cookies

  • _ga — Used to distinguish unique users. Expires after 2 years.
  • _ga_<container-id> — Used to persist session state. Expires after 2 years.
  • _gid — Used to distinguish users. Expires after 24 hours.
  • _gat — Used to throttle request rate. Expires after 1 minute.

Google AdSense Cookies (When Ads Are Shown)

  • IDE — Used by Google DoubleClick to register and report ad views. Expires after 13 months in most regions (up to 24 months in some jurisdictions).
  • NID — Contains a unique ID Google uses to remember preferences and other information. Expires after 6 months.
  • ANID — Used for advertising serving and measurement. Expires after 13 months.
  • DSID — Used to identify a signed-in user on non-Google sites. Expires after 2 weeks.
  • 1P_JAR — Used to gather website statistics. Expires after 1 week.
  • CONSENT — Stores cookie consent choices. Expires after 20 years.

Functional Cookies

  • Session cookies — Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser. Used to remember your preferences (such as selected zodiac sign) during a single visit.

2.5 Cookie Lifespan Summary

  • Short-term (session): expire when you close your browser
  • Medium-term (1 day – 13 months): most analytics and advertising cookies
  • Long-term (13–24 months): persistent identifiers for returning users

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services we use, such as Google Analytics and advertising partners. We do not control these cookies and recommend reviewing the respective privacy policies of those providers:

  • Google Cookie Usage
  • Google Privacy Policy

4. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Block all cookies or only third-party cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Set preferences for specific websites

Instructions vary by browser. Check your browser's help section for guidance. Note that blocking cookies may affect your experience on our Site.

5. Cookie Consent

By continuing to use our Site, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy. If you do not wish to accept cookies, you can adjust your browser settings or leave the Site.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please contact us.
