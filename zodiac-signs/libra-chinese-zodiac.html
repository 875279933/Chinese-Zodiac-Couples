How to Determine Your Chinese Zodiac Sign

  1. Find your birth year in the selector above
  2. The Chinese zodiac follows a 12-year cycle
  3. Each year is associated with one of 12 animals: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, or Pig
  4. Your zodiac sign is determined by your birth year, not month
