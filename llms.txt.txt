# Chinese Zodiac Couples - Love Compatibility Guide

> Chinese Zodiac Couples is a specialized guide that explores love compatibility through the lens of the Chinese zodiac, providing a free interactive tool and in-depth articles on the underlying philosophy.

This website offers a practical resource for understanding relationship dynamics from a traditional Chinese zodiac perspective. The core philosophy is based on Yin and Yang pairing, Animal Sign Chemistry, the Five Elements, the Three Harmony Triads, and the Six Harmony Pairs.

The main interactive feature is a compatibility checker that generates a score and detailed analysis across dimensions like Emotional, Communication, Values, and Intimacy when users input birth dates or select signs.

For deeper reading, detailed sign profiles and year-by-year perspectives are available. A comprehensive FAQ section answers common questions about the system and its interpretation.
