About Chinese Zodiac Personalities

The Chinese zodiac, or shengxiao, is more than just a fun cultural tradition—it offers profound insights into personality traits based on your birth year. Each of the 12 animal signs comes with unique characteristics, strengths, and weaknesses that have been studied for thousands of years.

Whether you're curious about your own traits, want to understand your friends and family better, or simply love learning about ancient cultures, this guide will take you on a fascinating journey through all 12 zodiac signs. Click on any sign to dive deeper into its personality!
