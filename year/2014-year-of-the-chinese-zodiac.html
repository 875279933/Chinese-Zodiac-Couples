2014 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2014 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Jia Wu

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2014 was the year of Jia Wu. "Jia" stands for Wood in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Wu" maps to the Horse in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2014 lunar year kicked off on Jan 31, 2014 and wrapped up on Feb 18, 2015. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Wood Horse. Born earlier in Jan 2014 -- before the 31 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yang Wood Energy

Jia Wood is Yang Wood -- the tall, sturdy kind that stretches upward and outward. It gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and a steady drive that doesn't quit, with a generous, collaborative streak that other Horse years sometimes lack.

Big Events That Shaped 2014

2014 was the year of a viral ice bucket, a missing plane, and a Crimea crisis. Here are five events that stood out from the year.

March 2014

Russia Annexes Crimea

Vladimir Putin signed treaties to absorb Crimea 18 days after unmarked Russian troops took control of the peninsula. The move triggered the worst East-West tensions since the Cold War.

March 8, 2014

MH370 Disappears

Malaysia Airlines Flight 370 vanished with 239 people on board en route from Kuala Lumpur to Beijing. The search became the most expensive in aviation history at $150 million.

Summer 2014

Ice Bucket Challenge Goes Viral

The fundraiser hit Facebook feeds everywhere in July and August, raising $115 million for the ALS Association in 8 weeks. Pete Frates, who inspired the campaign, died in 2019.

March 2014

Ebola Outbreak

The West African epidemic killed 11,325 people across Guinea, Liberia, and Sierra Leone. The US deployed 2,800 troops to Liberia and 11 patients were treated on US soil.

Sept 18, 2014

Scottish Independence Vote

Scotland voted 55-45 to stay in the UK in a turnout of 84.6%, the highest for any election in the UK since 1959. Cameron promised more devolution hours after the result.
