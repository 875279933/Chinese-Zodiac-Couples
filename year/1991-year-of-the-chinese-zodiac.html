1991 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 1991 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Xin Wei

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1991 was the year of Xin Wei. "Xin" stands for Metal in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Wei" maps to the Goat in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 1991 lunar year kicked off on Feb 15, 1991 and wrapped up on Feb 3, 1992. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Metal Goat. But if you were born earlier in Feb 1991 -- before the 15 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases. Standard year runs about 354 days; a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years.

Yin Metal Energy

Xin Metal is Yin Metal -- refined, soft-edged, and precise. It gives people born under its influence a quiet sharpness and a taste for detail over flash.
