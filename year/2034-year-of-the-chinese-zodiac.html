2034 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2034 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Jia Yin

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2034 is the year of Jia Yin. "Jia" stands for Wood in the Heavenly Stems, and "Yin" maps to the Tiger in the Earthly Branches. This combo only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2034 lunar year kicks off on Feb 18, 2034 and wraps up on Feb 7, 2035. If your birthday lands in that window, you're a Wood Tiger. Born earlier in Feb 2034 -- before the 18th -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yang Wood Energy

Jia Wood is Yang Wood -- tall, growing, and outward. It gives people born under its influence ambition and a drive to build. Wood fuels the Tiger's courage with creative vision.
