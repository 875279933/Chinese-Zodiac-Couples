2012 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2012 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Ren Chen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2012 was the year of Ren Chen. "Ren" represents Yang Water in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Chen" maps to the Dragon in the Earthly Branches. Ren Chen shows up only once every 60 years, giving the 2012 year of the Chinese zodiac a signature that's hard to mistake for any other year in the cycle.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2012 lunar year opened on Jan 23, 2012 and closed on Feb 9, 2013. Anyone born between those two dates carries the Water Dragon sign. Born earlier in January 2012 -- before the 23rd -- and you'd fall under the previous sign.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar follows the moon's phases, with each new month starting on a new moon. A standard year runs about 354 days, and a leap month is added every few years to keep the calendar aligned with the solar cycle.

Yang Water Energy

Ren Water is Yang Water -- active, outward, and persistent. It pairs well with the Dragon's natural fire, giving the 2012 year of the Chinese zodiac a kind of ambitious reach that rarely runs out of steam.

Big Events That Shaped 2012

2012 had a presidential re-election, a superstorm, and a new rover on Mars. Here are five events that made the year stick.

Oct 29, 2012

Hurricane Sandy Hits

Sandy made landfall in New Jersey with 80 mph winds and a 14-foot storm surge. Damage estimates hit $70.2 billion, making it the fourth-costliest US hurricane on record. 8 million lost power.

Nov 6, 2012

Obama Re-elected

Barack Obama defeated Mitt Romney with 332 electoral votes and 51.1% of the popular vote. Exit polls showed women voters went 55-44 for Obama, the gender gap that decided the race.

Aug 6, 2012

Curiosity Lands on Mars

NASA's 1-ton rover survived the seven minutes of terror and touched down in Gale Crater. The sky crane maneuver had never been tried before and the celebration at JPL went viral.

Dec 14, 2012

Sandy Hook Shooting

20 children and 6 staff were killed at Sandy Hook Elementary in Newtown, Connecticut. The tragedy reignited the national gun debate and led to Connecticut's sweeping 2013 gun control law.

July 27, 2012

London Summer Olympics Open

The first Olympics after a host city had already hosted twice. The US won 46 gold medals and Michael Phelps became the most decorated Olympian of all time with 22 medals.
