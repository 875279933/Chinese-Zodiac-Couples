2036 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2036 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Bing Chen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2036 is the year of Bing Chen. "Bing" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems, and "Chen" maps to the Dragon in the Earthly Branches. This combo only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2036 lunar year kicks off on Jan 27, 2036 and wraps up on Feb 14, 2037. If your birthday lands in that window, you're a Fire Dragon. Born earlier in Jan 2036 -- before the 27th -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yang Fire Energy

Bing Fire is Yang Fire -- the sun at midday. It gives people born under its influence a bright, commanding presence. Fire fuels the Dragon's charisma with steady, outward heat.
