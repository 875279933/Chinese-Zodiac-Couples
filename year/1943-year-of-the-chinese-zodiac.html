1943 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 1943 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Gui Wei

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1943 was the year of Gui Wei. "Gui" stands for Water in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Wei" maps to the Goat in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 1943

The 1943 lunar year kicked off on Feb 5, 1943 and wrapped up on Jan 24, 1944. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Water Goat. But if you were born earlier in Feb 1943 -- before the 5 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Why Yin Water Matters

It gives people born under its influence a depth of feeling and a quiet strength that runs deeper than it appears. Water brings adaptability and intuition. People born in this year are more flexible and perceptive than others of their sign, with a natural feel for people and situations.

1943 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 1943 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

1943 on the Western Calendar

1943 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Water Goat's adaptable, flexible energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 1943 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 1943, the Water Goat period ran from Feb 5, 1943 through Jan 24, 1944.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Feb 5 1943 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Goat. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.

Why This Distinction Matters

If you're looking into family history or just trying to understand what the 1943 year of the Chinese zodiac really means, getting the calendar right is half the battle. The Chinese zodiac gives you a completely different way of reading personality and life path compared to Western astrology.

Famous People Born in 1943

1943 produced one of the deepest benches of talent of any year on this list. Here are six Water Goats whose work still shows up everywhere.

Mick Jagger

Born July 26 in Dartford, England. Frontman of the Rolling Stones, a band that has been selling out arenas for sixty years. "Satisfaction," "Paint It Black," "Sympathy for the Devil." Jagger's energy on stage at 80 is a story on its own.

Keith Richards

Born December 18 in Dartford, England. The other Dartford kid. The guitar tone Richards worked out on "Satisfaction" basically defined rock rhythm for the next half century. He has also written one of the better rock memoirs ever published.

Robert De Niro

Born August 17 in New York City. Taxi Driver, The Godfather Part II, Raging Bull, Goodfellas. Two Academy Awards, six nominations, and a filmography that other actors measure their careers against.

Joni Mitchell

Born November 7 in Fort Macleod, Canada. "Big Yellow Taxi," "A Case of You," "River." Mitchell wrote songs nobody else could have written and sang them in tunings nobody else could have figured out. She changed what folk and pop could sound like at the same time.

Joe Namath

Born May 31 in Beaver Falls, Pennsylvania. Broadway Joe. Guaranteed victory in Super Bowl III and then delivered it. Namath was the first quarterback to throw for 4,000 yards in a season and probably the first athlete to turn celebrity into a full-time profession.

Billie Jean King

Born November 22 in Long Beach, California. 39 Grand Slam titles. The Battle of the Sexes match in 1973. King spent her career fighting for equal pay in tennis, then went on to run the whole Women's Tennis Association.
