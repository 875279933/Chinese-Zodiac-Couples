2042 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different rules. Here's how that affects the 2042 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Ren Xu

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2042 is the year of Ren Xu. "Ren" stands for Water in the Heavenly Stems, and "Xu" maps to the Dog in the Earthly Branches. This combo only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 2042

The 2042 lunar year kicks off on Jan 22, 2042 and wraps up on Feb 9, 2043. If your birthday lands in that window, you're a Water Dog. Born earlier in January 2042 —before the 22 —and you're actually the previous sign, not a Dog.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To stay in step with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years —which is why Chinese New Year moves around.

Why Yang Water Matters

The Ren Water in 2042 is Yang Water —think ocean swells, big rivers, heavy rain. It's more outward and active than Yin Water. Yang Water Dogs tend to ride the ups and downs without losing their cool.
