2042 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2042 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Ren Xu

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2042 is the year of Ren Xu. "Ren" stands for Water in the Heavenly Stems, and "Xu" maps to the Dog in the Earthly Branches. This combo only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2042 lunar year kicks off on Feb 18, 2042 and wraps up on Feb 7, 2043. If your birthday lands in that window, you're a Water Dog. Born earlier in Feb 2042 -- before the 18th -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yang Water Energy

Ren Water is Yang Water -- the sea at high tide, vast and outward. It gives people born under its influence a calm, generous nature. Water softens the Dog's guarded heart with quiet openness.
