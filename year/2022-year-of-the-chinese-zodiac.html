2022 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2022 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Ren Yin

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2022 was the year of Ren Yin. "Ren" stands for Yang Water in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Yin" maps to the Tiger in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2022 lunar year kicked off on Feb 1, 2022 and wrapped up on Jan 21, 2023. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Water Tiger. Born earlier in Feb 2022 -- before the 1 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yang Water Energy

Ren Water is Yang Water -- flowing, active, and outward. It gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and the ability to adapt on the fly. Water softens the Tiger's boldness into something more flexible.

Big Events That Shaped 2022

2022 had a war break out in Europe, the Queen die after 70 years, and the Supreme Court change America. Here are the five moments people will remember.

Feb 24, 2022

Russia Invades Ukraine

Putin launched the biggest European war since 1945 with 150,000 troops crossing the border. Ukraine's counteroffensive in September retook 12,000 square kilometers in 10 days. By year's end, 7.8 million Ukrainians had fled the country.

Sept 8, 2022

Queen Elizabeth II Dies

Britain's longest-reigning monarch died at Balmoral Castle in Scotland at age 96. She had been on the throne for 70 years and 214 days. King Charles III was proclaimed the next day at St James's Palace.

June 24, 2022

Roe v. Wade Overturned

The Supreme Court ruled 6-3 in Dobbs v. Jackson to overturn Roe v. Wade, ending 49 years of federal abortion rights. Within 100 days, 14 states had banned abortion entirely. It was the first major constitutional right removed in US history.

March 27, 2022

Will Smith Slaps Chris Rock at Oscars

Smith walked on stage and hit Rock after a joke about Jada Pinkett Smith's hair. Rock declined to press charges. The Academy banned Smith for 10 years on April 8 and he resigned from the Academy two days later.

Nov 20 - Dec 18, 2022

Qatar Hosts the World Cup

The first World Cup in an Arab country and the first held in winter. Argentina beat France in the final 3-3 (4-2 on penalties) in one of the greatest finals ever played. The US men's team made the round of 16 and lost 3-1 to the Netherlands.
