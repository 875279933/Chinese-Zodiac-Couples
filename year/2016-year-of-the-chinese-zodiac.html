2016 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 2016 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Bing Shen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2016 was the year of Bing Shen. "Bing" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Shen" maps to the Monkey in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 2016

The 2016 lunar year kicked off on Feb 8, 2016 and wrapped up on Jan 27, 2017. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Fire Monkey. But if you were born earlier in Feb 2016 -- before the 8 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

2016 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 2016 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

2016 on the Western Calendar

2016 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Fire Monkey's passionate, dynamic energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 2016 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 2016, the Fire Monkey period ran from Feb 8, 2016 through Jan 27, 2017.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Feb 8 2016 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Monkey. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.

Why This Distinction Matters

If you're looking into family history or just trying to understand what the 2016 year of the Chinese zodiac really means, getting the calendar right is half the battle. The Chinese zodiac gives you a completely different way of reading personality and life path compared to Western astrology.

Big Events That Shaped 2016

2016 had one of the biggest political upsets in US history, the Cubs' first title in 108 years, and a devastating nightclub shooting. Here are five things that mattered.

Nov 8, 2016

Trump Elected 45th President

Donald Trump won 304 electoral votes despite losing the popular vote by 2.9 million. The win came after the FBI reopened the Clinton email probe 11 days before election day.

June 23, 2016

Brexit Vote

The UK voted 52-48 to leave the European Union on a 72.2% turnout. David Cameron resigned the next morning and Theresa May took over as PM. Article 50 was triggered on March 29, 2017.

Nov 2, 2016

Cubs Win World Series

Chicago beat Cleveland 8-7 in 10 innings in Game 7, ending a 108-year drought. The final out drew an estimated 40 million viewers and the team's parade drew 5 million people.

June 12, 2016

Pulse Nightclub Shooting

A gunman killed 49 people and injured 53 at an Orlando LGBTQ+ nightclub. It was the deadliest mass shooting in US history at the time, prompting changes to Florida's gun laws.

Aug 5-21, 2016

Rio Summer Olympics

The first Olympics held in South America. The US won 46 gold medals, and the Russian doping scandal led to 271 athletes being banned and the team's first-ever ban.
