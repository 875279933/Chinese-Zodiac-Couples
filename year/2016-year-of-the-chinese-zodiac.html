2016 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2016 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Bing Shen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2016 was the year of Bing Shen. "Bing" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Shen" maps to the Monkey in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2016 lunar year kicked off on Feb 8, 2016 and wrapped up on Jan 27, 2017. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Fire Monkey. Born earlier in Feb 2016 -- before the 8 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yang Fire Energy

Bing Fire is Yang Fire -- the blazing kind, like the midday sun. It gives people born under its influence a fierce, visible energy that lights up a room and pushes everything around it forward.

Big Events That Shaped 2016

2016 had one of the biggest political upsets in US history, the Cubs' first title in 108 years, and a devastating nightclub shooting. Here are five things that mattered.

Nov 8, 2016

Trump Elected 45th President

Donald Trump won 304 electoral votes despite losing the popular vote by 2.9 million. The win came after the FBI reopened the Clinton email probe 11 days before election day.

June 23, 2016

Brexit Vote

The UK voted 52-48 to leave the European Union on a 72.2% turnout. David Cameron resigned the next morning and Theresa May took over as PM. Article 50 was triggered on March 29, 2017.

Nov 2, 2016

Cubs Win World Series

Chicago beat Cleveland 8-7 in 10 innings in Game 7, ending a 108-year drought. The final out drew an estimated 40 million viewers and the team's parade drew 5 million people.

June 12, 2016

Pulse Nightclub Shooting

A gunman killed 49 people and injured 53 at an Orlando LGBTQ+ nightclub. It was the deadliest mass shooting in US history at the time, prompting changes to Florida's gun laws.

Aug 5-21, 2016

Rio Summer Olympics

The first Olympics held in South America. The US won 46 gold medals, and the Russian doping scandal led to 271 athletes being banned and the team's first-ever ban.
