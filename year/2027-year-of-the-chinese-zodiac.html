2027 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 2027 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Ding Wei

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2027 was the year of Ding Wei. "Ding" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Wei" maps to the Goat in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 2027

The 2027 lunar year kicked off on Feb 6, 2027 and wrapped up on Jan 25, 2028. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Fire Goat. But if you were born earlier in Feb 2027 -- before the 6 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

2027 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 2027 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

2027 on the Western Calendar

2027 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Fire Goat's passionate, dynamic energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 2027 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 2027, the Fire Goat period ran from Feb 6, 2027 through Jan 25, 2028.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Feb 6 2027 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Goat. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.
