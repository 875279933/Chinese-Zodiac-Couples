2027 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2027 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Ding Wei

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2027 was the year of Ding Wei. "Ding" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Wei" maps to the Goat in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2027 lunar year kicked off on Feb 6, 2027 and wrapped up on Jan 25, 2028. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Fire Goat. Born earlier in Feb 2027 -- before the 6 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.
