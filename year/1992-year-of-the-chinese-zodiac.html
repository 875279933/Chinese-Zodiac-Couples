1992 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 1992 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Ren Shen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1992 was the year of Ren Shen. "Ren" stands for Water in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Shen" maps to the Monkey in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 1992 lunar year kicked off on Feb 4, 1992 and wrapped up on Jan 22, 1993. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Water Monkey. But if you were born earlier in Feb 1992 -- before the 4 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases. Standard year runs about 354 days; a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years.

Yang Water Energy

Ren Water is Yang Water -- the ocean, the river, the moving current. It gives people born under its influence a wide emotional range and an instinct for going with the flow.

Famous People Born in 1992

1992 turned out a memorable group. Here are six of the most recognizable names from that year.

Selena Gomez

Born July 22 in Grand Prairie, Texas. Wizards of Waverly Place for four seasons, then Revival in 2015, then Only Murders in the Building. Founded Rare Beauty in 2020, which crossed $1 billion in sales by 2024.

Cardi B

Born October 11 in the Bronx, New York. Bodak Yellow spent three weeks at #1 in 2017, making her the first solo female rapper to reach the top of the Hot 100 since Lauryn Hill. Six Billboard Music Awards and a Grammy for Invasion of Privacy.

Nicki Minaj

Born December 8 in Saint James, Trinidad. Pink Friday came out in 2010 and went platinum within a month. The Pinkprint Tour is the highest-grossing female rap tour in history, and she's released four more studio albums since.

Dua Lipa

Born August 22 in London. Don't Start Now spent 18 weeks in the Hot 100 top 10, then Future Nostalgia won six Brit Awards in 2021. Has been a steady concert draw and the 2024 Glastonbury Pyramid Stage headliner.

Zayn Malik

Born January 12 in Bradford, England. Left One Direction in 2015, then Pillowtalk spent two weeks at #1. Released four solo albums, with Room Under the Stairs in 2024 marking his most personal record yet.

Jennie Kim

Born January 16 in Cheongju, South Korea. Blackpink is the most-subscribed K-pop girl group on YouTube and one of the most-streamed acts in the world. Her solo Ruby dropped in 2025 and debuted at #1 on the Circle Albums chart.
