1961 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 1961 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Xin Chou

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1961 was the year of Xin Chou. "Xin" stands for Metal in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Chou" maps to the Ox in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 1961

The 1961 lunar year kicked off on Feb 15, 1961 and wrapped up on Feb 4, 1962. If your birthday falls in that window, you're an Metal Ox. But if you were born earlier in Feb 1961 -- before the 15 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Why Yin Metal Matters

It gives people born under its influence a depth of feeling and a quiet strength that runs deeper than it appears. Metal adds resolve and discipline. People born in this year are tougher and more focused than others of their sign, with an inner strength that keeps them on track no matter what.

1961 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 1961 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

1961 on the Western Calendar

1961 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Metal Ox's determined, resolute energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 1961 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 1961, the Metal Ox period ran from Feb 15, 1961 through Feb 4, 1962.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Feb 15 1961 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not an Ox. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.
