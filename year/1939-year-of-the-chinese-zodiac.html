1939 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar do not follow the same rules. Here is how that affects the 1939 year of the Chinese zodiac.

Year of Ji Mao

"Ji" stands for Yin Earth, "Mao" maps to the Rabbit. The pairing recurs once every 60 years, next in 1999.

Lunar Dates

Feb 19, 1939 through Feb 7, 1940. A January 1939 birthday falls under the 1938 year of the Chinese zodiac, which is the Earth Tiger.

How It Works

Months start on the new moon, and a leap month is added every few years. That is why Chinese New Year moves on the Western calendar.
