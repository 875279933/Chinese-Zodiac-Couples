1975 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 1975 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Yi Mao

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1975 was the year of Yi Mao. "Yi" stands for Wood in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Mao" maps to the Rabbit in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 1975

The 1975 lunar year kicked off on Feb 11, 1975 and wrapped up on Jan 30, 1976. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Wood Rabbit. But if you were born earlier in Feb 1975 -- before the 11 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Why Yin Wood Matters

The Yi Wood in the 1975 year of the Chinese zodiac is Yin Wood -- Yin energy is receptive, inward, and intuitive. It gives people born under its influence a depth of feeling and a quiet strength that runs deeper than it appears. Wood adds growth and cooperation. People born in this year tend to be more collaborative and open-minded than others of their sign, always looking for ways to expand and improve.

1975 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 1975 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

1975 on the Western Calendar

1975 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Wood Rabbit's growth-oriented, progressive energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 1975 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 1975, the Wood Rabbit period ran from Feb 11, 1975 through Jan 30, 1976.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Feb 11 1975 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Rabbit. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.

Why This Distinction Matters

If you're looking into family history or just trying to understand what the 1975 year of the Chinese zodiac really means, getting the calendar right is half the battle. The Chinese zodiac gives you a completely different way of reading personality and life path compared to Western astrology.
