1971 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 1971 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Xin Hai

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1971 was the year of Xin Hai. "Xin" stands for Metal in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Hai" maps to the Pig in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 1971

The 1971 lunar year kicked off on Jan 27, 1971 and wrapped up on Feb 14, 1972. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Metal Pig. But if you were born earlier in Jan 1971 -- before the 27 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Why Yin Metal Matters

The Xin Metal in the 1971 year of the Chinese zodiac is Yin Metal -- Yin energy is receptive, inward, and intuitive. It gives people born under its influence a depth of feeling and a quiet strength that runs deeper than it appears. Metal adds resolve and discipline. People born in this year are tougher and more focused than others of their sign, with an inner strength that keeps them on track no matter what.

1971 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 1971 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

1971 on the Western Calendar

1971 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Metal Pig's determined, resolute energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 1971 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 1971, the Metal Pig period ran from Jan 27, 1971 through Feb 14, 1972.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Jan 27 1971 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Pig. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.

Why This Distinction Matters
