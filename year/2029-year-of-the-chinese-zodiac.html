2029 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2029 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Ji You

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2029 was the year of Ji You. "Ji" stands for Earth in the Heavenly Stems system, while "You" maps to the Rooster in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2029 lunar year kicked off on Feb 13, 2029 and wrapped up on Feb 2, 2030. If your birthday falls in that window, you're an Earth Rooster. Born earlier in Feb 2029 -- before the 13 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yin Earth Energy

Ji Earth is Yin Earth -- receptive, inward, and grounding. It gives people born under its influence a quiet steadiness and a practical mind that doesn't chase trends. Earth anchors the Rooster's sharpness with patience.
