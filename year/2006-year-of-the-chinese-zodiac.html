2006 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2006 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Bing Xu

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2006 was the year of Bing Xu. "Bing" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Xu" maps to the Dog in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2006 lunar year kicked off on Jan 29, 2006 and wrapped up on Feb 17, 2007. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Fire Dog. Born earlier in Jan 2006 -- before the 29 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years.

Yang Fire Energy

Bing Fire is Yang Fire -- the sun at high noon, the bonfire, the open flame. It gives people born under its influence warmth, visibility, and a natural confidence.
