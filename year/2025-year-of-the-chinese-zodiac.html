2025 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 2025 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Yi Si

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2025 was the year of Yi Si. "Yi" stands for Wood in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Si" maps to the Snake in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 2025

The 2025 lunar year kicked off on Jan 29, 2025 and wrapped up on Feb 16, 2026. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Wood Snake. But if you were born earlier in Jan 2025 -- before the 29 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Why Yin Wood Matters

The Yi Wood in the 2025 year of the Chinese zodiac is Yin Wood -- Yin energy is receptive, inward, and intuitive. It gives people born under its influence a depth of feeling and a quiet strength that runs deeper than it appears. Wood adds growth and cooperation. People born in this year tend to be more collaborative and open-minded than others of their sign, always looking for ways to expand and improve.

2025 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 2025 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

2025 on the Western Calendar

2025 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Wood Snake's growth-oriented, progressive energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 2025 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 2025, the Wood Snake period ran from Jan 29, 2025 through Feb 16, 2026.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Jan 29 2025 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Snake. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.

Why This Distinction Matters

Big Events That Shaped 2025

2025 had LA burn, Trump return to the White House, and Notre-Dame reopen. Here are the five moments that shaped the year.

Jan 7, 2025

LA Wildfires Destroy Pacific Palisades

Wind-driven fires leveled over 16,000 structures across Pacific Palisades and Altadena. The Palisades fire alone burned 23,000 acres and killed 16 people. Insured losses hit $40 billion, making it the costliest wildfire in US history.

Jan 20, 2025

Trump Inaugurated 47th President

Donald Trump took the oath indoors at the Capitol after the ceremony was moved due to cold weather. He signed 220 executive orders in his first 100 days, the most of any modern president. JD Vance was sworn in as VP.

Dec 8, 2025

Notre-Dame Cathedral Reopens

Five years and four months after the April 2019 fire, Notre-Dame held its first Mass on Dec 8, 2025. The reopening ceremony was attended by 50 heads of state including Trump, Macron, and Zelensky. The restoration cost 700 million euros.

April 21, 2025

Pope Francis Dies, New Pope Elected

Pope Francis died at 88 from a stroke. The conclave elected Cardinal Robert Prevost as Pope Leo XIV on May 8 -- the first American pope in history. He had served as prefect of the Dicastery for Bishops under Francis.

Feb 23, 2025

US Men's Hockey Wins Olympic Gold

The US beat Canada 2-1 in overtime at the Milan-Cortina Winter Olympics to win its first men's hockey gold since the 1980 Miracle on Ice. Jack Hughes scored the game-winner 1:41 into OT. The final drew 19.4 million US viewers, the most-watched hockey game in 45 years.
