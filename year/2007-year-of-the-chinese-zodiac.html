2007 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2007 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Ding Hai

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2007 was the year of Ding Hai. "Ding" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Hai" maps to the Pig in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2007 lunar year kicked off on Feb 18, 2007 and wrapped up on Feb 6, 2008. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Fire Pig. Born earlier in Feb 2007 -- before the 18 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years.

Yin Fire Energy

Ding Fire is Yin Fire -- the candle, the hearth, the soft glow. It gives people born under its influence warmth, depth, and a quiet magnetism that draws people in.
