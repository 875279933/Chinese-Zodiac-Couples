1968 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 1968 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Wu Shen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1968 was the year of Wu Shen. "Wu" stands for Earth in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Shen" maps to the Monkey in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 1968

The 1968 lunar year kicked off on Jan 30, 1968 and wrapped up on Feb 16, 1969. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Earth Monkey. But if you were born earlier in Jan 1968 -- before the 30 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Why Yang Earth Matters

The Wu Earth in the 1968 year of the Chinese zodiac is Yang Earth -- Yang energy is active, outward, and assertive. It pushes things forward and gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and drive. Earth brings stability and practicality. People born in this year are more grounded and reliable than others of their sign, with a no-nonsense approach that gets results.

1968 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 1968 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

1968 on the Western Calendar

1968 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Earth Monkey's grounded, stable energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 1968 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 1968, the Earth Monkey period ran from Jan 30, 1968 through Feb 16, 1969.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Jan 30 1968 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Monkey. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.

Why This Distinction Matters

If you're looking into family history or just trying to understand what the 1968 year of the Chinese zodiac really means, getting the calendar right is half the battle. The Chinese zodiac gives you a completely different way of reading personality and life path compared to Western astrology.
