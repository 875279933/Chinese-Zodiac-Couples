What does 1982 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

Famous People Born in 1982

Plenty of well-known people were born in 1982. Here are six of the most recognizable.

Anne Hathaway

Born November 12 in Brooklyn, New York. The Princess Diaries, then Brokeback Mountain, then The Dark Knight Rises. Won the Best Supporting Actress Oscar for Les Miserables in 2013 and has been a regular Mother Monster since The Idea of You.

Kirsten Dunst

Born April 30 in Point Pleasant, New Jersey. Interview with the Vampire at 12, then Bring It On, then Spider-Man. Won the Best Actress award at Cannes for Melancholia in 2011 and has since settled into a run of indie projects.

Jessica Biel

Born March 3 in Ely, Minnesota. 7th Heaven for six seasons, then The Texas Chainsaw Massacre remake. Co-founded the restaurant chain Au Fudge and Kinder Farm, and has been producing TV projects under her Iron Fish Productions banner.

Kelly Clarkson

Born April 24 in Fort Worth, Texas. The original American Idol winner in 2002, then Since U Been Gone. Has won three Daytime Emmys for The Kelly Clarkson Show, which was renewed through 2025 in 80 markets.

Chris Pine

Born August 26 in Los Angeles. Star Trek and Wonder Woman, then The Finest Hours, then Don't Worry Darling. Has been working more indie projects in recent years, including the recent Poolman and the buzzy The Brutalist.

LeAnn Rimes

Born August 28 in Jackson, Mississippi. Blue came out when she was 11 and won two Grammys. Continued recording country and pop through the 2010s and was a contestant on The Masked Singer in 2020.
