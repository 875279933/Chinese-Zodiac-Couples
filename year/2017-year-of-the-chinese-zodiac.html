2017 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2017 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Ding You

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2017 was the year of Ding You. "Ding" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "You" maps to the Rooster in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2017 lunar year kicked off on Jan 28, 2017 and wrapped up on Feb 15, 2018. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Fire Rooster. Born earlier in Jan 2017 -- before the 28 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yin Fire Energy

Ding Fire is Yin Fire -- inward, focused, and quietly intense. It burns steady rather than wild. People born under it carry a depth of feeling and a fierce inner drive that doesn't always show on the surface.

Big Events That Shaped 2017

2017 had a new US president, a massive Las Vegas shooting, and a total solar eclipse across America. Here are five events that stood out.

Jan 20, 2017

Trump Inaugurated

Donald Trump took the oath on the Capitol steps, with estimates putting attendance at 250,000 to 600,000. The Women's March the next day drew 4.1 million worldwide, the largest single-day protest in US history.

Oct 1, 2017

Las Vegas Shooting

A gunman fired more than 1,000 rounds from the 32nd floor of the Mandalay Bay into a country music festival, killing 60 and wounding 867. It became the deadliest mass shooting in modern US history.

Aug 25, 2017

Hurricane Harvey Hits Houston

Harvey dropped 60.58 inches of rain, the most ever recorded in the continental US. Damage hit $125 billion, tying Katrina as the costliest US hurricane. 30,000 people were rescued from flooded neighborhoods.

Aug 21, 2017

Total Solar Eclipse Crosses US

The first total solar eclipse visible coast-to-coast since 1918. NASA estimated 215 million US adults watched either in person or online, making it the most-viewed eclipse ever.

Oct 15, 2017

#MeToo Goes Viral

Alyssa Milano's tweet 12 days after the Weinstein allegations exploded into a 24-hour trend with 500,000 tweets in the US alone. By year's end, 190 high-profile men had been accused across industries.
