2002 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2002 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Ren Wu

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2002 was the year of Ren Wu. "Ren" stands for Water in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Wu" maps to the Horse in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2002 lunar year kicked off on Feb 12, 2002 and wrapped up on Jan 31, 2003. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Water Horse. Born earlier in Feb 2002 -- before the 12 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years.

Yang Water Energy

Ren Water is Yang Water -- the ocean, the river, the moving current. It gives people born under its influence a wide emotional range and an instinct for going with the flow.
