2035 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different rules. Here's how that affects the 2035 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Yi Mao

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2035 is the year of Yi Mao. "Yi" stands for Wood in the Heavenly Stems, and "Mao" maps to the Rabbit in the Earthly Branches. This combo only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 2035

The 2035 lunar year kicks off on Feb 8, 2035 and wraps up on Jan 27, 2036. If your birthday lands in that window, you're a Wood Rabbit. Born earlier in February 2035 —before the 8th —and you're actually the previous sign, not a Rabbit.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To stay in step with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years —which is why Chinese New Year moves around.

Why Yin Wood Matters

The Yi Wood in 2035 is Yin Wood —think vine, sapling, soft grass bending in the breeze rather than a hard tree. It brings a flexible, patient energy to the Rabbit. Yin Wood Rabbits grow into themselves slowly, and they don't need the spotlight to do it.

2035 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 2035 look like through a Western calendar lens, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

2035 on the Western Calendar

2035 is a common year —365 days, starting on a Monday. The headline event is a deep total solar eclipse over the South Pacific. The Chinese calendar pulses with the Wood Rabbit's patient, easygoing energy.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 2035 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run Jan 1 to Dec 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts, the zodiac year straddles two Western years. For 2035, the Wood Rabbit period runs from Feb 8, 2035 through Jan 27, 2036.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Jan 30, 2035? You're the previous sign, not a Rabbit. Born Feb 14, 2035? You're a Wood Rabbit. The lunar dates are the ones that count —always double-check if your birthday falls near Chinese New Year.

Why This Distinction Matters

If you're looking into family history, sorting out a kid's zodiac, or just curious about what 2035 means, getting the calendar right is half the battle. The Chinese zodiac reads personality and life path completely differently from Western astrology.

Big Moments in 2035

From sky-shaking eclipses to global political moments, here's what 2035 has on the calendar. These are the moments people born in 2035 will grow up hearing about.

The Year of the Wood Rabbit gets underway, with celebrations, red envelopes, and 15 days of festivities worldwide. The Lantern Festival lands on Feb 22, 2035.

Annular Solar Eclipse

An annular eclipse crosses the Southern Ocean and parts of Antarctica. Skywatchers in the right spots see a "ring of fire" effect as the moon covers most but not all of the sun.

Total Solar Eclipse

The headline sky event of the year. The path of totality crosses China, the Koreas, and Japan, putting it in front of hundreds of millions of people. Astronomers expect a major tourism spike across East Asia.

October 14

Annular Solar Eclipse

A second solar eclipse of the year —this one annular —crosses the Pacific, Hawaii, and parts of Central America. A rare double-feature year for skywatchers.

December 7

Pearl Harbor Remembrance Day

The 84th anniversary of the attack on Pearl Harbor. Veterans' groups in Hawaii host a memorial service, as they do every year on the date that pulled the US into World War II.
