2030 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2030 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Geng Xu

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2030 was the year of Geng Xu. "Geng" stands for Metal in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Xu" maps to the Dog in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2030 lunar year kicked off on Feb 3, 2030 and wrapped up on Jan 22, 2031. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Metal Dog. Born earlier in Feb 2030 -- before the 3 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yang Metal Energy

Geng Metal is Yang Metal -- assertive, outward, and strong. It gives people born under its influence a quiet confidence and a strong sense of self. Metal anchors the Dog's loyal heart with discipline.
