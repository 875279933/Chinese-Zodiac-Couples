1998 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 1998 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Wu Yin

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1998 was the year of Wu Yin. "Wu" stands for Earth in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Yin" maps to the Tiger in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 1998 lunar year kicked off on Jan 28, 1998 and wrapped up on Feb 15, 1999. If your birthday falls in that window, you're an Earth Tiger. Born earlier in Jan 1998 -- before the 28 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years.

Yang Earth Energy

Wu Earth is Yang Earth -- active, outward, and assertive. It gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and grounded drive.
