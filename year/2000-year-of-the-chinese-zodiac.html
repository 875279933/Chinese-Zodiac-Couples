2000 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2000 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Geng Chen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2000 was the year of Geng Chen. "Geng" stands for Metal in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Chen" maps to the Dragon in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2000 lunar year kicked off on Feb 5, 2000 and wrapped up on Jan 23, 2001. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Metal Dragon. Born earlier in Feb 2000 -- before the 5 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years.

Yang Metal Energy

Geng Metal is Yang Metal -- firm, sharp, and unyielding. It gives people born under its influence a spine of steel and a will that bends but does not break.
