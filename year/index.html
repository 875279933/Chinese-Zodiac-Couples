Year of the Chinese Zodiac at a Glance

Pick any year of the Chinese zodiac below for the animal, the element, and a short profile of what that combination looks like in practice.

The Chinese zodiac runs on a 12-year animal cycle. Each year also pairs with one of five elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water), which extends the combined cycle to 60 years. The animal sets the broad shape; the element shifts the texture.

This page covers the range from 1936 to 2042. Click any year to read the animal, the element, and a brief read on how that combination tends to play out.

Quick Facts

  • Cycle length12 years (animal), 60 years (full)
  • Animals in orderRat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig
  • New Year cutoffLate January to mid-February
