Year of the Chinese Zodiac

Every year in the Chinese calendar is ruled by one of twelve animals. Look up any year from 1936 to 2042 and discover which zodiac sign and element governs it.

Complete Year-by-Year Guide

Find your birth year below to discover your Chinese zodiac animal, element, and what it means for your personality and destiny.

The Chinese zodiac follows a 12-year cycle, with each year represented by an animal sign. But it goes deeper than that — each year also carries one of five elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water), creating a 60-year cycle that shapes personality and fortune.

This guide covers every year from 1936 to 2042. Click any year to read the full breakdown of its zodiac sign, element, personality traits, and compatibility insights.
