2023 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 2023 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Gui Mao

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2023 was the year of Gui Mao. "Gui" stands for Water in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Mao" maps to the Rabbit in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 2023

The 2023 lunar year kicked off on Jan 22, 2023 and wrapped up on Feb 9, 2024. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Water Rabbit. But if you were born earlier in Jan 2023 -- before the 22 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

2023 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 2023 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

2023 on the Western Calendar

2023 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Water Rabbit's adaptable, flexible energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 2023 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 2023, the Water Rabbit period ran from Jan 22, 2023 through Feb 9, 2024.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Jan 22 2023 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Rabbit. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.

Why This Distinction Matters

If you're looking into family history or just trying to understand what the 2023 year of the Chinese zodiac really means, getting the calendar right is half the battle. The Chinese zodiac gives you a completely different way of reading personality and life path compared to Western astrology.
