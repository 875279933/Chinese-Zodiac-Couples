2023 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2023 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Gui Mao

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2023 was the year of Gui Mao. "Gui" stands for Yin Water in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Mao" maps to the Rabbit in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2023 lunar year kicked off on Jan 22, 2023 and wrapped up on Feb 9, 2024. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Water Rabbit. Born earlier in Jan 2023 -- before the 22 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yin Water Energy

Gui Water is Yin Water -- still, reflective, and inward. It gives people born under its influence a deep emotional range and a quiet strength. Water flows through the Rabbit's grace and gives it room to feel.

Big Events That Shaped 2023

2023 was the year AI went mainstream, a Titan imploded, and Trump was indicted four times. Here are the five events that stood out.

Nov 30, 2022 - 2023

ChatGPT Sparks the AI Boom

OpenAI released ChatGPT on Nov 30, 2022. By January 2023, it had 100 million monthly users -- the fastest-growing consumer app in history. Microsoft invested $10 billion in OpenAI on Jan 23, kicking off the AI arms race.

June 18, 2023

Titan Submersible Implodes

OceanGate's Titan imploded 12,500 feet below the Atlantic surface while descending to the Titanic wreck, killing all 5 aboard. The US Coast Guard confirmed the implosion on June 22 after finding debris. Debris was found 1,600 feet from the Titanic bow.

Aug 8, 2023

Maui Wildfire

A brush fire driven by 60 mph winds destroyed the historic town of Lahaina on Maui, killing 101 people. It was the deadliest US wildfire in over 100 years. 2,200 structures burned in 6 hours.

2023

Trump Indicted Four Times

Trump was indicted by federal and state prosecutors four times in five months: by Manhattan DA (March 30), special counsel Jack Smith (June 8 and Aug 1), and Fulton County DA Fani Willis (Aug 14). 91 felony charges total across 4 jurisdictions.

Nov 17, 2023

OpenAI Briefly Fires Sam Altman

OpenAI's board fired CEO Sam Altman on Nov 17, saying he 'was not consistently candid in his communications.' Within 48 hours, 95% of the 770 employees threatened to quit, and Microsoft offered him a job. Altman was reinstated as CEO on Nov 21.
