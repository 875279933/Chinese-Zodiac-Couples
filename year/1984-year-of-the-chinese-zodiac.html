1984 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 1984 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Jia Zi

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1984 was the year of Jia Zi. "Jia" stands for Wood in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Zi" maps to the Rat in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 1984

The 1984 lunar year kicked off on Feb 2, 1984 and wrapped up on Feb 19, 1985. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Wood Rat. But if you were born earlier in Feb 1984 -- before the 2 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Why Yang Wood Matters

The Jia Wood in the 1984 year of the Chinese zodiac is Yang Wood -- Yang energy is active, outward, and assertive. It pushes things forward and gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and drive. Wood adds growth and cooperation. People born in this year tend to be more collaborative and open-minded than others of their sign, always looking for ways to expand and improve.

1984 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 1984 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

1984 on the Western Calendar

1984 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Wood Rat's growth-oriented, progressive energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 1984 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 1984, the Wood Rat period ran from Feb 2, 1984 through Feb 19, 1985.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Feb 2 1984 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Rat. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.

Why This Distinction Matters
