2037 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2037 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Ding Si

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2037 is the year of Ding Si. "Ding" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems, and "Si" maps to the Snake in the Earthly Branches. This combo only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2037 lunar year kicks off on Feb 14, 2037 and wraps up on Feb 3, 2038. If your birthday lands in that window, you're a Fire Snake. Born earlier in Feb 2037 -- before the 14th -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yin Fire Energy

Ding Fire is Yin Fire -- candle flame, lantern light. It gives people born under its influence a warm, focused presence. Fire lights up the Snake's intuition with quiet intensity.
