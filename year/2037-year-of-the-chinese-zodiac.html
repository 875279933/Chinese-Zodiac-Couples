2037 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different rules. Here's how that affects the 2037 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Ding Si

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2037 is the year of Ding Si. "Ding" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems, and "Si" maps to the Snake in the Earthly Branches. This combo only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 2037

The 2037 lunar year kicks off on Feb 15, 2037 and wraps up on Feb 3, 2038. If your birthday lands in that window, you're a Fire Snake. Born earlier in February 2037 — before the 15th — and you're actually the previous sign, not a Snake.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To stay in step with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years — which is why Chinese New Year moves around.

Why Yin Fire Matters

The Ding Fire in 2037 is Yin Fire — think candle flame, ember, lantern light. It's not the roaring sun of Yang Fire. Yin Fire Snakes are intense without being loud, magnetic without being obvious.
