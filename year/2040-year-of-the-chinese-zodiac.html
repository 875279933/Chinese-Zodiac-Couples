2040 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different rules. Here's how that affects the 2040 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Geng Shen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2040 is the year of Geng Shen. "Geng" stands for Metal in the Heavenly Stems, and "Shen" maps to the Monkey in the Earthly Branches. This combo only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 2040

The 2040 lunar year kicks off on Feb 12, 2040 and wraps up on Jan 31, 2041. If your birthday lands in that window, you're a Metal Monkey. Born earlier in February 2040 —before the 12th —and you're actually the previous sign, not a Monkey.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To stay in step with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years —which is why Chinese New Year moves around.

Why Yang Metal Matters

The Geng Metal in 2040 is Yang Metal —think sword, ax, anything with a sharp edge. It cuts through the noise. Yang Metal Monkeys are quick, decisive, and a little bit relentless. They don't ramble. They just get to the point.

The 2040 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run Jan 1 to Dec 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts, the zodiac year straddles two Western years. For 2040, the Metal Monkey period runs from Feb 12, 2040 through Jan 31, 2041.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully
