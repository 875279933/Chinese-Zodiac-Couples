2040 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2040 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Geng Shen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2040 is the year of Geng Shen. "Geng" stands for Metal in the Heavenly Stems, and "Shen" maps to the Monkey in the Earthly Branches. This combo only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2040 lunar year kicks off on Feb 11, 2040 and wraps up on Jan 30, 2041. If your birthday lands in that window, you're a Metal Monkey. Born earlier in Feb 2040 -- before the 11th -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yang Metal Energy

Geng Metal is Yang Metal -- a sword, a tool, an edge. It gives people born under its influence discipline and precision. Metal shapes the Monkey's cleverness into something sharper.
