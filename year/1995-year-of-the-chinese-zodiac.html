1995 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 1995 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Yi Hai

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1995 was the year of Yi Hai. "Yi" stands for Wood in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Hai" maps to the Pig in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 1995 lunar year kicked off on Jan 31, 1995 and wrapped up on Feb 18, 1996. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Wood Pig. But if you were born earlier in Jan 1995 -- before the 31 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases. Standard year runs about 354 days; a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years.

Yin Wood Energy

Yi Wood is Yin Wood -- the vine, the soft branch, the gentle bend. It gives people born under its influence a flexible resilience and a knack for thriving in changing conditions.
