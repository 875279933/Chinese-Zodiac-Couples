2019 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2019 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Ji Hai

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2019 was the year of Ji Hai. "Ji" stands for Earth in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Hai" maps to the Pig in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2019 lunar year kicked off on Feb 5, 2019 and wrapped up on Jan 24, 2020. If your birthday falls in that window, you're an Earth Pig. Born earlier in Feb 2019 -- before the 5 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yin Earth Energy

Ji Earth is Yin Earth -- nurturing, grounded, and quietly powerful. It shapes people born under it into caretakers who don't ask for credit and who follow through on every commitment.

Big Events That Shaped 2019

2019 had Notre-Dame burning, a Hong Kong uprising, and the first COVID-19 cases in Wuhan. Here are five events that set the stage for the 2020s.

April 15, 2019

Notre-Dame Fire

The 856-year-old cathedral's spire and roof collapsed in a 9-hour blaze watched by the world. Macron promised to rebuild within 5 years; the restoration is on track to reopen in December 2024.

March 15, 2019

Christchurch Mosque Shooting

A gunman live-streamed attacks on two mosques in New Zealand, killing 51 and wounding 40. Jacinda Ardern banned military-style semi-automatics within 27 days, one of the fastest gun law overhauls in modern history.

June 9, 2019

Hong Kong Protests Begin

An estimated 1 million people marched against a bill allowing extraditions to mainland China. The bill was suspended, but the protests expanded into demands for full democracy and lasted 18 months.

Dec 18, 2019

First Trump Impeachment

The House impeached President Trump 230-197 on abuse of power and 229-198 on obstruction of Congress. The Senate voted 52-48 to acquit on Feb 5, 2020, with Mitt Romney the only senator to cross party lines.

Dec 31, 2019

First COVID-19 Cases Reported

China reported a cluster of pneumonia cases of unknown cause in Wuhan to the WHO. The first genome was published Jan 11, 2020. The rest of the decade's biggest story started with 27 cases in one city.
