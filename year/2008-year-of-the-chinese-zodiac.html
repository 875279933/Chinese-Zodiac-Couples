2008 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2008 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Wu Zi

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2008 was the year of Wu Zi. "Wu" stands for Earth in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Zi" maps to the Rat in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2008 lunar year kicked off on Feb 7, 2008 and wrapped up on Jan 25, 2009. If your birthday falls in that window, you're an Earth Rat. Born earlier in Feb 2008 -- before the 7 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yang Earth Energy

Wu Earth is Yang Earth -- active, outward, and assertive. It gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and a steady drive that doesn't quit. Earth anchors the Rat's clever mind to a practical body.

Big Events That Shaped 2008

2008 was a wild year. From the first Black US president to a full-blown financial meltdown, here are five events that shaped the year.

Sept 15, 2008

Lehman Brothers Collapses

The 158-year-old investment bank filed for bankruptcy with $619 billion in debt. The Dow dropped 504 points the next day and the global financial crisis was on.

Nov 4, 2008

Obama Elected 44th President

Barack Obama won 365 electoral votes and 53% of the popular vote, becoming the first Black president in US history. He defeated John McCain in a turnout that hit 61.6%.

Aug 8-24, 2008

Beijing Summer Olympics

Michael Phelps won eight gold medals and broke Mark Spitz's 1972 record. Usain Bolt ran 9.69 in the 100m, then 9.72 in Beijing to set a new world record. China topped the gold-medal count for the first time.

July 11, 2008

iPhone 3G Launches

Apple's second-gen iPhone went on sale at $199 with a 2-year contract, half the price of the original. The App Store opened the same week and changed how software gets shipped forever.

Jan 22, 2008

Super Tuesday Primary

24 states voted on a single day, the largest primary contest in US history. Obama won 13 states including California and New York, while Hillary Clinton took the big industrial states.
