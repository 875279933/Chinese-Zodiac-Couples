1960 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 1960 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Geng Zi

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1960 was the year of Geng Zi. "Geng" stands for Metal in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Zi" maps to the Rat in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 1960

The 1960 lunar year kicked off on Jan 28, 1960 and wrapped up on Feb 14, 1961. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Metal Rat. But if you were born earlier in Jan 1960 -- before the 28 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Why Yang Metal Matters

The Geng Metal in the 1960 year of the Chinese zodiac is Yang Metal -- Yang energy is active, outward, and assertive. It pushes things forward and gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and drive. Metal adds resolve and discipline. People born in this year are tougher and more focused than others of their sign, with an inner strength that keeps them on track no matter what.

1960 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 1960 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

1960 on the Western Calendar

1960 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Metal Rat's determined, resolute energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 1960 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 1960, the Metal Rat period ran from Jan 28, 1960 through Feb 14, 1961.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Jan 28 1960 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Rat. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.
