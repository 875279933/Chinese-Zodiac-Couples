2041 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different rules. Here's how that affects the 2041 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Xin You

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2041 is the year of Xin You. "Xin" stands for Metal in the Heavenly Stems, and "You" maps to the Rooster in the Earthly Branches. This combo only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 2041

The 2041 lunar year kicks off on Feb 1, 2041 and wraps up on Jan 21, 2042. If your birthday lands in that window, you're a Metal Rooster. Born earlier in January 2041 —before Feb 1 —and you're actually the previous sign, not a Rooster.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To stay in step with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years —which is why Chinese New Year moves around.

Why Yin Metal Matters

The Xin Metal in 2041 is Yin Metal —think jewelry, fine cutlery, well-worn coin. It's sharper and more refined than Yang Metal. Yin Metal Roosters are detail-oriented without being showy, polished without being flashy.

What does 2041 look like through a Western calendar lens, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

The 2041 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run Jan 1 to Dec 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts, the zodiac year straddles two Western years. For 2041, the Metal Rooster period runs from Feb 1, 2041 through Jan 21, 2042.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Jan 22, 2041? You're the previous sign, not a Rooster. Born Feb 8, 2041? You're a Metal Rooster. The lunar dates are the ones that count —always double-check if your birthday falls near Chinese New Year.
