2041 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2041 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Xin You

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2041 is the year of Xin You. "Xin" stands for Metal in the Heavenly Stems, and "You" maps to the Rooster in the Earthly Branches. This combo only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2041 lunar year kicks off on Jan 30, 2041 and wraps up on Feb 18, 2042. If your birthday lands in that window, you're a Metal Rooster. Born earlier in Jan 2041 -- before the 30th -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yin Metal Energy

Xin Metal is Yin Metal -- jewelry, fine blades, polished edges. It gives people born under its influence refinement and precision. Metal shapes the Rooster's confidence into something elegant and exact.
