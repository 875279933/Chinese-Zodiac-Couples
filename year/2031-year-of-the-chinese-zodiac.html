2031 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2031 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Xin Hai

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2031 was the year of Xin Hai. "Xin" stands for Metal in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Hai" maps to the Pig in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2031 lunar year kicked off on Jan 23, 2031 and wrapped up on Feb 10, 2032. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Metal Pig. Born earlier in Jan 2031 -- before the 23 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Xin Metal is Yin Metal -- refined, inward, and precise. It gives people born under its influence a quiet strength and a sharp sense of what's worth their energy. Metal anchors the Pig's open heart with focus.
