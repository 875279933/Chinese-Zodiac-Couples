The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar do not follow the same rules. Here is how that affects the 1941 year of the Chinese zodiac.

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1941 was the year of Xin Si. "Xin" stands for Yin Metal in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Si" maps to the Snake in the Earthly Branches. The pairing recurs once every 60 years, with the next Xin Si year falling in 2001.

Lunar Year Dates for 1941

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month is added roughly every three years to keep pace with the solar year.
