1936 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar do not follow the same rules. Here is how that affects the 1936 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Bing Zi

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1936 was the year of Bing Zi. "Bing" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Zi" maps to the Rat in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years, with the next Bing Zi year falling in 1996.

Lunar Year Dates for 1936

The 1936 lunar year kicked off on Jan 24, 1936 and wrapped up on Feb 10, 1937. If your birthday falls in that window, you are a Fire Rat. But if you were born earlier in Jan 1936, before the 24, you would actually fall under the previous zodiac sign instead. The full window for the 1937 year of the Chinese zodiac does not start until February.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years, which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Why Yang Fire Matters

The Bing Fire in the 1936 year of the Chinese zodiac is Yang Fire, and Yang energy is active, outward, and assertive. It pushes things forward and gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and a visible presence in a room. Compared to other Rat years, the 1936 profile tends to leave a stronger first impression.

1936 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 1936 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

1936 on the Western Calendar

1936 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward, running January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Fire Rat's dynamic energy, and that reading shapes how people born that year tend to approach a new project or a room full of strangers.

Why the Dates Do Not Line Up

The 1936 year of the Chinese zodiac does not run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 1936, the Fire Rat period ran from Jan 24, 1936 through Feb 10, 1937.

Check the Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Jan 23 1936 or earlier? You are the previous sign, not a Rat. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count, and it pays to double-check when the birthday falls close to the Chinese New Year cutoff.
