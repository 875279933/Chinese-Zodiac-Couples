2039 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different rules. Here's how that affects the 2039 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Ji Wei

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2039 is the year of Ji Wei. "Ji" stands for Earth in the Heavenly Stems, and "Wei" maps to the Goat in the Earthly Branches. This combo only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 2039

The 2039 lunar year kicks off on Jan 24, 2039 and wraps up on Feb 11, 2040. If your birthday lands in that window, you're an Earth Goat. Born earlier in January 2039 —before the 24th —and you're actually the previous sign, not a Goat.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To stay in step with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years —which is why Chinese New Year moves around.

Why Yin Earth Matters

The Ji Earth in 2039 is Yin Earth —think garden soil, soft loam, things that grow. It's gentler than Yang Earth, and it gives Goats a more nurturing, patient read. Yin Earth Goats don't rush. They let things —and people —bloom at their own pace.

The 2039 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run Jan 1 to Dec 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts, the zodiac year straddles two Western years. For 2039, the Earth Goat period runs from Jan 24, 2039 through Feb 11, 2040.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully
