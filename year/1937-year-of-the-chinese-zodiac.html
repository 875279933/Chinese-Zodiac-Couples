1937 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar do not start on the same date. For 1937, the practical effect is a February 11, 1937 start and a January 30, 1938 end.

The Year of Ding Chou

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1937 is the year of Ding Chou. "Ding" stands for Yin Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Chou" maps to the Ox in the Earthly Branches. The pairing recurs once every 60 years, with the next Ding Chou year falling in 1997.

Lunar Year Dates for 1937

The 1937 lunar year began on February 11, 1937 and ended on January 30, 1938. A birthday inside that window falls under the Fire Ox sign. A January 1937 birthday falls under the Fire Rat sign of the 1936 year of the Chinese zodiac, since the 1937 window has not opened yet.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month is added roughly every three years to keep pace with the solar year, which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Why Yin Fire Matters

The Ding Fire in the 1937 year of the Chinese zodiac is Yin Fire, closer to candlelight than a bonfire. Yin Fire is refined, inward, and precise. It does not broadcast, but it gives the 1937 Ox a sharper read on what the situation actually calls for than the louder Yang Fire years.

1937 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 1937 look like through the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the 1937 year of the Chinese zodiac?

1937 on the Western Calendar

1937 on the Gregorian calendar runs January 1 through December 31. In Chinese culture, the year carried the Fire Ox's intense, focused energy, shaping how people born that year approach the work in front of them.

Why the Dates Do Not Line Up

The 1937 year of the Chinese zodiac does not run from January 1 to December 31. Chinese New Year moves around the Gregorian calendar, so the zodiac year spans two Gregorian years. For 1937, the Fire Ox period ran from February 11, 1937 through January 30, 1938.

Check the Birthdate Carefully

Born February 10, 1937? You are still the previous sign, the Fire Rat. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count, so always double-check when the birthday falls close to the Chinese New Year cutoff.

Born December 21 in New York City. Two Best Actress Oscars, seven decades on screen, and a second act as a producer and activist that may be more influential than the first. Still working, still in the news.
