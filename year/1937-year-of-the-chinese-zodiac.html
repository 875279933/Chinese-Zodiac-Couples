1937 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 1937 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Ding Chou

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1937 was the year of Ding Chou. "Ding" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Chou" maps to the Ox in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 1937

The 1937 lunar year kicked off on Feb 11, 1937 and wrapped up on Jan 30, 1938. If your birthday falls in that window, you're an Fire Ox. But if you were born earlier in Feb 1937 -- before the 11 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

1937 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 1937 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

1937 on the Western Calendar

1937 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Fire Ox's passionate, dynamic energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 1937 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 1937, the Fire Ox period ran from Feb 11, 1937 through Jan 30, 1938.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Feb 11 1937 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not an Ox. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.

Why This Distinction Matters
