1980 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 1980 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Geng Shen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1980 was the year of Geng Shen. "Geng" stands for Metal in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Shen" maps to the Monkey in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 1980

The 1980 lunar year kicked off on Feb 16, 1980 and wrapped up on Feb 4, 1981. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Metal Monkey. But if you were born earlier in Feb 1980 -- before the 16 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Why Yang Metal Matters

The Geng Metal in the 1980 year of the Chinese zodiac is Yang Metal -- Yang energy is active, outward, and assertive. It pushes things forward and gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and drive. Metal adds resolve and discipline to the Monkey's clever mind.

1980 on the Western Calendar

1980 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Metal Monkey's determined, resolute energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 1980 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 1980, the Metal Monkey period ran from Feb 16, 1980 through Feb 4, 1981.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Feb 16 1980 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Monkey. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.
