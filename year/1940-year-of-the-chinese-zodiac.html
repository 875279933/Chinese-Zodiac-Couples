1940 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 1940 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Geng Chen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1940 was the year of Geng Chen. "Geng" stands for Metal in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Chen" maps to the Dragon in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 1940

The 1940 lunar year kicked off on Feb 8, 1940 and wrapped up on Jan 26, 1941. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Metal Dragon. But if you were born earlier in Feb 1940 -- before the 8 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Why Yang Metal Matters

The Geng Metal in the 1940 year of the Chinese zodiac is Yang Metal -- Yang energy is active, outward, and assertive. It pushes things forward and gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and drive. Metal adds resolve and discipline. People born in this year are tougher and more focused than others of their sign, with an inner strength that keeps them on track no matter what.

1940 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 1940 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

1940 on the Western Calendar

1940 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Metal Dragon's determined, resolute energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 1940 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 1940, the Metal Dragon period ran from Feb 8, 1940 through Jan 26, 1941.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Feb 8 1940 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Dragon. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.
