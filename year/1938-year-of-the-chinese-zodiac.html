1938 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar do not follow the same rules. Here is how that affects the 1938 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Wu Yin

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1938 was the year of Wu Yin. "Wu" stands for Earth in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Yin" maps to the Tiger in the Earthly Branches. The pairing recurs once every 60 years, with the next Wu Yin year falling in 1998.

Lunar Year Dates for 1938

The 1938 lunar year kicked off on Jan 31, 1938 and wrapped up on Feb 18, 1939. A birthday inside that window is an Earth Tiger. A January 1 to 30, 1938 birthday falls under the Fire Ox of the 1937 year of the Chinese zodiac, since the 1938 lunar year has not opened yet.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month is added roughly every three years to keep pace with the solar year, which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.
