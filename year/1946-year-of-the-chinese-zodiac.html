1946 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 1946 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

Cycle Position
What is the year of Bing Xu in the 60-year cycle?
In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1946 was the year of Bing Xu. "Bing" stands for Yang Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Xu" maps to the Dog in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.
Lunar Window
When does the 1946 Fire Dog window open and close?
The 1946 lunar year kicked off on Feb 2, 1946 and wrapped up on Jan 21, 1947. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Fire Dog. Born earlier in Feb 1946 -- before the 2 -- and you're the previous zodiac sign instead.
Calendar Mechanics
How does the Chinese lunar calendar actually work?
The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.
Element Reading
Why does Yang Fire matter for the 1946 Fire Dog?
The Bing Fire in the 1946 year of the Chinese zodiac is Yang Fire -- Yang energy is active, outward, and assertive. It pushes things forward and gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and drive that the typical Dog year does not always carry.
