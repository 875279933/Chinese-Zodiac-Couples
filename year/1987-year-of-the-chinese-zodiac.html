1987 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 1987 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Ding Mao

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1987 was the year of Ding Mao. "Ding" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Mao" maps to the Rabbit in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 1987 lunar year kicked off on Jan 29, 1987 and wrapped up on Feb 16, 1988. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Fire Rabbit.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases. Standard year runs about 354 days; a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years.

Yin Fire Energy

Ding Fire is Yin Fire -- receptive, inward, and intuitive. It gives people born under its influence a depth of feeling and a quiet strength.
