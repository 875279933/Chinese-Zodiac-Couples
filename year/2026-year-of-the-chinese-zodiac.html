2026 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 2026 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Bing Wu

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2026 was the year of Bing Wu. "Bing" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Wu" maps to the Horse in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 2026

The 2026 lunar year kicked off on Feb 17, 2026 and wrapped up on Feb 5, 2027. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Fire Horse. But if you were born earlier in Feb 2026 -- before the 17 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.
