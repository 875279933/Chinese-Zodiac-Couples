2026 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2026 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Bing Wu

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2026 was the year of Bing Wu. "Bing" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Wu" maps to the Horse in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2026 lunar year kicked off on Feb 17, 2026 and wrapped up on Feb 5, 2027. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Fire Horse. Born earlier in Feb 2026 -- before the 17 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Bing Fire is Yang Fire -- active, outward, and assertive. It pushes things forward and gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and drive. Fire adds heat to the Horse's already kinetic spirit.
