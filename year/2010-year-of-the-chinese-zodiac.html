2010 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2010 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Geng Yin

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2010 was the year of Geng Yin. "Geng" represents Yang Metal in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Yin" maps to the Tiger in the Earthly Branches. This particular combination rolls around only once every 60 years, giving the 2010 year of the Chinese zodiac a rare signature in the broader cycle.

Yang Metal, often associated with blades and armor, brings a sharp, decisive quality that doesn't bend easily. It works in combination with the Tiger's natural fire to make 2010 Tigers more focused, more structured, and more direct than their Wood- or Water-element counterparts.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2010 lunar year opened on Feb 14, 2010 and closed on Feb 2, 2011. Anyone born in that window carries the Metal Tiger sign.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar follows the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard year runs about 354 days, with a leap month added roughly every three years to keep pace with the solar cycle.

Yang Metal Energy

Geng Metal is Yang Metal -- firm, clear, and outward-facing. It cuts away the unnecessary and leaves only what matters.

Big Events That Shaped 2010

2010 brought an oil spill that ran for months, a rescue that captured the world's attention, and a healthcare overhaul. Here are five things that defined the year.

April 20, 2010

Deepwater Horizon Explodes

The BP oil rig blew up 41 miles off the Louisiana coast, killing 11 workers. The well gushed 4.9 million barrels of crude into the Gulf over 87 days before it was finally capped in July.

Jan 12, 2010

Haiti Earthquake

A 7.0 magnitude quake hit Port-au-Prince, killing an estimated 220,000-300,000 people and leaving 1.5 million homeless. The US launched Operation Unified Response with 22,000 troops.

March 23, 2010

ACA Becomes Law

Obama signed the Affordable Care Act in the East Room, the largest US healthcare overhaul since Medicare in 1965. The law extended coverage to 20 million Americans over the next decade.

Oct 13, 2010

Chilean Miners Rescued

33 miners trapped 2,300 feet underground for 69 days were pulled out one by one in a custom-made capsule. The longest underground survival in history got a global TV audience.

April 3, 2010

iPad Launches

Apple sold 300,000 iPads on day one and 1 million within the first month. Critics called it a giant iPhone, but it kicked off the modern tablet market and a $1 trillion app ecosystem.
