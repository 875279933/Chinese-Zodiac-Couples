1948 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 1948 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Wu Zi

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1948 was the year of Wu Zi. "Wu" stands for Earth in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Zi" maps to the Rat in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 1948

The 1948 lunar year kicked off on Feb 10, 1948 and wrapped up on Jan 28, 1949. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Earth Rat. But if you were born earlier in Feb 1948 -- before the 10 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

It pushes things forward and gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and drive. Earth brings stability and practicality. People born in this year are more grounded and reliable than others of their sign, with a no-nonsense approach that gets results.

1948 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 1948 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

1948 on the Western Calendar

1948 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Earth Rat's grounded, stable energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 1948 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 1948, the Earth Rat period ran from Feb 10, 1948 through Jan 28, 1949.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Feb 10 1948 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Rat. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.

Famous People Born in 1948

1948 was a stacked year. Here are six well-known Earth Rats who made a name for themselves.

Samuel L. Jackson

Born December 21 in Washington, D.C. Jackson worked as a social worker and sat in on Martin Luther King Jr.'s funeral as a usher before he ever got a big break. Pulp Fiction, The Hateful Eight, and the Marvel films as Nick Fury made him one of the highest-grossing actors in film history.

James Cameron

Born August 16 in Kapuskasing, Ontario. Cameron directed the first two Terminator films, Aliens, Titanic, and Avatar. Titanic and Avatar are the only two movies in history to gross over $2 billion. He's also a deep-sea explorer who's done dozens of solo dives to the Titanic wreck.

Terry Bradshaw

Born September 17 in Shreveport, Louisiana. Bradshaw quarterbacked the Pittsburgh Steelers to four Super Bowl wins in the 1970s, the only quarterback to do it. He won league MVP twice, then transitioned into broadcasting and is still on Fox NFL Sunday.

Cat Stevens

Born July 21 in London. Stevens had a string of UK and US hits in the 1970s ("Peace Train," "Wild World," "Father and Son") before converting to Islam in 1977 and walking away from music entirely. He came back as Yusuf Islam in 2006 and has released acclaimed albums since.

Kenny Loggins

Born January 1 in Everett, Washington. Loggins started with Loggins and Messina, then went solo and became the unofficial voice of the 1980s movie soundtrack. "Footloose," "Danger Zone" from Top Gun, and "I'm Alright" from Caddyshack are all his.

Jean-Michel Basquiat

Born December 22 in Brooklyn. Basquiat went from spraying graffiti around Lower Manhattan to selling paintings for over $100 million in less than ten years. He died at 27 in 1988, and his work is now in the permanent collections of every major modern art museum.
