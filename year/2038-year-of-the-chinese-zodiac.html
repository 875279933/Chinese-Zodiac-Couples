2038 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2038 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Wu Wu

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2038 is the year of Wu Wu. "Wu" stands for Earth in the Heavenly Stems, and "Wu" maps to the Horse in the Earthly Branches. This combo only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2038 lunar year kicks off on Feb 3, 2038 and wraps up on Jan 23, 2039. If your birthday lands in that window, you're an Earth Horse. Born earlier in Feb 2038 -- before the 3rd -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.
