2015 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2015 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Yi Wei

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2015 was the year of Yi Wei. "Yi" stands for Wood in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Wei" maps to the Goat in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2015 lunar year kicked off on Feb 19, 2015 and wrapped up on Feb 7, 2016. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Wood Goat. Born earlier in Feb 2015 -- before the 19 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yin Wood Energy

Yi Wood is Yin Wood -- flexible, intuitive, and quietly persistent. It gives people born under its influence a depth of feeling and a soft strength that bends without breaking, perfect fuel for the Goat's creative mind.

Big Events That Shaped 2015

2015 brought nationwide marriage equality, a wave of terrorist attacks, and a new chapter with Cuba. Here are five events that made the year.

June 26, 2015

Same-Sex Marriage Legalized

The Supreme Court ruled 5-4 in Obergefell v. Hodges that same-sex couples have a constitutional right to marry. Marriage licenses were issued in 32 states the next day.

Nov 13, 2015

Paris Attacks

Coordinated ISIS strikes at the Bataclan theater and six other locations killed 130 people. France closed its borders for the first time since WWII and invoked Article 42.7 of the EU treaty for the first time.

July 14, 2015

New Horizons Flies by Pluto

The NASA probe made its closest pass at 7,800 miles after a 9.5-year, 3-billion-mile journey. The first photos showed a heart-shaped glacier and a young surface with no craters.

Sept 18, 2015

Volkswagen Emissions Scandal

The EPA caught VW using defeat-device software on 11 million diesel cars. The company later pleaded guilty and paid $30 billion in fines and settlements, the largest auto industry penalty in US history.

July 20, 2015

Cuba-US Relations Restored

The Cuban and US embassies reopened after 54 years. Google, Airbnb, and Starbucks all announced Cuba expansion plans, and the first commercial cruise from Miami docked in Havana in 2016.
