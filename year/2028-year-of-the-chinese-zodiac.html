2028 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2028 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Wu Shen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2028 was the year of Wu Shen. "Wu" stands for Earth in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Shen" maps to the Monkey in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2028 lunar year kicked off on Jan 26, 2028 and wrapped up on Feb 12, 2029. If your birthday falls in that window, you're an Earth Monkey. Born earlier in Jan 2028 -- before the 26 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yang Earth Energy

Wu Earth is Yang Earth -- grounded, outward, and steady. It gives people born under its influence a sense of structure and follow-through. Earth grounds the Monkey's quick mind with patience and practicality.
