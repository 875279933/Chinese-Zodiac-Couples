2028 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 2028 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Wu Shen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2028 was the year of Wu Shen. "Wu" stands for Earth in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Shen" maps to the Monkey in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 2028

The 2028 lunar year kicked off on Jan 26, 2028 and wrapped up on Feb 12, 2029. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Earth Monkey. But if you were born earlier in Jan 2028 -- before the 26 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

2028 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 2028 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

2028 on the Western Calendar

2028 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Earth Monkey's grounded, stable energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 2028 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 2028, the Earth Monkey period ran from Jan 26, 2028 through Feb 12, 2029.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Jan 26 2028 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Monkey. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.
