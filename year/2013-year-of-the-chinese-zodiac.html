2013 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2013 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Gui Si

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2013 was the year of Gui Si. "Gui" stands for Water in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Si" maps to the Snake in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2013 lunar year kicked off on Feb 10, 2013 and wrapped up on Jan 30, 2014. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Water Snake. Born earlier in Feb 2013 -- before the 10 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Water brings adaptability and intuition. People born in this year are more flexible and perceptive than others of their sign.

Big Events That Shaped 2013

2013 had a marathon bombing, the start of the Me Too hashtag, and a typhoon that killed thousands. Here are five events that defined the year.

April 15, 2013

Boston Marathon Bombing

Two pressure-cooker bombs exploded near the finish line, killing 3 and injuring 264. A citywide manhunt ended four days later in Watertown when the Tsarnaev brothers were found in a boat.

June 6, 2013

Snowden NSA Leaks

The Guardian and Washington Post published the first stories based on Edward Snowden's documents revealing PRISM and bulk phone metadata collection. Snowden fled to Hong Kong, then Moscow.

June 26, 2013

DOMA Struck Down

The Supreme Court ruled 5-4 in United States v. Windsor that Section 3 of the Defense of Marriage Act was unconstitutional. Same-sex marriages resumed in California the next day.

Nov 8, 2013

Typhoon Haiyan

One of the strongest tropical cyclones ever recorded hit the Philippines with 195 mph winds. The official death toll hit 6,300 with another 1,000 still missing. Tacloban was 90% destroyed.

Dec 5, 2013

Nelson Mandela Dies

The anti-apartheid icon and South Africa's first Black president died at 95. Ten days of national mourning culminated in a state funeral attended by 91 heads of state.
