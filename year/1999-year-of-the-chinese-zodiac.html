1999 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 1999 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Ji Mao

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1999 was the year of Ji Mao. "Ji" stands for Earth in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Mao" maps to the Rabbit in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 1999 lunar year kicked off on Feb 16, 1999 and wrapped up on Feb 4, 2000. If your birthday falls in that window, you're an Earth Rabbit. Born earlier in Feb 1999 -- before the 16 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years.

Yin Earth Energy

Ji Earth is Yin Earth -- receptive, nurturing, and stable. It gives people born under its influence a deep sense of responsibility and care for those around them.

Famous People Born in 1999

1999 produced a pretty solid group. Here are six of the most well-known names from that year.

Lil Nas X

Born April 9 in Atlanta, Georgia. Old Town Road spent 19 weeks at #1 in 2019, the longest run in Hot 100 history. Montero and Industry Baby both made the top 10, and the Long Live Montero tour was a 2022-2023 highlight.

Sabrina Carpenter

Born May 11 in Quakertown, Pennsylvania. Girl Meets World, then five solo albums, then Please Please Please and Espresso which both hit #1 in 2024. The Short n' Sweet tour grossed $250 million in 2024-2025.

Madison Beer

Born March 5 in Jericho, New York. Discovered on YouTube at 14, then Melodies in 2018, then The Spinnin Sessions. Life Support came out in 2021 and Silence Between Songs in 2023, both on the Billboard 200 top 30.

Kiernan Shipka

Born November 10 in Chicago. Sally Draper on Mad Men, then the 2014 Chilling Adventures of Sabrina on Netflix for four seasons, then the 2024 horror comedy Totally Killer. Has been a steady indie film presence and Dior regular.

Luka Doncic

Born February 28 in Ljubljana, Slovenia. Real Madrid debut at 16, then three EuroLeague titles. Joined the Mavericks in 2018, won Rookie of the Year, then made five All-Star teams before his 2025 trade to the Lakers.

Ja Morant

Born August 10 in Dalzell, South Carolina. Murray State, then the #2 pick in 2019, then Rookie of the Year. Two All-Star nods, a Most Improved Player award in 2022, and one of the most-watched highlights players of the modern NBA.
