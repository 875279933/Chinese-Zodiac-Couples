1942 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar do not follow the same rules. Here is how that affects the 1942 year of the Chinese zodiac.

Year of Ren Wu

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1942 was the year of Ren Wu. "Ren" stands for Yang Water in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Wu" maps to the Horse in the Earthly Branches. The pairing recurs once every 60 years, with the next Ren Wu year falling in 2002.

Lunar Dates

Lunar Window for 1942

The 1942 lunar year kicked off on Feb 15, 1942 and wrapped up on Feb 4, 1943. A birthday inside that window is a Water Horse. A January 1942 birthday falls under the Metal Snake of the 1941 year of the Chinese zodiac, since the 1942 lunar year has not opened yet.

How It Works

The Lunar Calendar in Practice

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month is added roughly every three years to keep pace with the solar year.

What does 1942 look like through the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the 1942 year of the Chinese zodiac?

1942 on the Western Calendar

Why the Dates Do Not Line Up

The 1942 year of the Chinese zodiac does not run from January 1 to December 31. Chinese New Year moves around the Gregorian calendar, so the zodiac year spans two Gregorian years. For 1942, the Water Horse period ran from February 15, 1942 through February 4, 1943.

Check the Birthdate Carefully

Born February 14, 1942 or earlier? You are the Metal Snake, not a Horse. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count, and it pays to double-check when the birthday falls close to the Chinese New Year cutoff.
