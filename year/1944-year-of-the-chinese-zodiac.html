1944 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 1944 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Jia Shen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1944 was the year of Jia Shen. "Jia" stands for Wood in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Shen" maps to the Monkey in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 1944

The 1944 lunar year kicked off on Jan 25, 1944 and wrapped up on Feb 12, 1945. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Wood Monkey. But if you were born earlier in Jan 1944 -- before the 25 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Why Yang Wood Matters

The Jia Wood in the 1944 year of the Chinese zodiac is Yang Wood -- Yang energy is active, outward, and assertive. It pushes things forward and gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and drive. Wood adds growth and cooperation. People born in this year tend to be more collaborative and open-minded than others of their sign, always looking for ways to expand and improve.

1944 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 1944 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

1944 on the Western Calendar

1944 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Wood Monkey's growth-oriented, progressive energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 1944 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 1944, the Wood Monkey period ran from Jan 25, 1944 through Feb 12, 1945.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Jan 25 1944 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Monkey. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.

Why This Distinction Matters

If you're looking into family history or just trying to understand what the 1944 year of the Chinese zodiac really means, getting the calendar right is half the battle. The Chinese zodiac gives you a completely different way of reading personality and life path compared to Western astrology.

Born January 26 in Birmingham, Alabama. Activist, philosopher, professor. Davis spent decades in political movements, wrote several influential books, and is the kind of figure who shows up in American history textbooks for more reasons than one.
