1966 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 1966 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Bing Wu

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1966 was the year of Bing Wu. "Bing" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Wu" maps to the Horse in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 1966

The 1966 lunar year kicked off on Jan 21, 1966 and wrapped up on Feb 8, 1967. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Fire Horse. But if you were born earlier in Jan 1966 -- before the 21 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

It pushes things forward and gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and drive. Fire cranks up the intensity. People born in this year have more drive, more passion, and more presence than others of their sign. They light up rooms and push hard for what they want.

1966 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 1966 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

1966 on the Western Calendar

1966 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Fire Horse's passionate, dynamic energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 1966 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 1966, the Fire Horse period ran from Jan 21, 1966 through Feb 8, 1967.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Jan 21 1966 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Horse. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.
