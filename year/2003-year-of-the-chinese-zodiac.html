2003 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2003 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Gui Wei

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2003 was the year of Gui Wei. "Gui" stands for Water in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Wei" maps to the Goat in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2003 lunar year kicked off on Feb 1, 2003 and wrapped up on Jan 21, 2004. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Water Goat. Born earlier in Feb 2003 -- before the 1 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years.

Yin Water Energy

Gui Water is Yin Water -- gentle rain, morning dew, deep still pools. It gives people born under its influence a quiet depth and an instinct for nurturing.
