Senior planner, county infrastructure office

She takes the file, reads the four pages of context, finds the line that no one else looked at, and writes the staff report on Friday. She is not the one who speaks up in the kickoff. She is the one who is still there at the end of the week with a working draft.

The Ox steadiness shows up as the willingness to take the project nobody else wanted. The Water layer shows up in the way she reads the room before the work starts. Priya is not the loudest voice in the meeting, but she is the one whose draft the team learns to read first.
