2005 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2005 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Yi You

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2005 was the year of Yi You. "Yi" stands for Wood in the Heavenly Stems system, while "You" maps to the Rooster in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2005 lunar year kicked off on Feb 9, 2005 and wrapped up on Jan 28, 2006. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Wood Rooster. Born earlier in Feb 2005 -- before the 9 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years.

Yin Wood Energy

Yi Wood is Yin Wood -- the vine, the reed, the flexible branch. It gives people born under its influence an adaptability and a quiet strength that bends without breaking.
