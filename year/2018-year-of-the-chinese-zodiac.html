2018 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2018 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Wu Xu

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2018 was the year of Wu Xu. "Wu" stands for Earth in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Xu" maps to the Dog in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2018 lunar year kicked off on Feb 16, 2018 and wrapped up on Feb 4, 2019. If your birthday falls in that window, you're an Earth Dog. Born earlier in Feb 2018 -- before the 16 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yang Earth Energy

Wu Earth is Yang Earth -- active, outward, and assertive. It gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and a steady drive that doesn't quit. Earth anchors the Dog's loyal heart to a practical outlook.

Big Events That Shaped 2018

2018 brought a historic Trump-Kim summit, a Florida school shooting, and the disappearance of a Saudi journalist. Here are five events that shaped the year.

Feb 14, 2018

Parkland School Shooting

17 students and staff were killed at Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School. Surviving students organized the March for Our Lives rally in DC, drawing 800,000 protesters, then 1.2 million at 800 satellite events.

June 12, 2018

Trump-Kim Summit in Singapore

President Trump met Kim Jong Un at the Capella Hotel on Sentosa Island, the first ever meeting between a sitting US president and North Korean leader. Both signed a vague denuclearization agreement.

Oct 2, 2018

Jamal Khashoggi Murdered

The Saudi journalist entered the Saudi consulate in Istanbul and was killed and dismembered. The CIA later concluded with high confidence that Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman ordered it.

Nov 8, 2018

Camp Fire

California's deadliest wildfire killed 85 people and destroyed the town of Paradise. 18,804 structures burned, the most ever lost in a single wildfire in state history.

Oct 1, 2018

Kavanaugh Confirmed

Brett Kavanaugh was confirmed to the Supreme Court 50-48, the narrowest margin since 1881. Christine Blasey Ford's allegations and FBI investigation dominated 10 days of hearings.
