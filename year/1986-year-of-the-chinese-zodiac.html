1986 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 1986 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Bing Yin

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1986 was the year of Bing Yin. "Bing" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Yin" maps to the Tiger in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years. The Fire element amplifies the Tiger's natural intensity, making 1986 Tigers some of the most driven, present, and assertive of any Tiger year.
