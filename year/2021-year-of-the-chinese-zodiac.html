2021 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2021 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Xin Chou

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2021 was the year of Xin Chou. "Xin" stands for Yin Metal in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Chou" maps to the Ox in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2021 lunar year kicked off on Feb 12, 2021 and wrapped up on Jan 31, 2022. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Metal Ox. Born earlier in Feb 2021 -- before the 12 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yin Metal Energy

Xin Metal is Yin Metal -- inward, refined, and patient. It gives people born under its influence a quiet strength and a willingness to do the work others avoid. Metal anchors the Ox's steady nature with discipline and focus.

Big Events That Shaped 2021

2021 was the year America tried to turn the page. A mob stormed the Capitol, vaccines went into arms, and the US pulled out of its longest war. Here are five events that made the year.

Jan 6, 2021

Capitol Building Attacked

A mob of Trump supporters broke into the US Capitol while Congress was certifying the election. Five people died, 140 officers were injured, and it was the worst attack on the Capitol since the War of 1812. Four officers later died by suicide.

Jan 20, 2021

Biden Inaugurated 46th President

Biden was sworn in at a US Capitol ringed by 25,000 National Guard troops. Just 12 days earlier the building had been under attack. 40 million fewer viewers watched than in 2017.

Dec 14, 2020 - 2021

COVID Vaccines Roll Out

Pfizer shipped the first US-authorized vaccine on Dec 14, 2020. By April 19, 2021, 50% of US adults had at least one dose. The CDC approved vaccines for kids 5-11 on Nov 2, 2021.

July 23, 2021

Tokyo Olympics Held Without Fans

The first Summer Games postponed in Olympic history. Held a year late with no spectators at most venues. The US won 39 gold medals, second to China's 38, and the Tokyo organizing committee lost an estimated $15 billion.

Aug 15, 2021

Kabul Falls to Taliban

The Afghan government collapsed as the Taliban took Kabul just 11 days before the US planned to complete its withdrawal. 123,000 people were evacuated in 17 days -- the largest US airlift since the 1975 Vietnam fall of Saigon.
