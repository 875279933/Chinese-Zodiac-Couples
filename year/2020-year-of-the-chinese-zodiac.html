2020 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar don't follow the same rules. Here's how that affects the 2020 year of the Chinese zodiac.

The Year of Geng Zi

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2020 was the year of Geng Zi. "Geng" stands for Metal in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Zi" maps to the Rat in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 2020

The 2020 lunar year kicked off on Jan 25, 2020 and wrapped up on Feb 11, 2021. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Metal Rat. But if you were born earlier in Jan 2020 -- before the 25 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

It pushes things forward and gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and drive. Metal adds resolve and discipline. People born in this year are tougher and more focused than others of their sign, with an inner strength that keeps them on track no matter what.

2020 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 2020 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

2020 on the Western Calendar

2020 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Metal Rat's determined, resolute energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 2020 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 2020, the Metal Rat period ran from Jan 25, 2020 through Feb 11, 2021.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Jan 25 2020 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Rat. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.

Why This Distinction Matters

Big Events That Shaped 2020

2020 was the year everything went sideways. A pandemic shut down the world, a Minneapolis police officer killed George Floyd, and the US elected a new president. Here are the five events that defined the year.

March 11, 2020

WHO Declares COVID-19 a Pandemic

The World Health Organization made it official after the virus hit 114 countries and killed 4,000 people in just 12 weeks. By April, 90% of the world's students were out of school -- 1.6 billion kids in 191 countries.

May 25, 2020

George Floyd Killed in Minneapolis

Derek Chauvin knelt on Floyd's neck for 9 minutes and 29 seconds. The video went viral overnight and sparked protests in all 50 states. Minneapolis banned chokeholds within 11 days, and 25 states passed police reform laws by year's end.

Nov 3, 2020

Biden Defeats Trump

Joe Biden won 306 electoral votes and 81.3 million popular votes, both records. Trump refused to concede for weeks, then refused to attend Biden's inauguration on Jan 20, 2021 -- the first outgoing president to skip since 1869.

Aug 4, 2020

Beirut Explosion

2,750 tons of improperly stored ammonium nitrate blew up at the port, killing 218 people and injuring 7,000. The blast was felt in Cyprus, 120 miles away, and left 300,000 homeless in a city of 2 million.

May 30, 2020

SpaceX Crew Dragon Demo-2 Launches

NASA astronauts Doug Hurley and Bob Behnken lifted off from Kennedy Space Center on a Falcon 9. It was the first crewed orbital flight by a private company and the first American crewed launch from US soil since 2011.
