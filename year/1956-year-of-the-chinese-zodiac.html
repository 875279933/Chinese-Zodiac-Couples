1956 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 1956 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Bing Shen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1956 was the year of Bing Shen. "Bing" stands for Fire in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Shen" maps to the Monkey in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates for 1956

The 1956 lunar year kicked off on Feb 12, 1956 and wrapped up on Jan 30, 1957. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Fire Monkey. But if you were born earlier in Feb 1956 -- before the 12 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

How the Lunar Calendar Works

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. To keep pace with the solar year, a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Why Yang Fire Matters

The Bing Fire in the 1956 year of the Chinese zodiac is Yang Fire -- Yang energy is active, outward, and assertive. It pushes things forward and gives people born under its influence a natural confidence and drive. Fire cranks up the intensity. People born in this year have more drive, more passion, and more presence than others of their sign. They light up rooms and push hard for what they want.

1956 in the Gregorian Calendar

What does 1956 look like through the lens of the Western calendar, and why does the date cutoff matter for the Chinese zodiac?

1956 on the Western Calendar

1956 on the Gregorian calendar is straightforward -- January 1 through December 31. But in Chinese culture, the year pulsed with the Fire Monkey's passionate, dynamic energy, shaping how people born that year see the world.

Why the Dates Don't Line Up

The 1956 year of the Chinese zodiac doesn't run from January 1 to December 31. Because Chinese New Year shifts each year, the zodiac year straddles two Gregorian years. For 1956, the Fire Monkey period ran from Feb 12, 1956 through Jan 30, 1957.

Check Your Birthdate Carefully

This matters more than most people realize. Born Feb 12 1956 or earlier? You're the previous sign, not a Monkey. The lunar calendar dates are the ones that count -- always double-check if your birthday falls near the Chinese New Year cutoff.

Born April 18 in Biloxi, Mississippi. Roberts was nominated for the Best Actor Oscar for Star 80 (1983) at age 26, then struggled with addiction before rebuilding his career with over 700 credits. His sister is Julia Roberts and his daughter is Emma Roberts.
