1993 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 1993 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Gui You

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 1993 was the year of Gui You. "Gui" stands for Water in the Heavenly Stems system, while "You" maps to the Rooster in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 1993 lunar year kicked off on Jan 23, 1993 and wrapped up on Feb 9, 1994. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Water Rooster. But if you were born earlier in Jan 1993 -- before the 23 -- you'd actually be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases. Standard year runs about 354 days; a leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years.

Yin Water Energy

Gui Water is Yin Water -- the dew, the mist, the still pool. It gives people born under its influence an inner richness and a quiet depth that doesn't always show on the surface.
