2024 Lunar Calendar Explained

The Chinese lunar calendar and the Gregorian calendar run on different tracks. That gap is why the 2024 year of the Chinese zodiac starts and ends on different dates than the Western calendar suggests.

The Year of Jia Chen

In the traditional Chinese lunar calendar, 2024 was the year of Jia Chen. "Jia" stands for Yang Wood in the Heavenly Stems system, while "Chen" maps to the Dragon in the Earthly Branches. This particular pairing only rolls around once every 60 years.

Lunar Year Dates

The 2024 lunar year kicked off on Feb 10, 2024 and wrapped up on Jan 28, 2025. If your birthday falls in that window, you're a Wood Dragon. Born earlier in Feb 2024 -- before the 10 -- you'd be the previous zodiac sign instead.

Lunar System

The Chinese lunar calendar tracks the moon's phases, with each month starting on the new moon. A standard lunar year runs about 354 days across 12 months. A leap month gets tacked on roughly every three years -- which is why Chinese New Year jumps around on the Western calendar.

Yang Wood Energy

Jia Wood is Yang Wood -- a tall tree reaching for the sun. It gives people born under its influence a strong growth mindset and a generous, expansive outlook. Wood shapes the Dragon's fire into something that builds lasting things.

Big Events That Shaped 2024

2024 had a total solar eclipse cross America, a Trump-Biden rematch, and a major airline meltdown. Here are five events that mattered.

April 8, 2024

Total Solar Eclipse Crosses US

The path of totality ran from Texas to Maine, with 32 million Americans living in the path and another 150 million within a 4-hour drive. Totality in some places lasted 4 minutes 28 seconds, the longest in the US since 1806.

Nov 5, 2024

Trump Wins 2024 Election

Donald Trump defeated Kamala Harris 312-226 in the Electoral College and won the popular vote 50%-48.3%. Trump flipped all 7 swing states and became the first convicted felon elected president. He was sworn in on Jan 20, 2025.

July 19, 2024

CrowdStrike Outage Grounds Flights

A faulty CrowdStrike security update crashed 8.5 million Windows computers worldwide. Delta was hit hardest, canceling 7,000 flights over 5 days and losing $500 million. The outage also took down 911 services in multiple US states.

June 5, 2024

Boeing Starliner Crew Launch

NASA astronauts Butch Wilmore and Suni Williams launched on Boeing's Starliner for an 8-day mission. Helium leaks and thruster failures forced NASA to keep them on the ISS for 9 months instead. They returned via SpaceX Crew Dragon on Feb 25, 2025.

Sept 26, 2024

Hurricane Helene

Helene came ashore in Florida as a Cat 4 then stalled over western North Carolina. The storm killed 230 people and dropped 30+ inches of rain, triggering mudslides in Asheville. It was the deadliest hurricane to hit the US mainland since Katrina.
