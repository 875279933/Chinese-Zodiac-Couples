What is the 1947 Chinese zodiac sign?

The 1947 Chinese zodiac sign is the Pig, paired with the Fire element. Anyone born between Jan 22, 1947 and Feb 9, 1948 on the Gregorian calendar falls under this sign.

Why does the 1947 Pig have a different personality from other Pig years?

Each Pig year cycles through all five elements over a 60-year span. The 1947 Pig carries Fire energy on top of the base Pig personality, which shifts the temperature. The 1947 cohort skews faster, louder, more visible.

How does the lunar calendar affect the 1947 zodiac year?

The 1947 zodiac year runs from Jan 22, 1947 to Feb 9, 1948 on the Gregorian calendar. Anyone born inside that window is a 1947 Pig. Anyone born in the days before Jan 22, 1947 falls under the previous zodiac sign -- the date cutoff matters more than the calendar year alone.
