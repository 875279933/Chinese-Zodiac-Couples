What Shapes the 1958 Dog Personality

Two forces stack on top of each other in 1958: the Earth element, and the base Dog personality. The combination is what makes the 1958 cohort different from a Dog born under any other element.

01

The Earth Element on Top

Earth adds a quiet, grounded layer to the Dog's usual fire. The result is a cohort that is loyal, slow to act, and very hard to argue out of a position once they take it.

02

The Dog Base Underneath

Loyal, watchful, and slow to trust -- once they let you in, they are in for good. The base Dog reads every room before they act on it.

03

What the Combination Produces

The 1958 Dog is a layered read. Earth energy sets the temperature, and the base Dog personality sets the defaults. When the two align, the cohort comes across as steady, grounded, and slow to start. The 1958 Chinese zodiac sign is the Dog, paired with the Earth element. In the 60-year cycle, 1958 maps to the Wu stem and the Xu branch, where Earth is the element and Dog is the animal. The sign applies to anyone born between Feb 18, 1958 and Feb 7, 1959 on the Gregorian calendar.

Why does the 1958 Dog read differently from other Dog years?

Each Dog year rotates through all five elements over 60 years. The 1958 cohort carries Earth energy on top of the base Dog personality, which changes the temperature. The 1958 group tends to be patient, deliberate, hard to rush.
