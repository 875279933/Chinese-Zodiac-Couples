Personality Traits

Dragons don't do small. Big plans, big voice, big presence - they take up space and they like it that way. It's not arrogance (well, not always) - they just have a lot going on and they want you to know about it.

Charismatic

Dragons walk in and people look up. It's not a thing they practice - they're just born with that pull. They tell a story and the room leans in. Most Dragons learn to take it easy with this; some never do.

Ambitious

Small goals aren't in a Dragon's vocabulary. They've got the kind of ambition that either builds an empire or burns out spectacularly. Most of the time, it's somewhere in the middle - and even the middle is more than most people aim for.

Original Thinker

Dragons don't follow the playbook. They toss it. New ways of doing things, weird combinations, approaches nobody else would try - that's their comfort zone. Sometimes it works, sometimes it doesn't, but it's never boring.

High Energy

Dragons are powered by something most people don't have. They work late, wake up early, and have opinions about both. The energy is contagious too - being around a Dragon at full speed can pull you out of a rut.

Born Leader

Dragons don't get elected. They just end up in charge, and somehow everyone goes with it. They've got the vision, the voice, the nerve to make the call nobody else wants to. The leadership thing isn't a skill they learned - it's a setting they're born with.

Inspiring

There's a reason people stick around a Dragon. They make you want to do more, try harder, reach further. Even when a Dragon's being annoying, they're usually pointing at something worth aiming for.

Put the Dragon's traits together and you've got someone who doesn't do small dreams. They want to matter, and they usually find a way. The flip side of all that energy is the burnt-out Dragon who can't slow down. The healthiest version knows when to dial it back - and that's the one everyone wants to be around.

Compatibility & Matches

Dragons need partners who can match their scale. Someone small-thinking won't last long - look for ambition, confidence, and the patience to handle a lot of personality.
