Personality Traits

Monkeys are the ones who figure the thing out first, crack the joke at the right time, and somehow end up charming the room by the end of the night. Smart, fast, and never boring.

Quick on the Uptake

Monkeys pick things up fast. Joke, system, person - they read it quick and they remember it. Give a Monkey a problem and they'll have three workable answers before the rest of the table has finished reading the question.

Insanely Curious

Monkeys want to know how stuff works. Why the sky looks that way, what's in the new gadget, the backstory on the person across the room. The world is full of questions, and a Monkey is always working on a few at once.

Playful

Monkeys don't take life too seriously, and they don't want the people around them to either. Pranks, banter, the kind of chaos that loosens up a stiff room - that's the Monkey in their natural habitat.

Big-Brained

Monkeys find shortcuts, workarounds, and weird new angles nobody else tried. They don't do things the way they were taught if the taught way is dumb. Sometimes it works, sometimes it's a mess, but it's always original.

Goes with the Flow

Plans change, people change, the weather changes - a Monkey rolls with it. New city, new job, last-minute trip - they don't freak out. They just start figuring out the new game.

Center of the Room

Monkeys can hold a crowd. Stories, impressions, the perfect one-liner - they bring it. People invite Monkeys places because they know the night will be better with one around.

Monkeys are the kind of person who makes everyone else a little more interesting. They're the friend who knows the shortcut, the joke that lands, the story that gets retold. The risk is they scatter themselves: a Monkey with too many open tabs is a tired Monkey. The best version picks a few things and actually finishes them.
