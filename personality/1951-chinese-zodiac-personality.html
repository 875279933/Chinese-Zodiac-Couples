What is the 1951 Chinese zodiac sign?

The 1951 Chinese zodiac sign is the Rabbit, paired with the Metal element. In the traditional 60-year cycle, 1951 corresponds to the Xin stem and the Mao branch, where Metal represents the element and the Rabbit represents the animal of the year. Anyone born between Jan 24, 1951 and Feb 10, 1952 on the Gregorian calendar falls under this sign.

Why does the 1951 Rabbit have a different personality from other Rabbit years?

Each Rabbit year cycles through all five elements over a 60-year span. The 1951 Rabbit carries Metal energy on top of the base Rabbit personality, which shifts the temperature. The 1951 cohort skews sharper, more structured, more decisive.

The 1951 zodiac year runs from Jan 24, 1951 to Feb 10, 1952 on the Gregorian calendar. Anyone born inside that window is a 1951 Rabbit. Anyone born in the days before Jan 24, 1951 falls under the previous zodiac sign -- the date cutoff matters more than the calendar year alone.
