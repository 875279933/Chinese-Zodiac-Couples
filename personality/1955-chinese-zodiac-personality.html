What is the 1955 Chinese zodiac sign?

The 1955 Chinese zodiac sign is the Goat, paired with the Wood element. In the traditional 60-year cycle, 1955 corresponds to the Yi stem and the Wei branch, where Wood represents the element and the Goat represents the animal of the year. Anyone born between Jan 24, 1955 and Feb 11, 1956 on the Gregorian calendar falls under this sign.

Why does the 1955 Goat have a different personality from other Goat years?

Each Goat year cycles through all five elements over a 60-year span. The 1955 Goat carries Wood energy on top of the base Goat personality, which shifts the temperature. The 1955 cohort skews steadier, more growth-focused, more patient.

How does the lunar calendar affect the 1955 zodiac year?

The 1955 zodiac year runs from Jan 24, 1955 to Feb 11, 1956 on the Gregorian calendar. Anyone born inside that window is a 1955 Goat. Anyone born in the days before Jan 24, 1955 falls under the previous zodiac sign -- the date cutoff matters more than the calendar year alone.
