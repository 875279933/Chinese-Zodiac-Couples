What is the 1954 Chinese zodiac sign?

The 1954 Chinese zodiac sign is the Horse, paired with the Wood element. In the traditional 60-year cycle, 1954 corresponds to the Jia stem and the Wu branch, where Wood represents the element and the Horse represents the animal of the year. Anyone born between Feb 3, 1954 and Jan 23, 1955 on the Gregorian calendar falls under this sign.

Why does the 1954 Horse have a different personality from other Horse years?

Each Horse year cycles through all five elements over a 60-year span. The 1954 Horse carries Wood energy on top of the base Horse personality, which shifts the temperature. The 1954 cohort skews steadier, more growth-focused, more patient.

How does the lunar calendar affect the 1954 zodiac year?

The 1954 zodiac year runs from Feb 3, 1954 to Jan 23, 1955 on the Gregorian calendar. Anyone born inside that window is a 1954 Horse. Anyone born in the days before Feb 3, 1954 falls under the previous zodiac sign -- the date cutoff matters more than the calendar year alone.
