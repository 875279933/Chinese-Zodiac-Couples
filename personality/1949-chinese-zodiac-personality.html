02

The Ox Base Underneath

The Ox is the slow, deliberate backbone of the zodiac. Patient, dependable, and capable of outworking almost everyone in the room.

03

Common Questions About the 1949 Ox

Short, direct answers to the questions readers ask most about the 1949 Chinese zodiac sign.

What is the 1949 Chinese zodiac sign?

The 1949 Chinese zodiac sign is the Ox, paired with the Earth element. In the traditional 60-year cycle, 1949 corresponds to the Ji stem and the Chou branch, where Earth represents the element and the Ox represents the animal of the year. Anyone born between Jan 29, 1949 and Feb 5, 1950 on the Gregorian calendar falls under this sign.

Why does the 1949 Ox have a different personality from other Ox years?

Each Ox year cycles through all five elements over a 60-year span. The 1949 Ox carries Earth energy on top of the base Ox personality, which shifts the temperature. The 1949 cohort skews slower, more grounded, longer-term.

Which sign is most compatible with the 1949 Ox?

The strongest matches for the 1949 Ox are Rat, Snake, Rooster, based on shared element, complementary traits, and cycle position. The toughest matches are Goat, Horse, Dragon, where the energy runs in different directions. The full scoring chart is on this page.

What are the lucky colors and numbers for the 1949 Ox?

The Earth element favors Yellow, Olive, Tan. The most commonly listed lucky numbers for the 1949 cohort are 5, 8, 1. Lucky stone: Agate, Emerald. Auspicious direction: Center. These come from the traditional five-elements framework and the Earth element's seasonal associations.

How does the lunar calendar affect the 1949 zodiac year?

The 1949 zodiac year runs from Jan 29, 1949 to Feb 5, 1950 on the Gregorian calendar. Anyone born inside that window is a 1949 Ox. Anyone born in the days before Jan 29, 1949 falls under the previous zodiac sign -- the date cutoff matters more than the calendar year alone.
