Why the 1949 Ox Reads the Way They Do

Three drivers, working together. None of them stands alone -- the 1949 cohort gets its flavor from the way these three layers stack.

  • Stamina that outlasts the room. The Ox is the last one standing on a long project. They do not need applause -- they need the work to be done right.
