Personality Traits

Dogs are the people you text at 2am - and the ones who'd actually answer. They're loyal almost to a fault, and they want the people in their life to be straight with them. No games, no fakery.

Loyal to a Fault

Dogs don't drop people. The friend from high school, the ex they still check on, the cousin who keeps messing up - the Dog keeps showing up. They take "ride or die" literally, and they expect the same in return. When they don't get it, it stings for a long time.

Honest to the Bone

Dogs will tell you when your haircut is bad. They're not trying to be harsh - they just think lying is worse. That directness is part of why people trust them with the things they'd never say out loud.

Guardian

Dogs notice when something's off. The friend in a bad relationship, the kid being mistreated, the person at the party who looks uncomfortable - the Dog clocks it and steps in. It's not heroic, it's just what they do.

Fair-Minded

Dogs believe in doing things the right way, even when the wrong way is easier. They're the ones who flag the unfair policy, who split the bill exactly, who won't take credit for someone else's work. Quiet, solid integrity.

Down-to-Earth

Dogs don't do flash. They want what works, what's real, what lasts. New fad, new trend, new person trying too hard - the Dog sees right through it. They'd rather have one good thing than five shiny ones.

Steady Under Pressure

When things fall apart, the Dog is the one who isn't panicking. Not because they don't care - they do - but they handle the crisis and break down later, in private. People lean on Dogs a lot, and Dogs usually let them.

Dogs are the kind of person everyone should have in their corner - and few people are lucky enough to have. They give more than they ask for, and they remember more than they let on. The risk is they pick up too many other people's weight; the healthiest Dog learns to put some of it down without feeling guilty.
