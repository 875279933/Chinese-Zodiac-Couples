What is the 1969 Chinese zodiac sign?

The 1969 Chinese zodiac sign is the Rooster, paired with the Earth element. In the 60-year cycle, 1969 maps to the Ji stem and the You branch, where Earth is the element and Rooster is the animal. The sign applies to anyone born between Feb 17, 1969 and Feb 5, 1970 on the Gregorian calendar.

Why does the 1969 Rooster read differently from other Rooster years?

Each Rooster year rotates through all five elements over 60 years. The 1969 cohort carries Earth energy on top of the base Rooster personality, which changes the temperature. The 1969 group tends to be patient, deliberate, hard to rush.

How does the lunar calendar affect the 1969 zodiac year?

The 1969 zodiac year runs from Feb 17, 1969 to Feb 5, 1970 on the Gregorian calendar. Anyone born inside that window is a 1969 Rooster. Anyone born in the days before Feb 17, 1969 is the previous zodiac sign -- the cutoff matters more than the calendar year alone.

How does the 1969 Rooster handle work and career?

The 1969 Rooster cohort tends to do best in work that rewards their natural style. They are not built for every job. The strengths show up in roles that match their element and their sign, and the friction shows up in roles that fight both.
