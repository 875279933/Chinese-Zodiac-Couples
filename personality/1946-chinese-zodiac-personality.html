What is the 1946 Chinese zodiac sign?

The 1946 Chinese zodiac sign is the Dog, paired with the Fire element. In the traditional 60-year cycle, 1946 corresponds to the Bing stem and the Xu branch, where Fire represents the element and the Dog represents the animal of the year. Anyone born between Feb 2, 1946 and Jan 21, 1947 on the Gregorian calendar falls under this sign.

Why does the 1946 Dog have a different personality from other Dog years?

Each Dog year cycles through all five elements over a 60-year span. The 1946 Dog carries Fire energy on top of the base Dog personality, which shifts the temperature. The 1946 cohort skews faster, louder, more visible.

The strongest matches for the 1946 Dog are Rabbit, Tiger, Horse, based on shared element, complementary traits, and cycle position. The toughest matches are Dragon, Goat, Rooster, where the energy runs in different directions. The full scoring chart is on this page.

How does the lunar calendar affect the 1946 zodiac year?

The 1946 zodiac year runs from Feb 2, 1946 to Jan 21, 1947 on the Gregorian calendar. Anyone born inside that window is a 1946 Dog. Anyone born in the days before Feb 2, 1946 falls under the previous zodiac sign -- the date cutoff matters more than the calendar year alone.
