Personality Traits

You know those people who always seem to have a clever workaround for everything? Chances are, they're a Rat. Quick minds, sharp instincts, and a real talent for getting out of tight spots - that's what you're dealing with here.

Quick-Witted

Rats catch on fast. Like, embarrassingly fast sometimes. They're the ones finishing your sentence at the meeting, or figuring out the new software before anyone else even opens the manual. Boredom hits them quickly, so they keep their brain busy with something.

Resourceful

Give a Rat three odds and they'll find a fourth option. They're naturals at making something out of nothing, and they get a real kick out of solving problems others have given up on. Budget tight? They already know three ways around it.

Ambitious

Rats don't sit around waiting for stuff to fall in their lap. They've got goals, they've got plans, and they're working them. That hustle usually pays off, too - Rats tend to climb ladders most people didn't even notice.

Financially Savvy

Watch a Rat with their money and you'll learn something. They're careful, they save, and they're annoyingly good at spotting a bad deal. A Rat's wallet has padding even when times are tight.

Charming

Rats work a room without breaking a sweat. There's something about the way they talk - warm, a little playful - that just draws people in. They make friends easily and keep them, too.

Energetic

Sitting still isn't really a Rat thing. They're juggling projects, hopping between ideas, always moving. Tired? They don't really get that word, or at least they don't admit to it.

Put it all together and the Rat reads as someone who's sharp, busy, and a step ahead. They've got the energy of a project always in motion - not chaotic, just always up to something. The shadow side is the same: a Rat who's off their game gets restless, sharp-tongued, hard to be around. The good days are when that quick mind has somewhere to go.
