Personality Traits

Tigers walk into a room and the room notices. Big energy, big opinions, big plans. They don't do small talk well, but if you want someone who'll actually go after the thing, you're looking at a Tiger.

Brave

Tigers don't flinch at the scary stuff. They volunteer for the hard projects, walk into the awkward conversations, take the road nobody else wants. Some of that is courage, some of it is just not knowing any better - either way, it works out.

Confident

A Tiger's got this quiet (or not-so-quiet) certainty about them. They trust their own take, and most of the time that take turns out to be right. Even when it isn't, they commit fully - and that alone is impressive.

Passionate

Halfway isn't a word Tigers use. They love hard, work hard, fight hard. Sometimes that intensity is a lot for the people around them - but it's never faked. You always know where you stand with a Tiger.

Adventurous

Routine is a Tiger's enemy. They'd rather wing it, take the weird detour, try the new thing. Trips get planned an hour before departure, hobbies change monthly, and dinner tonight might be in a different country next month. It's chaos, but it's their chaos.

Leader

Tigers don't really campaign for leadership - it just lands on them. People look to them in a group, defer to their calls, follow them into whatever they're doing. The Tiger just assumes the role, and somehow everyone else goes along.

Energetic

Tigers run hot. They can pull an all-nighter and still want to hit the gym in the morning. The energy isn't always convenient for the people around them, but it's the kind of energy that pulls a group out of a slump.

All in all, the Tiger reads as someone with nerve. They walk into situations most people are still planning for. Whether you find that thrilling or exhausting depends on who you ask. The Tiger doesn't really care either way - they're already off chasing the next thing.
