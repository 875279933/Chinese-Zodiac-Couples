The 1960 Chinese zodiac sign is the Rat, paired with the Metal element. In the 60-year cycle, 1960 maps to the Geng stem and the Zi branch, where Metal is the element and Rat is the animal. The sign applies to anyone born between Jan 28, 1960 and Feb 14, 1961 on the Gregorian calendar.

Each Rat year rotates through all five elements over 60 years. The 1960 cohort carries Metal energy on top of the base Rat personality, which changes the temperature. The 1960 group tends to be cleaner, more direct, more exacting.
