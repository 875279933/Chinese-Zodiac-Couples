Personality Traits

Pigs are the warm ones. The host who makes sure everyone's eaten, the friend who remembers the little things, the person who shows up when it matters. They don't try to be liked - it just happens.

Open-Handed

Pigs give. Not to show off, not for credit - they just see someone who needs something and they handle it. The extra portion, the ride home, the small loan they know they won't get back. They've got a lot of heart, and they spend it freely.

Soft with People

Pigs assume the best. They give people the benefit of the doubt, the second chance, the room to grow. Sometimes that gets them hurt, but they don't really know how to be any other way - and they wouldn't want to be.

Glass-Half-Full

Pigs look for the bright side, even when it's a stretch. Things usually do work out for them, partly because they keep going when other people would have given up. That optimism isn't naive - it's chosen, every day.

Hates Drama

Pigs would rather smooth things over than win the argument. They walk away from fights, defuse tense rooms, and try to find the version where everyone gets to be okay. It's a quiet strength that doesn't always get the credit it deserves.

Shows Up and Stays

Pigs aren't flashy, but they get it done. Steady worker, reliable friend, the one who remembers the appointment a year from now. They don't burn hot - they just keep going, day after day, and that adds up to a lot.

Gravitates to People

Pigs are social without trying. They draw people in - the good host, the easy laugh, the friend you call when something good happens. Pigs don't do lonely, and they don't let the people they love be lonely either.

Pigs are the kind of person you'd want to be stuck on a desert island with. They feed you, they keep the mood up, and they don't let things get ugly. The downside is they often give more than they get back, and they mistake kindness for trust a little too easily. The smartest Pig learns to spot who's worth the open hand and who isn't - and saves some of that warmth for themselves.

Compatibility & Matches
