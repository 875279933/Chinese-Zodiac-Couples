Personality Traits

Rabbits are the calm in everyone's storm. Soft-spoken, thoughtful, and somehow always knowing the right thing to say. They're not the loudest in the room, but they're usually the one you want sitting next to you.

Gentle

Rabbits don't have a sharp edge. The way they talk, the way they handle people, the way they let conflicts cool off - it's all soft. Being around them feels like the volume got turned down on a stressful day.

Compassionate

A Rabbit picks up on what you're feeling before you say it. They're the friend who texts to check in when you're quiet, who notices the small stuff. They don't just sympathize - they actually feel it with you.

Homebody at Heart

Rabbits will take a cozy night in over almost any big plan. They've got their space just the way they like it, their routines, their people close by. Their idea of a good time is a quiet house and good food.

Artistic

There's an eye for beauty in most Rabbits. They notice colors, textures, the way light falls. A lot of them end up doing something creative, even if it's just how they dress or arrange their home. They make things prettier just by being there.

Peacekeeper

Group chat getting heated? Send in a Rabbit. They have this way of finding the middle ground that doesn't sound fake. People trust a Rabbit to be fair, and they take that role seriously.

Careful

Rabbits don't jump first and ask questions later. They think it through, they look at the angles, they wait until they feel safe. That caution can look like overthinking sometimes, but it also means they rarely regret the big choices.

Take it all in and the Rabbit comes across as someone who feels things deeply and acts on them carefully. They won't bulldoze their way through life, but they get where they're going. The Rabbit's challenge is the same as its strength: when the world feels too loud, they go quiet - sometimes too quiet. The right balance makes them quietly impressive.

Compatibility & Matches

Rabbits want a partner who respects the quiet life. Look for someone who doesn't mistake softness for weakness, and who brings their own kind of steady to the relationship.
