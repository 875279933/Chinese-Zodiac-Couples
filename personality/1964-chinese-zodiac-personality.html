The 1964 Chinese zodiac sign is the Dragon, paired with the Wood element. In the 60-year cycle, 1964 maps to the Jia stem and the Chen branch, where Wood is the element and Dragon is the animal. The sign applies to anyone born between Feb 13, 1964 and Feb 1, 1965 on the Gregorian calendar.

Why does the 1964 Dragon read differently from other Dragon years?

Each Dragon year rotates through all five elements over 60 years. The 1964 cohort carries Wood energy on top of the base Dragon personality, which changes the temperature. The 1964 group tends to be ambitious, collaborative, and durable.

The 1964 zodiac year runs from Feb 13, 1964 to Feb 1, 1965 on the Gregorian calendar. Anyone born inside that window is a 1964 Dragon. Anyone born in the days before Feb 13, 1964 is the previous zodiac sign -- the cutoff matters more than the calendar year alone.
