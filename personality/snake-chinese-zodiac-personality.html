Personality Traits

Snakes are the quiet ones in the corner who see everything. They don't say much, but when they do, it's usually the thing you needed to hear. Calm, sharp, and a little hard to read - that's the Snake in a sentence.

Intuitive

Snakes read rooms before they walk into them. They've got a sixth sense for what's about to happen, who's lying, what's not being said. They trust that gut feeling and it usually pays off - they look lucky, but it's actually good radar.

Mysterious

Snakes don't give you all of themselves at once. They let you in slowly, layer by layer. That's not a game to them - they just value what's private. The mystery isn't fake; you've just got to stick around long enough to earn the full story.

Sharp

Snakes think before they speak, and they notice what others miss. Three moves ahead is their natural resting place. In a meeting, they're the ones quietly seeing the whole board while everyone else argues about one square.

Refined

Snakes have taste. The good stuff appeals to them - nice clothes, well-made things, considered surroundings. It's not snobbery, just a high bar. They'll choose one great thing over three okay things every time.

Patient Hunter

Snakes don't rush. They wait, watch, then move - and by the time they move, the outcome is pretty much decided. It's not lazy; it's strategic. They outlast people who try to sprint through everything.

Deep Thinker

Snakes live in their head a lot. They'll chew on a question for days before answering, and the answer is usually worth the wait. They like the weird, philosophical stuff - what life means, why people do what they do, what's actually going on under the surface.

Snakes are a study in contrasts - quiet but intense, private but warm, gentle but sharp. They don't let everyone see all of that, and the ones who stick around long enough usually find the payoff is worth the wait. They're not cold, just selective with their warmth.
