The 1963 Chinese zodiac sign is the Rabbit, paired with the Water element. In the 60-year cycle, 1963 maps to the Gui stem and the Mao branch, where Water is the element and Rabbit is the animal. The sign applies to anyone born between Jan 25, 1963 and Feb 12, 1964 on the Gregorian calendar.

Each Rabbit year rotates through all five elements over 60 years. The 1963 cohort carries Water energy on top of the base Rabbit personality, which changes the temperature. The 1963 group tends to be quieter, more reflective, more observant.
