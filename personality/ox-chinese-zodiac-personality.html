Personality Traits

Ox people don't move fast, but they move. Slow and steady is their whole thing, and honestly it works. Show up, put in the work, don't make a fuss - that's the Ox way of getting stuff done.

Dependable

If an Ox tells you they'll be there at 7, they're pulling up at 6:55. They're not flashy about it, but you can set your watch by them. The kind of friend who answers the phone at 2am, no questions asked.

Hardworking

Oxen are the last ones to leave the office and the first ones back. They don't really know how to do something halfway. It's a slow burn, but the results speak for themselves over time.

Patient

Where other signs lose their cool, the Ox is still sipping coffee. They get that most things worth having take a while. Watching an Ox handle a crisis is kind of calming - they just don't panic.

Traditional

Oxen like things done the way they've always worked. Family dinners, Sunday routines, the right way to fold a shirt - these matter to them. They're not stuck in the past, they just see no reason to fix what isn't broken.

Loyal

Cross an Ox once and you'll know it. Stay on their good side, and you've got a friend for life. They take relationships seriously - family, partners, old coworkers from twenty years ago. Loyalty isn't a word for them, it's a default setting.

Determined

Once an Ox decides something, that's pretty much it. They've got that quiet stubborn streak that carries them through the long, boring middle parts of any project. The flashy start is easy - the Ox finishes.

Strip away the slow-and-steady talk and the Ox is just a person who finishes what they start. They don't need the spotlight, don't need applause. They need a job done well and the time to do it. A tired Ox looks like anyone else - but a working Ox is a different kind of impressive, even if they'd never call it that themselves.
