Leo Soulmate Questions Worth Asking

Who is Leo most compatible with?

Aries and Sagittarius are the strongest matches. They share Leo fire and Leo honesty. A Leo soulmate from these signs tends to last the longest.

What does Leo need in love?

Attention, loyalty, and a partner with their own life. Leo wants to be admired but also challenged, and the right Leo soulmate knows the difference.

Is Leo loyal?

When they commit, yes. A loyal Leo soulmate will defend the relationship in public and protect the partner in private. Betrayal is one of the few things Leo does not forgive.

How does Leo show love?

Through effort and grand gestures. Leo plans the trip, throws the party, and writes the note. A Leo soulmate should not mistake the show for the substance.

Can Leo date a water sign?

Yes, especially Cancer, Scorpio, and Pisces. A Leo soulmate from these signs brings emotional depth that fire signs do not always reach on their own.
