What Is a Soulmate Test?

A soulmate test compares two zodiac signs to see how well they match. It looks at shared elements, personality traits, values, and lifestyle habits to give you a clear picture of your connection. Plenty of people use it as a fun way to explore relationship dynamics and figure out what makes two signs click.

How the Compatibility Score Works

Our calculator breaks compatibility into four areas. Spiritual connection and shared values each carry the most weight at 35%. Lifestyle fit matters too at 20%, while growth potential rounds it out at 10%. This gives you a balanced view of where you align and where you might need to compromise.

Why Elements Matter

Every sign belongs to one of four elements: fire, earth, air, or water. Fire signs bring passion and energy. Earth signs offer stability and practicality. Air signs keep things intellectual and social. Water signs add emotional depth and intuition. Signs from compatible elements usually find it easier to understand each other.

Fire Signs: Aries, Leo, Sagittarius

These signs are bold, spontaneous, and full of life. They pair well with other fire signs or air signs who can match their energy and keep up with their adventurous spirit.

Earth Signs: Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn

Grounded and reliable, earth signs value loyalty and long-term security. They connect best with other earth signs or water signs who appreciate their steady, nurturing nature.

Air Signs: Gemini, Libra, Aquarius

Social and curious, air signs love deep conversations and new ideas. They thrive with other air signs or fire signs who bring excitement and shared enthusiasm.

Water Signs: Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces

Emotional and intuitive, water signs seek meaningful bonds. They feel most at home with other water signs or earth signs who provide the safety and consistency they crave.
