Orc Naming Traditions

Orcs in fantasy have always been about strength, honor, and clan pride. Names in Warcraft, D&D, and other fantasy settings tend to sound guttural and powerful - lots of hard consonants and deep vowels. Our orc name generator draws from all of that.

Male orc names usually feature strong sounds like Grom, Duro, Orgrim, and Kargath. They often reference battle, iron, blood, and leadership. Female orc names keep that fierce energy but tend to end in -a, -ah, or -sha, giving them a distinct warrior-woman feel. The orc name generator keeps these traditions in mind when creating new names.
