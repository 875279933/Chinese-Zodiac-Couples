Aries Male Compatibility in One Read

An Aries man runs hottest with fire signs (Leo, Sagittarius) and most talkative with air signs (Gemini, Aquarius). Earth and water pairings ask him to slow down, plan more, and feel before he acts, which is rarely his default. Leo at 91% sits at the easy end of the chart; Virgo at 31% sits at the hard end. Most Aries men land somewhere in between, and the right partner is whoever can match the energy without flinching at the blunt edge.

Aries Male Compatibility FAQ

Quick answers to the questions readers most often ask about Leo male compatibility, from the strongest match to the toughest pairing on the chart.

Which sign is the best match for an Aries man?

Leo is the strongest match for an Aries man at 91% overall. The pairing is unbreakable, loud, and built on shared fire-sign energy. Sagittarius also lands at 91%, with Gemini (84%) close behind.

Which sign is the worst match for an Aries man?

Virgo is the trickiest match for an Aries man at 31% overall. Scorpio (34%) and Capricorn (47%) follow. None of these pairings are impossible — they just take more translation than the top of the chart.

Does an Aries man get along with another Aries?

Aries man + Aries woman sits at 79% overall. The chemistry is instant and the energy is high, but two cardinal fire signs can also turn a small argument into a big one without warning.

What do the six sub-scores mean?

The six columns are mutual affection, lasting bond, friendship, love, marriage, and family. They break the overall score into the parts of a real relationship — not just the chemistry, but also the day-to-day, the in-laws, and the long run.

Do elements matter for Aries male compatibility?

Yes. Fire signs (Leo, Sagittarius) average 87% with Aries. Air signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) average 75%. Earth signs land at 48% and water signs at 50%. The pattern is clear: same fuel, easiest match.
