How the Male Compatibility Pages Read Together

The pattern is clean if you step back. Earth and water signs sit on the steady end of the chart — Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn, Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces all crack 90% with at least one partner. Air and fire signs trade the long-game for chemistry: their best pairings are loud and instant, and their tough pairings drag the most. Three pairings hit the 100% line across the male pages — Taurus with Virgo, Capricorn with Virgo, and Pisces with Cancer — and each one is a same-element match. Read the pages as a set rather than in isolation; the cross-chart trend is the part that holds up.

Male Zodiac Compatibility FAQ

Quick answers to the questions readers ask most when landing on the male compatibility hub.

What is male zodiac compatibility?

Male zodiac compatibility is a chart-based look at how a man of one sign pairs with a woman of any other sign. Each pairing is given an overall score and six sub-scores: mutual affection, lasting bond, friendship, love, marriage, and family. The 11 pages on this site walk through one sign at a time.

Which sign is the easiest match for a man overall?

Taurus men average the highest overall across all 12 pairings. The earth-on-earth pattern holds up, and the chart rarely drops into the patchy zone for them. Virgo and Capricorn women are usually the strongest partner for a Taurus man.

Which sign is the toughest match for a man overall?

Gemini men have the lowest average across the wheel, mostly because Scorpio sits at 27% on the chart. The Gemini pairing is conversational, not cold, but the depth column is hard for fixed water signs to climb.

What do the six sub-scores mean?

The six columns are mutual affection, lasting bond, friendship, love, marriage, and family. They break the overall score into the parts of a real relationship — not just the chemistry, but also the day-to-day, the in-laws, and the long run. The overall score is the straight average of those six.

Does this chart use the western zodiac?

Yes. The chart is built on the western tropical zodiac. It is one of two systems the site covers; the other is the Chinese zodiac, which lives under the zodiac and year pages.

How We Score Male Compatibility

A short note on where the numbers come from, so you can read the male compatibility chart with the right context.

Each match score is the average of six dimensions — mutual affection, lasting bond, friendship, love, marriage, and family. The numbers are drawn from long-running western compatibility literature and from our own scoring across thousands of paired sign combinations. They are a starting point for comparison, not a prediction. Two real people will always bend the chart in directions the data cannot see.
