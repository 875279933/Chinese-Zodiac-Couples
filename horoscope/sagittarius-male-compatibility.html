Sagittarius Male Compatibility FAQ

Quick answers to the questions readers most often ask about Leo male compatibility, from the strongest match to the toughest pairing on the chart.

Which sign is the best match for a Sagittarius man?

Leo and Aries are tied as the strongest matches for a Sagittarius man, both at 97% overall. The pairing is loud, loyal, and built on shared fire-sign energy. Libra (95%) sits just behind.

Which sign is the worst match for a Sagittarius man?

Cancer is the trickiest match for a Sagittarius man at 39% overall. Pisces and Taurus both land at 47%. None of these pairings are impossible — they just take more translation than the top of the chart.

Does a Sagittarius man get along with another Sagittarius?

Sagittarius man + Sagittarius woman sits at 88% overall. The energy is huge and the fun is constant. The risk is that two mutable fire signs can also agree to never put a plan on paper.

What do the six sub-scores mean?

The six columns are mutual affection, lasting bond, friendship, love, marriage, and family. They break the overall score into the parts of a real relationship — not just the chemistry, but also the day-to-day, the in-laws, and the long run.

Do elements matter for Sagittarius male compatibility?

Yes. Fire signs (Aries, Leo) average 94% with Sagittarius. Air signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) average 88%. Earth signs land at 56% and water signs at 51%. The pattern is clear: same fuel, easiest match.
