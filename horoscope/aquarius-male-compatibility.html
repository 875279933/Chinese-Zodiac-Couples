Aquarius Male Compatibility in One Read

An Aquarius man runs brightest with air signs (Gemini, Libra) and most exciting with fire signs (Sagittarius, Aries). Earth and water pairings ask him to commit to a schedule and feel on demand, which is rarely his default. Gemini at 91% sits at the easy end of the chart; Virgo at 34% sits at the hard end. Most Aquarius men land somewhere in between, and the right partner is whoever can let him be weird without trying to fix it.

Aquarius Male Compatibility FAQ

Quick answers to the questions readers most often ask about Leo male compatibility, from the strongest match to the toughest pairing on the chart.

Which sign is the best match for an Aquarius man?

Gemini is the strongest match for an Aquarius man at 91% overall. The pairing is light, cerebral, and built on shared air-sign energy. Libra (87%) and Sagittarius (85%) round out the top three.

Which sign is the worst match for an Aquarius man?

Virgo is the trickiest match for an Aquarius man at 34% overall. Cancer (46%) and Taurus (53%) follow. None of these pairings are impossible — they just take more translation than the top of the chart.

Does an Aquarius man get along with another Aquarius?

Aquarius man + Aquarius woman sits at 84% overall. The connection is instant and the weirdness is mutual. The risk is that two fixed air signs can also agree to never make a decision.

What do the six sub-scores mean?

The six columns are mutual affection, lasting bond, friendship, love, marriage, and family. They break the overall score into the parts of a real relationship — not just the chemistry, but also the day-to-day, the in-laws, and the long run.

Do elements matter for Aquarius male compatibility?

Yes. Air signs (Gemini, Libra) average 87% with Aquarius. Fire signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) average 80%. Water signs land at 61% and earth signs at 54%. The pattern is clear: same element, easiest match.
