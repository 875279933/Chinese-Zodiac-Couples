Scorpio Male Compatibility in One Read

A Scorpio man runs deepest with water signs (Pisces, Cancer) and steadiest with earth signs (Virgo, Capricorn). Air signs tend to keep things light, which can read as fickle to a Scorpio man, and fire signs want the spotlight he is quietly unwilling to share. Pisces at 96% sits at the easy end of the chart; Gemini at 33% sits at the hard end. Most Scorpio men land somewhere in between, and the right partner is whoever is willing to dive as deep as he does without needing a map.
