Gemini Male Compatibility FAQ

Quick answers to the questions readers most often ask about Leo male compatibility, from the strongest match to the toughest pairing on the chart.

Which sign is the best match for a Gemini man?

Libra is the strongest match for a Gemini man at 96% overall. The pairing is fast, fresh, and built on shared air-sign energy. Aquarius (92%) and Leo (83%) round out the top three.

Which sign is the worst match for a Gemini man?

Scorpio is the trickiest match for a Gemini man at 27% overall. Capricorn (40%) and Pisces (51%) follow. None of these pairings are impossible — they just take more translation than the top of the chart.

Does a Gemini man get along with another Gemini?

Gemini man + Gemini woman sits in the middle of the chart at 68% overall. The connection is instant and the conversation never stops, but two air signs can also outwit each other into a stalemate when feelings come up.

What do the six sub-scores mean?

The six columns are mutual affection, lasting bond, friendship, love, marriage, and family. They break the overall score into the parts of a real relationship — not just the chemistry, but also the day-to-day, the in-laws, and the long run.

Do elements matter for Gemini male compatibility?

Yes. Air signs (Libra, Aquarius) average 95% with Gemini. Fire signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) average 74%. Earth signs land at 48% and water signs at 46%. The pattern is clear: same element, easiest match.
