Capricorn Male Compatibility FAQ

Quick answers to the questions readers most often ask about Leo male compatibility, from the strongest match to the toughest pairing on the chart.

Which sign is the best match for a Capricorn man?

Virgo is the strongest match for a Capricorn man at 100% overall. The pairing is the steadiest on the wheel. Taurus (96%) and Scorpio (93%) round out the top three.

Which sign is the worst match for a Capricorn man?

Gemini is the trickiest match for a Capricorn man at 37% overall. Leo (45%) and Libra (51%) follow. None of these pairings are impossible — they just take more translation than the top of the chart.

Does a Capricorn man get along with another Capricorn?

Capricorn man + Capricorn woman sits at 76% overall. The bond is steady and the marriage column hits 100%. The risk is that two cardinal earth signs can also agree to never admit feelings.

What do the six sub-scores mean?

The six columns are mutual affection, lasting bond, friendship, love, marriage, and family. They break the overall score into the parts of a real relationship — not just the chemistry, but also the day-to-day, the in-laws, and the long run.

Do elements matter for Capricorn male compatibility?

Yes. Earth signs (Taurus, Virgo) average 91% with Capricorn. Water signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) average 86%. Air signs land at 51% and fire signs at 54%. The pattern is clear: same ground, easiest match.
