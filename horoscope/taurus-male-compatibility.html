Taurus Male Compatibility in One Read

A Taurus man runs steadiest with earth signs (Virgo, Capricorn) and warmest with water signs (Cancer, Pisces). Air and fire pairings ask him to fly, take risks, and shout, which is rarely his default. Virgo at 100% sits at the easy end of the chart; Sagittarius at 46% sits at the hard end. Most Taurus men land somewhere in between, and the right partner is whoever can sit still in the same room for a long, quiet evening without checking the door.

Taurus Male Compatibility FAQ

Quick answers to the questions readers most often ask about Leo male compatibility, from the strongest match to the toughest pairing on the chart.

Which sign is the best match for a Taurus man?

Virgo is the strongest match for a Taurus man at 100% overall. The pairing is famously steady. Cancer (94%) and Capricorn (87%) round out the top three.

Which sign is the worst match for a Taurus man?

Sagittarius is the trickiest match for a Taurus man at 46% overall. Leo and Aquarius both land at 51%. None of these pairings are impossible — they just take more translation than the top of the chart.

Does a Taurus man get along with another Taurus?

Taurus man + Taurus woman sits at 78% overall. The chemistry is calm and the household is rock-solid. The risk is that two fixed earth signs can also agree to never change anything, ever.

What do the six sub-scores mean?

The six columns are mutual affection, lasting bond, friendship, love, marriage, and family. They break the overall score into the parts of a real relationship — not just the chemistry, but also the day-to-day, the in-laws, and the long run.

Do elements matter for Taurus male compatibility?

Yes. Earth signs (Virgo, Capricorn) average 88% with Taurus. Water signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) average 78%. Air signs land at 55% and fire signs at 53%. The pattern is clear: same ground, easiest match.
