Libra Male Compatibility FAQ

Quick answers to the questions readers most often ask about Leo male compatibility, from the strongest match to the toughest pairing on the chart.

Which sign is the best match for a Libra man?

Gemini is the strongest match for a Libra man at 97% overall. The pairing is fast, fresh, and built on shared air-sign energy. Leo (87%) and Sagittarius (86%) round out the top three.

Which sign is the worst match for a Libra man?

Taurus is the trickiest match for a Libra man at 36% overall. Cancer (52%) and Pisces (53%) follow. None of these pairings are impossible — they just take more translation than the top of the chart.

Does a Libra man get along with another Libra?

Libra man + Libra woman sits at 72% overall. The connection is easy and the taste is shared. The risk is that two cardinal air signs can also weigh every decision until the moment passes.

What do the six sub-scores mean?

The six columns are mutual affection, lasting bond, friendship, love, marriage, and family. They break the overall score into the parts of a real relationship — not just the chemistry, but also the day-to-day, the in-laws, and the long run.

Do elements matter for Libra male compatibility?

Yes. Air signs (Gemini, Aquarius) average 84% with Libra. Fire signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) average 78%. Water signs and earth signs both land at 57%. The pattern is clear: same wind, easiest match.
