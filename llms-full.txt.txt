# Chinese Zodiac Couples - Full Context Summary

## 1. Website Overview
**Chinese Zodiac Couples** is a specialized guide that explores love compatibility through the lens of the Chinese zodiac. It provides a free, interactive tool for couples to check their compatibility based on their Chinese zodiac signs, supplemented by in-depth articles on the underlying philosophy, detailed sign profiles, and a comprehensive FAQ. The website serves as a practical resource for understanding relationship dynamics, strengths, and potential friction points from a traditional Chinese zodiac perspective.

## 2. Core Content & Features
- **Compatibility Checker:** An interactive tool that generates a compatibility score and detailed dimension analysis (Emotional, Communication, Values, Intimacy) when users input their birth dates or manually select two zodiac signs.
- **Sign Profiles:** Detailed pages for each of the 12 zodiac animals (Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig), covering personality traits, best matches, and challenging matches.
- **Guides & Philosophy:** Explanatory content covering the core principles of the system, including Yin and Yang pairing, Animal Sign Chemistry, the Five Elements, the Three Harmony Triads, and the Six Harmony Pairs.
- **FAQ Section:** Answers to common questions about the system, its accuracy, and how to interpret compatibility results in real-life relationships.

## 3. Main Compatibility Framework
The website bases its compatibility readings on a combination of:
1.  **Yin and Yang Pairing:** Smooth pairings often balance receptive (Yin) and active (Yang) energy.
2.  **Animal Sign Chemistry:** Each sign has natural affinities (e.g., **Three Harmony Triads**: Rat-Dragon-Monkey, Ox-Snake-Rooster, Tiger-Horse-Dog, Rabbit-Goat-Pig) and natural friction points.
3.  **Five Elements Layer:** Each year's element (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) modifies the core animal sign, leading to different temperaments (e.g., a Wood Rat vs. a Fire Rat).
4.  **Six Harmony Pairs:** A set of particularly compatible pairs: Rat-Ox, Tiger-Pig, Rabbit-Dog, Dragon-Rooster, Snake-Monkey, and Horse-Goat.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
- **Does the Chinese zodiac couples chart really predict relationship success?**
    No. It is a personality-and-rhythm reference to start conversations, not a forecast or verdict.
- **Are some Chinese zodiac couples considered incompatible?**
    Yes, some pairings are traditionally marked as harder, but these require more explicit expectations and communication, not an inevitable outcome.
- **How accurate is the compatibility score?**
    The score blends animal pairing, triads, harmony pairs, and elements. It is useful for ranking but not for fine differences.
- **Should couples ignore the year they were born in?**
    No, the year and its element are important modifiers that add nuance to the animal sign.
- **Can Chinese zodiac couples work if their signs are 'challenging'?**
    Yes. The 'challenging' label flags friction points to manage, not dealbreakers.
- **What is the best way to use this reading?**
    Use it as an orientation and a starting point. Then, refer to the detailed sign profiles and year-by-year pages for a deeper dive.
