1939 Chinese Zodiac FAQ

What chinese zodiac animal is 1939?
1939 is the Rabbit year, paired with the Earth element. The chinese zodiac cycle repeats every 12 years, so the next Rabbit year after 1939 is 1951, and the full chinese zodiac rotation covers Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, and Pig in that order.
What does the Earth element mean for the Rabbit?
Earth on a Rabbit year is the most grounded of the Rabbit cycles. The Rabbit's natural caution is still there, but it sits on a more patient, more practical base. The result is a Rabbit who is easier to lean on and harder to rush.
Who is the 1939 chinese zodiac most compatible with?
The Earth Rabbit of 1939 pairs most smoothly with the Goat, Dog, and Pig. Steady matches include the Tiger, Horse, and Monkey, and the Rat, Rooster, and Dragon are the more demanding pairings that need extra communication and patience. The best matches share the Rabbit's basic tempo and approach to daily life.
What are the lucky numbers for the 1939 chinese zodiac?
The numbers traditionally linked to the 1939 chinese zodiac are 3, 4, 9. They sit alongside the lucky colors, flowers, directions, and stone listed in the lucky elements section above. These symbols are a cultural reference point for the 1939 Rabbit, not a substitute for practical planning.
When does the 1939 chinese zodiac year start?
The Earth Rabbit year 1939 starts on Chinese New Year in 1939, which fell on February 19 on the Gregorian calendar. People born between January 1 and February 18, 1939 are part of the previous chinese zodiac year (the Tiger). The 1939 chinese zodiac year runs from that date until the day before Chinese New Year in 1940. People born in the first few weeks of January should double-check which chinese zodiac year they fall into.
