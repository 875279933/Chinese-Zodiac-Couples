1937 Chinese Zodiac FAQ

What chinese zodiac animal is 1937?
1937 is the Ox year, paired with the Fire element. The chinese zodiac cycle repeats every 12 years, so the next Ox year after 1937 is 1949, and the full chinese zodiac rotation covers Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, and Pig in that order.
What does the Fire element mean for the Ox?
Fire on an Ox year shows up as steady effort with more visible warmth on top. The Ox is still the Ox, but the Fire element gives them more social reach and a slightly more open style than the more reserved Ox years.
Who is the 1937 chinese zodiac most compatible with?
The Fire Ox of 1937 pairs most smoothly with the Rat, Snake, and Rooster. The best matches share the Ox's basic tempo and approach to daily life.
What are the lucky numbers for the 1937 chinese zodiac?
The numbers traditionally linked to the 1937 chinese zodiac are 1, 4, 9. They sit alongside the lucky colors, flowers, directions, and stone listed in the lucky elements section above. These symbols are a cultural reference point for the 1937 Ox, not a substitute for practical planning.
When does the 1937 chinese zodiac year start?
The Fire Ox year 1937 starts on Chinese New Year in 1937, which fell on February 11 on the Gregorian calendar. People born between January 1 and February 10, 1937 are part of the previous chinese zodiac year (the Rat). The 1937 chinese zodiac year runs from that date until the day before Chinese New Year in 1938. People born in the first few weeks of January should double-check which chinese zodiac year they fall into.
