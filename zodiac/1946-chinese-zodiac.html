When does the 1946 chinese zodiac year start?
The Fire Dog year 1946 starts on Chinese New Year in 1946, which fell on February 2 on the Gregorian calendar. People born between January 1 and February 1, 1946 are part of the previous chinese zodiac year (the Rooster). People born in the first few weeks of January should double-check which chinese zodiac year they fall into.
