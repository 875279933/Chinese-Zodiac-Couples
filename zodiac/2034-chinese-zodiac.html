Filed under Cohort · Reading time: 3 minutes

The 2034 year pairs the Tiger with Wood, and people born in 2034 often carry a longer attention span and a growth-oriented pace. They are usually the colleague who picks the project and tends it for a year. They make the first move and follow through on it without making a show of it.

The Tiger side of the year brings the bold move and the willingness to act first. The Wood side adds patience and a longer view on the work. They build a reputation through quiet execution, through colleagues who learned to read their drafts first.
