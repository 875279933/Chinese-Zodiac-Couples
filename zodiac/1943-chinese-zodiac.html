Water on a Goat year produces the most perceptive version of the Goat cycle. The Goat's natural warmth is still there, but it is paired with a stronger sense of what other people are actually feeling and a more reflective sense of when to act on it.

When does the 1943 chinese zodiac year start?
The Water Goat year 1943 starts on Chinese New Year in 1943, which fell on February 5 on the Gregorian calendar. People born between January 1 and February 4, 1943 are part of the previous chinese zodiac year (the Horse). The 1943 chinese zodiac year runs from that date until the day before Chinese New Year in 1944. People born in the first few weeks of January should double-check which chinese zodiac year they fall into.
